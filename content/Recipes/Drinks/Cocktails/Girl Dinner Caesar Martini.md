# Girl Dinner Caesar Martini

*A caesar salad in a glass. A dirty martini stacked with umami: lemon-parmesan vermouth, anchovy-garlic tincture, and olive brine.*

**Serves:** 1
**Total Time:** 2 to 3 days for components plus 5 minutes per cocktail

---

## Components

- Lemon-Parmesan Vermouth
- Caesar Tincture
- Cocktail Build

---

## Lemon-Parmesan Vermouth

### Ingredients
- 1 bottle (375 ml) dry vermouth
- 2 to 3 parmesan rinds, shredded or cubed for surface area
- 3 strips lemon peel
- Optional: a few shavings of fresh parmesan

### Instructions
1. Add the parmesan rind and lemon peel to the vermouth. Shredding or cubing the rind speeds extraction significantly.
2. Refrigerate for 2 to 3 days, tasting daily. The vermouth will turn cloudy and take on salt and umami.
3. If the flavor is mellow, add a few shavings of fresh parmesan. Fresh cheese has more surface area and more active flavor compounds than an old rind.
4. Strain through a coffee filter when the flavor is where you want it. Refrigerate.

---

## Caesar Tincture

### Ingredients
- 2 to 3 anchovy fillets
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- Cracked black pepper
- Squeeze of fresh lemon juice
- 2 oz high-proof vodka

### Instructions
1. Mash the anchovies with the garlic, black pepper, and lemon juice into a rough paste.
2. Add the vodka. Stir, cover, and let sit overnight at minimum. An extra day deepens it further; there is no need to strain early.
3. Strain through a coffee filter into a small dropper bottle or jar. Refrigerate.

---

## Cocktail Build

### Ingredients
- 2 oz vodka
- ¾ oz lemon-parmesan vermouth
- 1 to 2 barspoons caesar tincture
- ½ oz olive brine
- Olives, for garnish

### Instructions
1. Combine vodka, parmesan vermouth, caesar tincture, and olive brine in a mixing glass. Add ice and stir for 30 seconds.
2. Strain into a chilled coupe.
3. Garnish with olives on a pick.

---

## Notes
- The tincture is potent. Start at 1 barspoon and adjust upward. It should read as savory depth, not fish.
- Shortcut comparison: good caesar dressing strained through a coffee filter yields a cloudy, intense liquid that can approximate both the vermouth and tincture in one product. It lacks the vermouth's dryness and alcohol but is worth testing side by side to decide whether building the components is worth the lead time.
- Old parmesan rinds extract mellow. Supplement with fresh shavings rather than extending the infusion time.
- Both components keep refrigerated for several weeks.
- Inspired by the Girl Dinner cocktail: vodka, lemon parmesan vermouth, caesar salad tincture, olive brine, olives.
