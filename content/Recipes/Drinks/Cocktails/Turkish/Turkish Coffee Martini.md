## Ingredients

### The Base
* 4 oz sugar-free Turkish coffee
* 1.5 oz (1 shot) vodka
* 1.5 oz (1 shot) coffee liqueur (e.g., Vita Vera)

### Aromatics & Garnish
* A pinch of ground cardamom
* 3 coffee beans (for garnish/good luck)
* Ice

## Instructions

### Prepare the Coffee
1. Brew 4 oz of sugar-free Turkish coffee on the stove.
2. Set aside to cool for 10 minutes.

### Mix the Drink
1. Fill a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Add the vodka, coffee liqueur, and ground cardamom to the shaker.
3. Strain the cooled Turkish coffee into the shaker using a cheesecloth to remove grounds.

### Shake and Serve
1. Shake vigorously; this is essential to create the signature froth.
2. Use the shaker strainer lid to pour the martini into a glass.
3. Garnish with 3 coffee beans.
