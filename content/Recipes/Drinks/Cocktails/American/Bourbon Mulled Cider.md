## Ingredients

- 16 fluid ounces unfiltered apple cider
- 4 fluid ounces bourbon
- 2 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 1/2 navel orange
- 1/2 lemon
- 10 allspice berries
- 1 star anise
- 4 cardamom pods
- 1 bay leaf

## Instructions

1. Put everything in a medium saucepan, stir, and bring to a gentle boil over medium-high heat.
2. Lower the heat and cook until fragrant, about 5 minutes.
3. Turn off the heat and cover. Rewarm occasionally if holding for guests.
