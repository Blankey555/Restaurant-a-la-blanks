## Ingredients

- 3/4 ounce bourbon
- 3/4 ounce Aperol
- 3/4 ounce Amaro Nonino Quintessentia
- 3/4 ounce lemon juice, freshly squeezed

## Instructions

1. Add the bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino and lemon juice into a shaker with ice and shake until well-chilled.
2. Strain into a coupe glass.

## Notes

- The Paper Plane is a simple variation on the classic Last Word.
- It may be the best bourbon drink you may not have tried yet.
