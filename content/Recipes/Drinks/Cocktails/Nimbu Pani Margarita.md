*A spiced Indian limeade margarita with black salt, chaat masala, and triple sec. Served on the rocks with a black salt rim.*

**Serves:** 1
**Time:** 5 minutes

---

## Ingredients

### Nimbu Pani Base
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice (approximately 1 to 2 limes)
- 1 tsp sugar, or to taste
- ¼ tsp black salt (kala namak)
- ¼ tsp chaat masala
- Pinch of black pepper powder

### Cocktail
- 2 oz blanco tequila
- ½ oz triple sec
- Ice

### Garnish
- Black salt and chaat masala mixed, for rim
- Lime wheel or wedge
- Fresh mint sprig (optional)

---

## Instructions

1. Prepare the rim. Mix black salt and a pinch of chaat masala on a small plate. Run a lime wedge around the rim of a rocks glass and dip into the salt mixture. Fill the glass with ice and set aside.
2. Combine lime juice and sugar in a shaker. Stir until the sugar dissolves.
3. Add black salt, chaat masala, and black pepper. Stir to combine.
4. Add tequila, triple sec, and a generous handful of ice. Shake hard for 15 seconds.
5. Strain over fresh ice into the prepared glass.
6. Garnish with a lime wheel and mint sprig if using.

---

## Notes
- Blanco tequila keeps the drink bright and lets the spices come forward. Reposado works for a rounder, slightly richer result.
- The chaat masala rim is the defining garnish here. Do not skip it.
- Mint is subtle but adds a pleasant freshness on the nose. Add it or leave it based on preference.
- Black salt already appears in chaat masala, so taste before adding more to the base.
- For a large batch, scale the nimbu pani base as a concentrate and shake individual portions to order.
