## Ingredients

### Base
- 3 oz fresh watermelon juice, strained
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice
- ¼ tsp black salt (kala namak), or to taste
- ½ tsp sugar or simple syrup, or to taste
- Ice

### Optional Spices
- Pinch of chaat masala
- Pinch of black pepper powder
- Pinch of Tajín or chili powder for heat
- Small pinch of cumin (use sparingly)

### Garnish
- Black salt and chaat masala mixed, for rim
- Watermelon wedge or lime wheel
- Fresh mint sprig

---

## Variants

### Vodka (Shaken)
- 1½ oz clean neutral vodka
- Add to base, shake hard over ice, strain into a black salt-rimmed rocks glass over fresh ice.

### Tequila (Shaken)
- 1½ oz blanco tequila
- Add an extra squeeze of lime to complement the agave.
- Shake hard over ice, strain into a black salt-rimmed rocks glass over fresh ice.

### Prosecco Spritz (Built)
- Omit spirit.
- Build base in a wine glass over ice.
- Top with 2 oz dry prosecco. Stir once gently.
- Increase sugar slightly to balance the acidity of the prosecco.

### Rosé Spritz (Built)
- Omit spirit.
- Build base in a wine glass over ice.
- Top with 2 oz dry sparkling rosé. Stir once gently.
- The rosé adds red fruit complexity that pairs particularly well with the chaat masala variant.

---

## Instructions

1. Prepare the rim. Mix black salt and a pinch of chaat masala on a small plate. Run a lime wedge around the rim of the glass and dip into the mixture.
2. Combine watermelon juice, lime juice, black salt, and sugar in a shaker or glass. Stir until sugar dissolves. Add any optional spices at this stage and taste as you go.
3. For shaken variants: add spirit and ice, shake hard for 15 seconds, and strain over fresh ice into the prepared glass.
4. For spritz variants: build the base over ice in a wine glass and top with prosecco or sparkling rosé. Stir once gently to combine.
5. Garnish with a watermelon wedge or lime wheel and mint sprig.

---

## Notes
- Strain the watermelon juice through a fine mesh sieve. Seeds and pulp will clog a shaker and cloud the drink unnecessarily.
- Black salt is the defining ingredient. Its sulfurous, savory depth transforms the watermelon from sweet to complex. Do not substitute regular salt.
- Chaat masala already contains black salt. Add it gradually and taste before adjusting the black salt level.
- The spritz variants benefit from a touch more sugar since they lack the alcohol to balance sweetness.
- Blanco tequila keeps the drink bright. Reposado adds a rounder, oaked quality that works well with the chaat masala variant.
- Mint is optional but adds a fresh herbal note on the nose that works especially well in the spritz versions.
- For a large batch, scale the base as a concentrate and build or shake individual portions to order.
