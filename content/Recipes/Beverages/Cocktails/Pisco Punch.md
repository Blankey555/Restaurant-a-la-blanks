*A bright, fruit-forward punch built around pisco. Light enough to drink freely, complex enough to be interesting. Still and sparkling variants.*

**Serves:** 8 to 10
**Time:** 15 minutes plus 1 hour chilling

---

## Ingredients

### Punch Base
- 12 oz pisco (use a good acholado or quebranta for complexity)
- 8 oz fresh pineapple juice, strained
- 3 oz fresh lemon juice
- 2 oz simple syrup, or to taste
- Lemon and lime wheels, for garnish and steeping

---

## Instructions

1. Combine pisco, pineapple juice, lemon juice, and simple syrup in a large pitcher or punch bowl. Stir well.
2. Add several lemon and lime wheels directly to the punch. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour to allow the citrus oils to infuse.
3. Taste and adjust before serving — more simple syrup for sweetness, more lemon for brightness.

### Still Variant
4. Pour over a large block of ice in a punch bowl or over ice in individual rocks glasses.
5. Garnish each serving with a fresh lemon or lime wheel.

### Sparkling Variant
4. Just before serving, add 12 oz chilled dry sparkling water or club soda. Stir once gently to preserve the carbonation.
5. Pour over ice and garnish with lemon and lime wheels.

---

## Scaling for One Serving

- 1½ oz pisco
- 1 oz fresh pineapple juice
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¼ oz simple syrup
- Lemon or lime wheel, for garnish

Shake with ice and strain into a rocks glass over ice for the still version, or strain and top with a splash of sparkling water for the sparkling version.

---

## Notes
- Pisco should lead but not overwhelm. The 12 oz to 8 oz ratio of pisco to pineapple keeps the spirit forward without being hot.
- Acholado pisco, made from a blend of grape varieties, adds more complexity than a single varietal. Quebranta is rounder and softer. Either works well here.
- Fresh pineapple juice is strongly preferred over canned. Strain well to remove pulp.
- Steeping the citrus wheels in the punch for an hour before serving draws out the oils from the peel and adds depth beyond what the juice alone provides.
- A large block of ice melts slower than cubes and keeps the punch cold without diluting it quickly. Freeze water in a loaf pan the night before.
- For the sparkling variant, add the sparkling water at the very last moment and do not stir aggressively.
