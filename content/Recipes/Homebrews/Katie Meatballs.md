*A pantry-driven meatball with Mediterranean character. Quantities are approximate — adjust to taste.*

---

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground pork
- 1 tbsp capers
- 3 to 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- ½ cup Kalamata or green olives, chopped (or more to taste)
- ½ cup marinated artichoke hearts, chopped
- Aleppo pepper, to taste
- Fenugreek, to taste
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 raw egg

---

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and mix until evenly incorporated.
2. Form into balls.
3. Pan fry over medium to medium-high heat, turning to brown on all sides, until cooked through.

---

## Notes
- The olive and artichoke ratio can be adjusted freely depending on what's on hand.
- Aleppo pepper brings mild, fruity heat. Fenugreek adds a subtle bitterness and depth. Both are flexible.
- These work well as an appetizer, over pasta, or alongside a simple green salad.
