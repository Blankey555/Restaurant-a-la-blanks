## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons (30 grams) soft butter, plus 1/2 pound (226 grams) soft butter for greasing the pan and shaping the buns
- 1 cup (225 grams) water, room temperature
- 3 tablespoons (45 grams) fresh yeast, or 1 tablespoon (15 grams) instant yeast
- 3 1/3 cups (450 grams) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup (90 grams) sugar
- 1 tablespoon (15 grams) kosher salt
- 2 eggs
- 2 tablespoons nigella seeds (optional)
- 1 ripe tomato, coarsely grated
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Butter a 9-inch springform pan and set it on a sheet tray. In a stand mixer with the dough hook, mix the water, yeast, flour, sugar, salt and 1 egg on low (save the other egg). Raise to medium-high and knead 10 minutes.
2. With the mixer running, add the 2 tablespoons butter bit by bit, letting each addition absorb. Knead about 5 minutes more, until very elastic. Cover loosely and rest 20 minutes.
3. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured board and cut into 16 equal pieces; keep covered.
4. Spread 2 tablespoons soft butter on the counter, set a piece of dough on it, and butter your palms with another tablespoon. Without lifting it, press and smear the dough out until thin and sheer in spots, about 12 inches across; small tears are fine. Sprinkle with nigella seeds, roll from the far edge toward you into a long thin log, coil it into a snail and set it in the center of the pan.
5. Repeat with the rest, re-buttering the counter and hands as needed, arranging the buns loosely around the first. Cover and let rise in a warm spot 1 hour, until almost doubled.
6. Heat the oven to 350°F. Whisk the remaining egg with 1 tablespoon water and brush over the buns. Bake 30 minutes, until golden and the center buns are as puffed as the outer ones.
7. Meanwhile, season the grated tomato with olive oil and salt. Cool the kubaneh a few minutes and serve with the tomato alongside.
