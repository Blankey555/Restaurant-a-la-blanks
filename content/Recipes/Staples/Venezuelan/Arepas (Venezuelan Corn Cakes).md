## Ingredients

- 2 cups arepa flour (precooked cornmeal)
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 2 1/2 cups warm water
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- Desired fillings (such as shredded cooked chicken or pork, stewed black beans with cheese and lime, corn salad with onion and fresh herbs; for serving)
- Lime wedges (for serving)

## Instructions

1. Combine the arepa flour and salt in a medium bowl. Make a well, pour in 2 1/2 cups warm water, and gradually stir in the flour with a wooden spoon until no dry lumps remain. Rest 5 minutes to hydrate.
2. Knead the dough a few times in the bowl, divide into 8 pieces, roll each into a ball, and gently flatten to about 1/2 inch thick.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium. Add 4 arepas, cover, and cook 6 to 8 minutes, until golden. Uncover, flip, and cook uncovered 6 to 8 minutes more, until the other side is golden. Move to a wire rack. Repeat with the remaining oil and dough.
4. Split the arepas, stuff with fillings, and serve with lime wedges.

## Notes

- Substitute recipe: the saved NPR essay about arepas had no recipe and the link is dead, so this is Bon Appétit's basic arepas.
- Arepa flour is precooked corn flour (sold as masarepa or harina precocida, e.g. Harina P.A.N.), not masa harina.
- Dietary tags apply to the arepas themselves; fillings vary.
