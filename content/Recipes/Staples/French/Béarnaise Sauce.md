## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup white-wine vinegar
- 1 small shallot, peeled and minced
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
- 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon chopped tarragon leaves
- 2 egg yolks
- 12 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Splash of lemon juice, optional

## Instructions

1. Combine the vinegar, shallot, pepper, and 1 tablespoon tarragon in a small saucepan over medium heat. Bring just to a boil, then simmer about 5 minutes until only a few tablespoons of liquid remain. Let cool.
2. Bring an inch or two of water to a boil in a small saucepan.
3. In a metal bowl, whisk the cooled reduction with 1 tablespoon water and the yolks.
4. Lower the heat under the water to its minimum and set the bowl on top without touching the water. Whisk 5 to 7 minutes, until the yolks thicken and roughly double in volume.
5. Slowly whisk in the butter a tablespoon or two at a time to emulsify, lifting the bowl off the heat now and then so the eggs do not overcook.
6. Season with salt. Add lemon juice if it needs more sharpness, or a splash of hot water if too thick. Stir in the remaining teaspoon of tarragon and serve.
