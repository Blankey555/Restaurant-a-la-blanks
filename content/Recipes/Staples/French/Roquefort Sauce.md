## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup minced shallot
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 cup dry white wine
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup milk
- 1 cup crumbled Roquefort (about 1/4 pound)
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh parsley leaves

## Instructions

1. In a small heavy saucepan, cook the shallot in the butter over moderately low heat, stirring, until soft. Add the wine and boil until reduced to about 1 tablespoon.
2. Whisk in the flour and cook, whisking, 3 minutes.
3. Pour in the milk in a stream, whisking, and boil 2 minutes, still whisking.
4. Lower the heat and whisk in the Roquefort a little at a time until melted; do not let it boil. Strain through a fine sieve into a bowl.
5. Stir in the parsley and season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- The source suggests serving it with roasted or grilled beef, veal or pork.
