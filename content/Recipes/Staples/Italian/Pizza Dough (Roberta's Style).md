## Ingredients

- 153 grams 00 flour (1 cup plus 1 tablespoon)
- 153 grams all-purpose flour (1 cup plus 1 tablespoon and 2 teaspoons)
- 8 grams fine sea salt (1 teaspoon)
- 2 grams active dry yeast (3/4 teaspoon)
- 4 grams extra-virgin olive oil (1 teaspoon)
- 200 grams lukewarm tap water (a little less than 1 cup)

## Instructions

1. Combine both flours and the salt in a large bowl.
2. Stir the lukewarm water, yeast and oil together in a small bowl, pour into the flour, and knead by hand about 3 minutes until combined. Rest 15 minutes.
3. Knead 3 minutes more, divide in two, and shape into balls. Set on a heavily floured surface under a damp cloth and let rise 3 to 4 hours at room temperature or 8 to 24 hours in the fridge.
4. To make pizza, stretch each ball on a heavily floured surface with your fingers, then shape into rounds or squares with your hands. Top and bake.

## Notes

- If refrigerated, take the dough out 30 to 45 minutes before shaping (per the source).
- The water is listed in the source's method rather than its ingredient list.
- Used for [[White Pizza with Mozzarella, Taleggio and Parmesan]].
