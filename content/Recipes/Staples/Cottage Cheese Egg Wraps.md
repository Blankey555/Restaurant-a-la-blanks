*A high-protein, low-carb wrap made from blended eggs and cottage cheese. Use in place of a tortilla or flatbread.*

**Makes:** 2 wraps
**Time:** 15 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp dried oregano

---

## Instructions

1. Combine eggs, cottage cheese, salt, and oregano in a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and lightly grease with oil or cooking spray.
3. Pour half the batter into the pan and swirl to spread into a thin, even circle.
4. Cook undisturbed for 2 to 3 minutes until the edges are set and the surface is no longer wet.
5. Carefully flip and cook for another 1 minute.
6. Remove and repeat with remaining batter.
7. Fill and roll as desired.

---

## Notes
- Do not rush the flip. The wrap tears easily if moved before fully set on the first side.
- A lid on the pan for the first 2 minutes helps the surface set without flipping too early.
- Works well filled with scrambled eggs, roasted vegetables, smoked salmon, or any sandwich filling.
- Store cooked wraps stacked between parchment paper in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat briefly in a dry pan.
