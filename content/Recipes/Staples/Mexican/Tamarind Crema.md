## Ingredients

- 1 cup Mexican crema (Crema Salvadoreña works; slightly thicker and tangier)
- 2 tbsp tamarind paste or concentrate
- 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1 tsp brown sugar, or to taste
- Pinch of salt

### Optional Heat
- Pinch of chipotle powder or a few drops of hot sauce

---

## Instructions

1. Whisk all ingredients together in a bowl until smooth.
2. Taste and adjust the sweet-sour balance. More tamarind for sourness, more brown sugar to round it out.
3. Refrigerate overnight if possible. The flavors meld and the tamarind integrates fully into the crema.
4. Stir before serving, as it may separate slightly.

---

## Worcestershire Variant

When tamarind is unavailable, Worcestershire sauce is the closest substitute since it contains tamarind and shares the sweet-sour-savory profile.

- 1 cup Mexican crema
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce, adding more to taste
- 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- Pinch of salt
- Optional: pinch of chipotle powder or a few drops of hot sauce

Whisk together and rest overnight as above. Start conservatively with the Worcestershire and build up.

---

## Notes
- Making it a day ahead is strongly recommended. The rest mellows the tamarind or Worcestershire into the crema rather than tasting like a stirred-in condiment.
- Keeps sealed in the fridge for about a week.
- Cultured crema (Salvadoreña style) adds a tang that complements tamarind rather than fighting it.
- Other tamarind substitutes in order of preference: pomegranate molasses, lime juice with brown sugar, amchur (dried mango powder).
- The crema base can be homemade: [[Crema Mexicana (Mexican Table Cream)]].
