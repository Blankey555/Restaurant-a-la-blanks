## Ingredients

- 450 g tomatillos, husked and rinsed
- 4 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 1 jalapeño or serrano pepper
- ½ white onion, quartered
- ½ cup fresh cilantro
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp cumin

---

## Instructions

1. Place tomatillos, garlic, pepper, and onion directly under a broiler or on a dry cast iron skillet over high heat. Char until blackened in spots, turning occasionally, until the tomatillos are soft and collapsing, about 8 to 10 minutes.
2. Peel the garlic once cool enough to handle. Remove the stem from the pepper. Keep the seeds for more heat.
3. Add all charred vegetables to a blender with cilantro, lime juice, salt, and cumin. Pulse to preferred texture, chunky or smooth.
4. Taste and adjust salt, lime, and heat. Refrigerate until serving.

---

## Notes
- The char is where the smoky depth comes from. Do not skip or shorten it.
- For more smoke without a grill, add a pinch of chipotle powder or a few drops of liquid smoke at the blending stage.
- Improves as it sits. Can be made 1 to 2 days ahead.
- Keeps in the fridge for 3 to 4 days.
