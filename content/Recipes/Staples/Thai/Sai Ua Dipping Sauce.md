# Sai Ua Dipping Sauce

*A simplified nam jim built as a reduction of lemongrass, galangal, and makrut lime leaf. Developed to restore aromatics to a batch of sai ua that came up short on them, but works as a general dipping sauce for grilled meats and sausage.*

**Yield:** Approximately ¾ cup
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised and roughly chopped
- 2 inch piece galangal, sliced
- 6 makrut lime leaves, torn
- ¼ cup fish sauce
- ¼ cup fresh lime juice
- 3 tbsp palm sugar
- ½ cup water

### Optional Heat
- 1 to 2 fresh Thai chilies, sliced, or toasted chili flakes to taste

---

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a small saucepan.
2. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, then reduce to medium-low.
3. Simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, until reduced and slightly syrupy, about 15 to 20 minutes. The sauce should coat a spoon lightly.
4. Strain out the solids, pressing to extract fully. Discard the solids.
5. Taste and adjust the balance: more lime for brightness, more palm sugar to round, more fish sauce for depth.
6. Cool and serve at room temperature.

---

## Notes
- This is functionally the traditional accompaniment to sai ua rather than a workaround. Serving it alongside puts the aromatic flavor back where it belongs if the sausage itself is under-seasoned on lemongrass, galangal, or lime leaf.
- The reduction concentrates quickly at the end. Pull it early rather than late since it thickens further as it cools.
- Add chilies during the simmer for integrated heat, or finish with toasted chili flakes for a sharper, separate heat.
- Keeps sealed in the fridge for about 2 weeks.
