## Ingredients

- 1 pot of water
- 2 inches soy milk (unflavored and minimally processed)
- Ladle or spoon for lifting yuba sheets

## Instructions

1. Bring a few inches of water to a simmer in a large saucepot.
2. Fill a second pot with about two inches of soy milk and rest it inside the pot of boiling water.
3. Monitor the yuba with a laser thermometer, noting that the soy milk begins to develop a skin at 165 degrees Fahrenheit.
4. Keep the water at a simmer or just below, as too-high heat will stall the yuba's formation.
5. It takes about 25 minutes for each sheet of yuba to form.
6. Once a full, solid sheet has come into existence, remove it by slicing around the edge of the pot and using your fingers to lift the sheet.

## Notes

- The double-boiler set-up is not necessary, but it makes the process even more hands-off.
- Yuba doesn't have a long shelf life; consume within a few days.
- Fresh sheets made this way are the starting point for [[Buddha's Roast Duck with Yuba]].
