## Ingredients

- 525g plain flour, plus extra for dusting
- 1 1/2 tbsp caster sugar, plus a pinch
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp fast-action dried yeast
- 50ml milk
- 1 tbsp sunflower oil, plus extra for brushing and for the bowl
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 200ml water, plus 1 tbsp warm water
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, caster sugar, and 1/2 tsp salt in a large bowl. Dissolve the yeast and a pinch of sugar in 1 tbsp warm water, then add to the flour with the milk, oil, rice vinegar, and 200ml water. Mix to a dough, adding a little more water if needed.
2. Knead on a lightly floured surface 10 to 15 minutes, until smooth. Place in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let rise 2 hours, or until doubled.
3. Punch the dough down and flatten it. Sprinkle over the baking powder and knead 5 minutes.
4. Roll into a long log about 3cm thick and cut into 3cm pieces (18 total). Roll each into a ball and rest 2 to 3 minutes.
5. Roll each ball into an oval 3 to 4mm thick. Oil the surface, lay an oiled chopstick across the middle, fold the dough over it, then slide the chopstick out.
6. Set each bun on a square of baking parchment on a tray, cover with a tea towel, and prove in a warm place 1 hour 30 minutes, or until doubled.
7. Steam over medium-high heat 8 minutes, until puffed, in batches. Open each bun, fill, and eat warm.
