## Ingredients

- Any cut of beef, as desired
- Salt (for marinating)
- Lime juice
- Olive oil
- Taco seasoning (optional, for vegan or gluten-free adaptations)

## Instructions

1. Place the beef in a zip-top bag and salt it generously.
2. Refrigerate the beef for at least 24 hours to allow the flavors to penetrate.
3. The morning of cooking, remove the beef from the refrigerator and bathe it in lime juice and olive oil. Seal the bag and let sit until ready to cook.
4. For vegan or gluten-free adaptations, mix the beef with taco seasoning before marinating.
5. Grill or pan-fry the meat until cooked through. Slice thinly.

## Notes

- The marination process enhances flavor significantly; ensure it's done 24 hours in advance if possible.
- This recipe can be fully prepared and stored for up to 26 hours, making it ideal for make-ahead meal planning.
