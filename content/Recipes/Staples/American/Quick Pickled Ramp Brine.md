## Ingredients

- 8 ramp bulbs, trimmed and cleaned
- 1 cup water
- ½ cup rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp black salt (kala namak); regular salt works as a substitute
- 1 tsp sugar

---

## Instructions

1. Combine water, rice vinegar, salt, and sugar in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer and stir until the salt and sugar dissolve, about 2 minutes.
2. Pack the ramp bulbs into a clean jar.
3. Pour the hot brine over the bulbs, making sure they are fully submerged. Let cool to room temperature, about 30 minutes.
4. Seal and refrigerate. Usable after a few hours, better after overnight.

---

## Notes
- Kala namak is the preferred salt. Its sulfurous character leans into the allium funk of the ramps and gives the brine a deeper savory quality in a dirty martini.
- For a martini, start with half an ounce of brine per drink and adjust to taste.
- The sugar rounds out the acidity rather than sweetening. It can be omitted for a sharper brine.
- Optional additions: a few black peppercorns or a smashed garlic clove in the jar.
- Keeps in the fridge for weeks.
- Pairs with ramp-infused gin: muddle crushed ramp leaves into gin and infuse a few days for the leaves, up to a week for bulbs. Taste daily and strain when ready.
