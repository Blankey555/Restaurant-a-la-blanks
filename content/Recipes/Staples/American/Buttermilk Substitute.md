## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon white vinegar (or lemon juice)
- 1 cup milk (scant cup)

## Instructions

1. Pour the tablespoon of vinegar into a 1-cup measure.
2. Fill the rest of the measure with milk.
3. Gently stir and let the mixture sit for about 5 minutes. The milk will slightly curdle, giving it a buttermilk-like texture and taste.

## Notes

- A plant-based milk can be substituted for a dairy-free version.
- Feeds directly into [[Crema Mexicana (Mexican Table Cream)]].
