## Ingredients

- Any cut of beef
- Salt
- Lime juice

## Instructions

1. Place the beef in a zip-top bag and salt it generously.
2. Refrigerate for 24 hours.
3. The morning of cooking, bathe the beef in lime juice. Seal the bag and let it sit until ready to cook.
4. Grill.

## Chicken Variant

- Chicken
- Lime juice
- Olive oil
- Taco seasoning (1 bag)

Combine the chicken, lime juice, olive oil, and taco seasoning in a zip-top bag. Marinate overnight, at least. Grill.
