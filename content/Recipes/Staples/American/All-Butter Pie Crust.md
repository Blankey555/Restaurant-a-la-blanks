## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour (150 grams)
- 1/4 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 10 tablespoons unsalted butter, cold and cut into cubes
- 2 to 4 tablespoons ice water, as needed

## Instructions

1. Pulse the flour and salt in a food processor. Add the butter and pulse until it is in lima bean-size pieces. Add ice water 1 tablespoon at a time, pulsing, until the dough just comes together; it should be moist but not wet.
2. Turn out onto a lightly floured surface, gather into a ball, and flatten into a disk. Wrap tightly and refrigerate at least 1 hour and up to 2 days.
3. Roll out on a lightly floured counter, rolling away from you and rotating the dough as you go, to a 12-inch circle. Use as little flour as possible.
4. Roll the dough onto the pin and unroll it into a 9-inch pie pan. Fold over the excess and crimp the edge (unless adding a top crust). Prick all over with a fork and chill 30 minutes. It is now ready to fill or blind bake.
5. To blind bake: line the crust with parchment or foil, fill with pie weights, rice, or dried beans, and bake at 425°F about 15 minutes, until firm but pale. Remove the liner and weights and bake 5 to 7 minutes more, until pale golden. Cool on a rack before filling.

## Notes

- From the NYT Cooking guide "How to Make a Pie Crust"; recovered from a 2017 Internet Archive snapshot.
- Double the recipe for a double-crust pie (lattice, full top, or cutouts) and shape it into two disks before chilling.
- The dough keeps 3 months in the freezer; thaw overnight in the fridge.
- A blind-baked crust can be made up to 24 hours before filling; cover loosely with a towel at room temperature.
- The guide recommends a high-fat European-style butter, and says 3 to 4 tablespoons of the butter can be swapped for shortening, lard, suet, duck fat, or an unsweetened nut butter.
- Cheddar variation from the guide: pulse 1 1/4 cups flour with 3/4 teaspoon salt, add 3/4 cup grated sharp cheddar, then 8 tablespoons cold cubed butter, and proceed as above.
- Bake pies on a rimmed baking sheet. An unbaked fruit pie can be frozen and baked from frozen, adding about 15 minutes.
