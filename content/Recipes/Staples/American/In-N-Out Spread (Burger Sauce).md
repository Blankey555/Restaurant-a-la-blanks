## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 3 tablespoons ketchup
- 2 tablespoons finely minced white onion
- 1 1/2 tablespoons finely minced dill pickle relish
- 3/4 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon white vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon yellow mustard
- 1/4 teaspoon paprika
- 1/8 teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Combine all the ingredients in a small bowl. Set aside until needed.

## Notes

- Use about 1 1/2 tablespoons per burger; the batch dresses about 8.
- The home of this spread is the [[In-N-Out Burger Double-Double Hack]].
