## Ingredients

- 510 grams (about 4 1/4 cups) sprouted whole wheat flour or whole wheat flour
- 1 1/4 teaspoons fine sea salt
- 1 1/4 teaspoons instant yeast
- 1 2/3 cups plus 1 1/2 teaspoons (408 milliliters) lukewarm water
- 1 tablespoon barley malt, agave syrup, or honey
- 2 tablespoons baking soda, malt syrup or honey for boiling water bath
- Cornmeal or semolina flour for baking sheets
- 2 to 4 tablespoons topping of your choice: sesame seeds, poppy seeds, caraway seeds, nigella seeds, onion flakes

## Instructions

1. In a stand mixer with the paddle (or a large bowl), mix flour, salt and yeast about 30 seconds. Separately whisk the water with the barley malt, honey or agave.
2. Add the liquid and mix on low (or stir) 1 minute until shaggy. Remove the paddle and rest the dough uncovered 5 minutes.
3. With the dough hook (or by hand on an oiled surface), knead 2 minutes until smooth and slightly tacky. If very sticky, add a few tablespoons of flour and knead 1 minute more. The dough should be firm, supple and smooth.
4. Form a ball, turn it in an oiled bowl, cover tightly and proof at room temperature 1 1/2 to 2 hours, until about 1 1/2 times its size.
5. Line 2 baking sheets with lightly oiled parchment. Divide the dough into 8 and roll each into a ball on an unfloured surface.
6. Roll each ball into an 8-inch rope tapered at the ends. Moisten the ends, wrap the rope around your hand, overlap the ends about 2 inches and roll on the counter to seal. Even out the ring; the hole should be about 2 inches. Set at least 1 inch apart on the sheets, oil the tops and cover loosely.
7. Proof 30 to 60 minutes until just starting to swell. Heat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the middle.
8. Move the bagels' parchment off the sheets and replace with fresh parchment, lightly oiled and dusted with cornmeal or semolina. Test one bagel in water: it should float within 15 seconds; if not, wait 20 minutes and test again.
9. Bring 4 to 6 inches of water to a boil in a large saucepan, add the baking soda, malt syrup or honey, and keep at a gentle boil. Boil bagels two at a time, 30 seconds per side. Lift onto the prepared sheet rounded side up and add topping immediately.
10. Bake 12 minutes, rotate, and bake 8 to 12 minutes more until golden. If the bottoms brown too fast, slide a second sheet underneath after the first 12 minutes. Cool.

## Notes

- Recipe by Peter Reinhart, published in NYT Cooking.
- Alternative shaping: push your thumbs through the center of each ball and stretch the hole to 2 inches; the source author prefers the rope method because poked holes tend to close up.
