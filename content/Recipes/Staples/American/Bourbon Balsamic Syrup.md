## Ingredients

- 1 cup balsamic vinegar
- 1/2 cup bourbon
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Combine vinegar and bourbon in a small nonreactive pan over medium-high heat. Bring to a boil, then drop the heat to a gentle bubble.
2. Reduce, stirring now and then, to about 1/2 cup, 15 to 20 minutes, until it coats a spoon (it thickens further as it cools). Salt to taste and serve warm or at room temperature.
