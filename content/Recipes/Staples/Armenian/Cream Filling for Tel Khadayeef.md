## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup heavy cream

## Instructions

1. Mix together cornstarch and sugar in a bowl. Stir in one-quarter cup of milk, blending until smooth.
2. In a saucepan bring the cream and remaining milk to a gentle boil. Lower heat and slowly stir in the cornstarch mixture, stirring constantly until thickened. Let simmer gently over low flame for one minute, then shut flame. When filling has cooled, refrigerate.
3. Use this chilled, thickened cream as a filling instead of the walnut filling for [[Tel Khadayeef (Shredded Pastry with Walnut Filling)]].
