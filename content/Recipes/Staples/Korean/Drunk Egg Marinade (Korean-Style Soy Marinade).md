## Ingredients

- 1 cup reduced sodium soy sauce (33% reduced)
- ½ cup water
- 1 heaping tbsp crystalline honey
- 2 tbsp chili crisp
- 2 to 3 tbsp sesame seeds
- Several dashes sesame oil
- 2 heaping tbsp garlic powder

---

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl or jar and whisk until the honey is fully dissolved.
2. Soft boil eggs to your preferred doneness, peel, and submerge fully in the marinade.
3. Refrigerate for a minimum of 4 hours, ideally overnight. The longer they sit, the deeper the flavor penetrates.

---

## Notes
- Crystalline honey dissolves more slowly than liquid. Whisk thoroughly or warm the marinade gently to help it incorporate.
- Chili crisp varies significantly by brand in heat and flavor. Adjust to taste.
- Eggs will continue to darken and absorb flavor the longer they sit. Beyond 24 hours they can become quite salty.
- The marinade can be reused once for a second batch. Strain and refrigerate between uses.
