# Shiso Syrup

A 1:1 simple syrup steeped with dried shiso. Purple shiso produces a deep magenta syrup; green shiso produces a paler, more herbal one. Built for Goblin Town and other cocktails.

**Yield:** Approximately 1.5 cups
**Total Time:** 45 minutes to 1 hour

---

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1 large handful dried shiso (purple/red preferred)

---

## Instructions

1. Combine sugar and water in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer, stirring until the sugar fully dissolves.
2. Remove from heat. Add the dried shiso, cover, and steep as the syrup cools.
3. Taste at 30 minutes. Purple shiso is robust and can steep up to an hour or until fully cool without turning bitter. Green shiso is more delicate; pull it earlier.
4. Strain through a fine mesh sieve, pressing the leaves to extract fully.
5. Bottle and refrigerate.

---

## Notes
- Purple shiso gives both flavor and a deep reddish-purple color that tints cocktails darker.
- Dried shiso extracts well in hot syrup and is a reliable substitute when fresh is out of season. Fresh shiso is still preferred for leaf garnishes.
- Yukari (shiso powder) is not a good substitute for the syrup since most is seasoned with salt and ume, which shifts the flavor profile.
- Make one consistent batch large enough for testing and service rather than mixing batches with slightly different intensities.
- If the syrup comes out too weak, simmer it down to concentrate. If too strong, dilute with additional plain simple syrup.
- Keeps in the fridge for about 2 weeks.
