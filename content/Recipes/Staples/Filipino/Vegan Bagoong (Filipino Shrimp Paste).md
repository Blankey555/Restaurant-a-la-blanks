## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- ⅓ cup red onion (grated)
- 4 teaspoons ginger (minced)
- 2 tablespoons garlic (minced)
- ½ cup oyster mushrooms (very finely chopped)
- 2 tablespoons aonori (or finely ground nori seaweed)
- 2 tablespoons gochugaru
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- ¼ cup shaoxing cooking wine
- ¼ cup white miso paste
- ¼ cup tamari (or other soy sauce of your preference)

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. After 90 seconds, when the oil is hot, add the red onion and cook for 3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
2. Add the ginger and garlic and oyster mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mushrooms release their moisture and begin to brown slightly.
3. Stir in the aonori (or ground nori sea weed), gochugaru, and brown sugar.. Cook for 2 minutes, stirring frequently.
4. Reduce to low heat and stir in the shaoxing wine, miso paste, and tamari. Cook for 3-4 minutes, stirring continuously, until the mixture is thick and dark in color.
5. Remove from heat and let cool to room temperature. Transfer to a sterilized jar and refrigerate until ready to use.

## Notes

- Improves with age. Can be stored in the fridge for up to 6 weeks as long as the jar has been sterilized, and you always take some out using a clean, dry metal spoon.
