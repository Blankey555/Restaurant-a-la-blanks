*A quick, bold dipping sauce and drizzle with sweet, spicy, umami depth. Ready in 5 minutes.*

**Prep:** 5 minutes
**Total:** 5 minutes
**Servings:** 4

---

## Ingredients

- 2 to 3 tbsp gochujang paste (use less for mild, more for spicy)
- 1½ tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp pure maple syrup

---

## Instructions

1. Whisk all ingredients together in a small bowl until smooth.
2. Use immediately or store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week.

---

## Uses
- Bibimbap sauce
- Drizzle over rice or grain bowls
- Dipping sauce for sweet potato fries or grilled vegetables
- Stir fry sauce
- Spread on veggie burgers or sandwiches
- Drizzle over roasted vegetables

---

## Notes
- Gochujang brands vary significantly in heat and flavor. Start with 2 tbsp and adjust up.
- Maple syrup can be substituted with honey or brown sugar.
- Rice vinegar can be substituted with apple cider vinegar in a pinch.
