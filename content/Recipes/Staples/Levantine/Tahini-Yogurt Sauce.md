## Ingredients

- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tablespoons tahini
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1/2 garlic clove, finely grated or minced
- Salt

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, combine the yogurt, tahini, lemon juice, and garlic; season to taste with salt.
2. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, or up to 3 days.

## Notes

- Made for [[Shawarma-Spiced Grilled Chicken]], and good on nearly anything grilled or in a wrap.
