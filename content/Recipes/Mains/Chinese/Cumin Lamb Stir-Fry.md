## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon whole cumin seeds
- 2 teaspoons Sichuan or regular peppercorns
- 1 pound boneless lamb
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 4 to 8 dried red chiles (or substitute 1/2 teaspoon or more crushed red pepper)
- 1 large white onion
- 1 bunch (about 8) scallions, trimmed
- 2 tablespoons peanut oil
- 3 large garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons Chinese cooking sherry (Shaoxing rice wine) or dry sherry
- 2 cups fresh cilantro, leaves and stems
- Rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Toast the cumin seeds and peppercorns in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes, until fragrant. Crush lightly in a mortar and pestle.
2. Slice the lamb across the grain into 1/2-inch-thick strips. Toss with the crushed spices, ground cumin, salt, and chiles.
3. Halve the onion through the root and cut each half lengthwise into 1/2-inch slices. Cut the white and light green scallion parts into 2-inch pieces; thinly slice the greens and keep them separate.
4. Heat a large skillet or wok over high heat for about 5 minutes, until very hot. Add the oil, then the onion and scallion bottoms. Cook, tossing now and then, about 2 minutes, until lightly charred but still crisp. Move to a bowl.
5. Add the lamb and chiles and stir-fry until the meat starts to brown. Add the garlic, soy sauce, and wine; cook about 2 minutes, until most of the liquid is gone and the lamb is cooked through. Return the onions and scallions.
6. Take off the heat, mix in the cilantro and scallion greens, and serve hot over rice.

## Notes

- The source recommends Sichuan peppercorns for the most authentic flavor; regular peppercorns work as a substitute.
