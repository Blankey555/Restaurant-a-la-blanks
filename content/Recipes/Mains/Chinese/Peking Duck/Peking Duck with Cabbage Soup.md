## Ingredients

* 1 roast Peking duck carcass, meat removed and reserved for other uses
* 1/2 pound napa cabbage, cut into 2-inch chunks
* 10 cups water
* 1-inch piece fresh ginger, smashed
* 2 green onions, halved
* 1 teaspoon salt, or to taste
* 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
* 1/2 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
* Fresh cilantro for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Simmer the Base:** Place the duck carcass, water, smashed ginger, and green onions in a large stockpot. Bring to a boil over high heat, then immediately reduce to low. 
2. **Extract Flavor:** Simmer uncovered for 1 hour. Occasionally skim any foam or excess fat that rises to the surface.
3. **Strain:** Carefully remove the carcass and aromatics from the pot. Strain the broth through a fine-mesh sieve into a clean pot to ensure a clear liquid.
4. **Cook Cabbage:** Bring the strained broth back to a simmer. Add the napa cabbage and cook until the ribs are tender (about 8 to 10 minutes).
5. **Season:** Stir in the salt, white pepper, and sesame oil. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed.
6. **Serve:** Ladle into warm bowls and garnish with cilantro if desired.
