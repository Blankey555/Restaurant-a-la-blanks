## Ingredients

* 1 1/2 cups (approx. 1/2 lb) roast Peking duck meat, cut into bite-size pieces
* 2 large green onions, cut on a steep bias into 2-inch pieces (separate white and green parts)
* 3/4-inch section fresh ginger, peeled and julienned
* 1 rib celery, cut on a bias to match onions
* 1/2 carrot, halved lengthwise and thinly sliced to match celery
* 1 heaping cup bean sprouts
* 2 tablespoons canola oil
* Salt to taste

### Seasoning Sauce
* 2 tablespoons plum sauce
* 1 tablespoon Shaoxing rice wine or dry sherry
* 1 tablespoon light soy sauce

## Instructions

1. **Prep:** Stir together the plum sauce, rice wine, and soy sauce in a small bowl. Keep the white and green sections of the onions separate.
2. **Aromatics:** Heat the oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat. Add the ginger and stir-fry until aromatic (about 20 seconds). Add the white parts of the onions and cook until the ginger begins to color (about 15 seconds).
3. **Vegetables:** Add the celery, carrot, and bean sprouts with a generous pinch of salt. Stir-fry until the vegetables soften slightly (about 2 minutes).
4. **Meat & Sauce:** Add the duck meat and stir for 1 minute to heat through. Pour in the seasoning sauce and cook for another minute to allow the duck to absorb the flavors.
5. **Finish:** Add the green sections of the onion and stir for 15 seconds until wilted. Serve immediately with rice or extra mandarin pancakes.
