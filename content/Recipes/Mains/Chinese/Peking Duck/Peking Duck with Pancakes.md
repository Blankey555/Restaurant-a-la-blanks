# Peking Duck with Handmade Pancakes (Custom Method)

A refined version of the classic roast, utilizing a beer can spit for even airflow and a double-scald technique for maximum skin crispness.

## Ingredients

### The Duck & Glaze
* 1 whole duck (approx. 2kg)
* 5 tbsp honey or maltose
* 1 tbsp red vinegar
* 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
* 2 tsp five spice powder (divided)
* 2 tsp salt (divided)
* 1 half-full can of beer (or water) in a stainless steel vertical spit

### The Pancakes (Yields 20)
* 2 cups all-purpose flour
* 3/4 cup boiling water
* 1 tsp vegetable oil (plus extra for brushing)
* 1 pinch salt

### For Serving
* Hoisin sauce or sweet bean sauce (Tianmianjiang)
* Cucumber (julienned)
* Scallions (shredded)

## Instructions

### Prepare the Duck (Day 1)
1. **Pump Inflation:** Slip pump into cavity by neck and inflate like balloon. Continue slipping and inflating until skin is loose all around. 
2. **Initial Scald:** Remove excess fat from the cavity. Pour a kettle of boiling water over the entire bird to tighten the skin. Pat completely dry. Let rest mounted or hanging for 30 minutes. 
3. **Glaze Scald:** Combine honey, vinegar, wine, 1 tsp five spice, and 1 tsp salt in a pan. Bring to a boil. Pour this boiling glaze over the duck to scald it a second time.
4. **Set the Spit:** Place 1 tsp five spice and 1 tsp salt directly into the center of the beer can spit. Mount the duck onto the stainless steel spit.
5. **Air Dry:** Place the mounted duck in the refrigerator, uncovered, for at least 24 hours. The vertical position and air circulation are critical for drying the skin. 
6. **Ready:** Skin should be tacky and completely dry to touch.

### Roast the Duck
1. Remove duck from fridge two hours before roasting to come to room temperature. 
2. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C).
3. Place the spit in a roasting pan.
4. Roast for 60 to 70 minutes with door slightly ajar. The vertical position ensures even rendering of fat and consistent browning, the open oven allows steam to escape.
5. Increase temperature to 375-400°F for 15 more minutes to brown skin.
6. Carve and serve immediately to avoid steam softening skin

### Make the Pancakes
1. Mix flour and salt. Add boiling water and oil, stirring until a dough forms.
2. Knead for 5 minutes, then cover and rest for 30 minutes.
3. Roll into a log and cut into 20 pieces. Flatten each into a disc.
4. Brush one disc with oil, stack another on top, and roll into a thin 6-inch circle.
5. Toast in a dry pan over medium heat for 30-45 seconds per side. 
6. Peel the two pancakes apart while warm and keep covered.

### Assembly
1. Carve the duck into thin slices with the crispy skin attached.
2. Serve with pancakes, sauce, cucumber, and scallions.
