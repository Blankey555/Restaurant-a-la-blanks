## Ingredients

- 3 to 4 eggplants
- 400g firm tofu
- 4 tablespoons neutral oil, divided
- 6 dried chillies, chopped
- 1 bird's eye chilli, sliced
- 3cm ginger, minced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons chilli bean sauce (doubanjiang)
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 teaspoons Sichuan peppercorn powder
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- 1 tablespoon dark soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
- Spring onion greens, to finish

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C. Poke each eggplant several times with a fork and roast on a lined tray for about 50 minutes, turning twice. Remove and let cool slightly.
2. Crumble the tofu. Heat 2 tablespoons of the oil in a pan over high heat and fry the tofu until browned and caramelized. Remove and set aside.
3. Add the remaining oil, dried chillies, and fresh chilli to the pan and cook for 3 to 4 minutes. Add the ginger and garlic and fry for 2 minutes, then stir in the chilli bean sauce and cook for another 2 minutes.
4. Add the water and bring to a simmer. Stir the tofu and Sichuan pepper back in, then simmer until most of the liquid has reduced. Remove from heat and stir through the sugar and soy sauces.
5. Slice open the eggplants and gently pry them apart to expose the flesh. Spoon over the spicy tofu filling and finish with spring onion greens.

## Notes

- Vegan as written. A tahini and yogurt drizzle is an optional finishing touch from the original recipe; use a plant-based yogurt to keep it vegan.
