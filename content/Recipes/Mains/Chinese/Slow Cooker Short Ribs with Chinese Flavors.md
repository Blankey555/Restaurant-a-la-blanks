## Ingredients
* 8 short ribs, about 3 pounds
* 1/2 cup soy sauce
* 1/4 cup sugar or honey
* 3 star anise
* 6 scallions, trimmed
* 1 3-inch piece cinnamon
* 5 nickel-size slices of ginger
* 1 teaspoon Sichuan peppercorns
* Salt
* Cooked white rice for serving
* Chopped scallions or fresh cilantro leaves for garnish

## Instructions

### Step 1: Slow Cook
1. Combine all ingredients, except salt, rice and garnish, in slow cooker.
2. Cover and cook until meat is very tender and falling from bone, 5 hours or more on high, 7 hours or more on low. 
3. Taste and add salt if necessary.

### Step 2: Finish and Serve
1. If you like, remove meat, strain liquid and refrigerate meat and liquid separately. 
2. Skim fat from liquid, and reheat with meat. 
3. Serve hot over white rice garnished with scallions or cilantro.
