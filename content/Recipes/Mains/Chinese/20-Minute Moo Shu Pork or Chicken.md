## Ingredients

### Marinade
* 1/2 cup hoisin sauce
* 1/4 cup rice vinegar
* 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
* 2 tablespoons soy sauce
* 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
* 6 cloves garlic, minced
* Freshly-cracked black pepper

### Moo Shu
* 1 pound boneless pork chops (or boneless skinless chicken breasts), sliced into very thin strips
* 3 tablespoons peanut or vegetable oil
* 2 large eggs, whisked
* 1 (14-ounce) bag coleslaw
* 8 ounces shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and thinly-sliced
* 4 scallions, thinly sliced (green parts only)
* **For serving:** flour tortillas, lettuce cups, rice, or quinoa
* **Optional toppings:** extra hoisin, extra soy sauce, extra thinly-sliced green onions, toasted sesame seeds

## Instructions

### Make the Marinade
1. Whisk all marinade ingredients together in a medium mixing bowl until combined.
2. Separate the marinade into two portions: one to marinate the meat, and one to serve as the cooking sauce.

### Make the Moo Shu
1. Add the thinly sliced **pork** (or **chicken**) to half of the marinade and toss until evenly coated. Set aside for 5 minutes.
2. While the meat marinates, heat 2 teaspoons of **oil** in a large saute pan or wok over medium heat. Add the whisked **eggs** and let them sit until cooked into a large omelet. Transfer the eggs to a cutting board and set aside. Chop the omelet into small, thin pieces when you have a chance.
3. Return the pan to the stove and increase the heat to high. Add 1 more tablespoon of **oil**. Use tongs to lift the meat out of the marinade and transfer it to the pan, discarding the excess marinade. Saute the meat for about 3 to 4 minutes, breaking it up and stirring occasionally until cooked and tender. Transfer the cooked meat to a separate plate.
4. Add the remaining **oil** to the pan along with the **coleslaw**, **mushrooms**, and half of the **scallions**. Saute for 2 to 3 minutes until the cabbage begins to wilt and soften.
5. Add the reserved marinade cooking sauce to the pan and toss to combine. Cook for an additional 2 minutes.
6. Stir in the cooked meat and chopped eggs, tossing until combined. Taste and season with extra salt, pepper, hoisin, or soy sauce as needed. Sprinkle with the remaining scallions.
7. Serve immediately with your choice of wrappers or base, and garnish with optional toppings if desired.
