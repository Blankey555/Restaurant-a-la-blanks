## Ingredients

- 8 round rice-paper wrappers
- 180g chicken mince
- 3/4 cup finely shredded cabbage
- 1/2 carrot, finely shredded
- 2 spring onions, finely sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 2 teaspoons sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Combine the chicken mince, cabbage, carrot, half the spring onions, and two of the minced garlic cloves.
2. Soften one rice-paper wrapper in water and place it on a damp board.
3. Add the filling and fold it tightly into a compact parcel. Repeat with all eight wrappers.
4. Heat one teaspoon of sesame oil in a pan over medium heat.
5. Fry the dumplings for 4 to 5 minutes per side, until crisp and the chicken is fully cooked.
6. Mix the soy sauce, honey, remaining garlic, and remaining sesame oil, then pour it into the pan.
7. Toss until glossy and sticky, then finish with sesame seeds and the remaining spring onion.
