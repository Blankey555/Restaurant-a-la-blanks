## Ingredients

### Sauce
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil
- 1 (1 1/2-inch) piece fresh ginger, peeled and minced
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and minced
- 2 tablespoons orange zest, plus the juice of one orange
- 3 garlic cloves, peeled and minced
- 1/4 cup light brown sugar
- 1/4 cup rice vinegar (do not use seasoned rice vinegar)
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon fish sauce

### Beef
- 1 large egg white
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 pinch kosher salt
- 1 boneless rib-eye steak, approximately 1 to 1 1/2 pounds, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1/4 cup neutral oil
- 6 scallions, white and green parts cut into inch-long pieces and separated
- 2 to 4 dried red chiles, or to taste

## Instructions

1. Sauce: heat the oil in a small saucepan over medium-high until shimmering. Cook ginger, jalapeño and zest until soft, 2 to 3 minutes; add garlic and cook 1 to 2 minutes more.
2. Stir in orange juice, brown sugar, vinegar, soy sauce and fish sauce. Bring to a boil, lower heat and simmer, stirring now and then, until reduced by half, 10 to 15 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, mix egg white, cornstarch and salt in a bowl and toss the beef in it.
4. Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat until nearly smoking. Add the beef in one layer and leave it until crisp and golden underneath, 60 to 90 seconds. Add scallion whites and chiles, flip the beef and cook about 3 minutes more for medium-rare. Move to a platter.
5. Pour the sauce into the hot pan and boil, stirring, as it thickens. Return the beef and scallion whites and toss to coat. Plate and scatter with scallion greens.

## Notes

- The source suggests serving with steamed broccoli and white rice.
