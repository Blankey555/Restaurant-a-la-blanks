## Ingredients

### Beef and Marinade
* 1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced across the grain, then cut into pieces about 1 1/2 inches long (not thick chunks)
* 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
* 1 tablespoon cornstarch
* 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine
* 1 tablespoon water
* 1 tablespoon soy sauce
* 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
* 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
* 1/2 cup cornstarch, for dredging

### Mongolian Sauce
* 1/4 cup brown sugar
* 1/2 cup beef broth
* 1/4 cup soy sauce
* 2 teaspoons dark soy sauce
* 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
* 1 teaspoon sesame oil

### Slurry
* 1 tablespoon cornstarch
* 2 tablespoons water

### Stir-Fry
* 1/3 cup vegetable oil, for frying
* 1/2 small yellow onion, thinly sliced
* 2 teaspoons ginger, minced
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 6 dried chili peppers
* 5 stalks green onion, cut into 2-inch pieces, white and green parts divided

## Instructions

1. **Marinate the beef.** In a large bowl, mix the sliced **beef** with the baking soda, cornstarch, Shaoxing wine, water, soy sauce, white pepper, and vegetable oil until thoroughly combined. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes.
2. **Mix the sauce.** In a medium bowl, mix the **brown sugar**, beef broth, soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil until combined.
3. **Dredge the beef.** Put the 1/2 cup **cornstarch** in a large bowl, dredge each slice of beef evenly, and shake off any excess.
4. **Fry the beef.** Heat the **vegetable oil** in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat until hot. Working in batches so the pan isn't crowded, cook the beef in a single layer for 1 minute per side. Remove the beef from the wok.
5. **Cook the aromatics.** Drain the oil, leaving about 1 tablespoon in the wok. Over medium heat, saute the **onion**, ginger, garlic, dried chilies, and the white parts of the **green onion** for 1 minute until fragrant.
6. **Sauce and thicken.** Add the sauce and simmer 1 to 2 minutes. Mix the **cornstarch** and water into a slurry and stir it in slowly until the sauce thickens.
7. **Finish.** Add the beef and the green parts of the green onion and toss about 30 seconds, until combined and the green onions are just wilted.
8. Serve immediately with steamed rice.

## Notes

- The written recipe's 30-minute total doesn't include its own "at least 30 minutes" marinade.
- The written sauce step lists water and white pepper, but its ingredient list (and the video) uses beef broth with no pepper in the sauce. This note follows the ingredient list.
