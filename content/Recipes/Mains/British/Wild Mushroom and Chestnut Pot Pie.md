## Ingredients

- 120g mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 35g cooked chestnuts, roughly chopped
- 25g onion, finely chopped
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 teaspoon butter
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon plain flour
- 80ml vegetable stock
- Fresh thyme
- Cream
- Soy sauce
- Puff pastry
- Egg, beaten, for the wash
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Brown the mushrooms in the butter and olive oil.
2. Add the onion, garlic, chestnuts, and thyme; cook until softened.
3. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
4. Gradually add the stock, cream, and soy sauce.
5. Simmer until thickened and season to taste.
6. Transfer the filling to a deep oven-safe ramekin.
7. Cover with puff pastry, seal the edges, and cut a steam slit.
8. Brush with the beaten egg.
9. Bake at 200°C for 20 to 25 minutes, until deeply golden.

## Notes

- The source didn't specify quantities for the cream, soy sauce, thyme, or puff pastry. Use enough cream and soy sauce to loosen the sauce to taste, and enough puff pastry to cover your ramekin.
