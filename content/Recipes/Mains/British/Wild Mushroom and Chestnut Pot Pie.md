## Ingredients

- 120g mixed mushrooms, sliced
- 35g cooked chestnuts, roughly chopped
- 25g onion, finely chopped
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 teaspoon butter
- 1 teaspoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon plain flour
- 80ml vegetable stock
- Fresh thyme: start with 1/4 teaspoon picked leaves (suggested)
- Cream: start with 1 tablespoon, adding more only if needed (suggested)
- Soy sauce: start with 1/4 teaspoon, then adjust to taste (suggested)
- Chilled puff pastry, enough to cover the dish with a small overhang
- Egg, beaten, for the wash; use only enough to brush the top
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C conventional (suggested interpretation of the saved temperature). If using a fan oven, follow the pastry manufacturer's equivalent setting. Keep the puff pastry chilled while preparing the filling.
2. Heat the butter and olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook until their released liquid has evaporated and they begin to brown.
3. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the onion, garlic, cooked chestnuts and thyme; cook, stirring, until the onion softens without scorching the garlic.
4. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually stir in the stock, then the suggested starting amounts of cream and soy sauce.
5. Simmer, stirring, until the sauce coats a spoon and no watery liquid pools around the filling. If too thick, add cream or water a teaspoon at a time. Taste before adding more soy sauce or salt; soy sauce is seasoning, not the liquid used to loosen the sauce.
6. Let the filling cool until no longer steaming, then transfer to a small oven-safe pie dish or ramekin. A 350 to 450ml dish is a suggested starting size, not source-verified. Leave about 1cm headroom; use a larger dish or a second ramekin if needed rather than overfilling.
7. Cut chilled pastry to cover the dish with a small overhang. Lightly brush the rim with beaten egg, lay the pastry over the filling without stretching it, and press the edges onto the rim to seal. Cut a steam slit in the center.
8. Lightly brush the pastry top with beaten egg. Place the dish on a baking sheet to catch drips.
9. Bake for approximately 20 to 25 minutes, until the pastry is risen and deeply golden and the filling bubbles through the slit. Timing may vary with the dish, pastry and filling temperature; extend as needed rather than relying on the clock alone.
