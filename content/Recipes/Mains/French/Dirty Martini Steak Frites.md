## Ingredients

- 10 cups neutral oil, divided
- 1.5 lbs skirt steak or NY strip steak
- Kosher salt
- 3/4 cup pitted chopped green olives (such as Castlevetrano or Cerignola), plus 2 tbsp olive brine
- 1 clove garlic, chopped
- 1/2 cup gin
- 1/4 cup dry vermouth
- 1/2 cup low-sodium beef broth
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 14 oz frozen shoestring French fries, divided
- 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp white wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp honey
- 4 oz mixed greens
- 1/2 cup fresh flat-leaf parsley leaves
- 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese (optional)

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven to 375°F.
2. Pat steak dry and season with salt. Cook in a skillet over medium-high heat until browned, about 2-3 minutes per side.
3. Transfer steak to a cutting board and cook olives in the same skillet until starting to brown, about 2 minutes.
4. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute. Stir in gin and vermouth to deglaze pan.
5. Bring sauce to a simmer and cook until reduced by two-thirds, about 5 minutes.
6. Add broth and stir to combine. Remove from heat and stir in olive brine and butter.
7. Fry French fries in batches until crispy, about 3-4 minutes per batch.
8. Whisk together mustard, vinegar, honey, and a pinch of salt. Drizzle in oil until dressing is smooth.
9. Toss mixed greens and parsley with the dressing until coated.
10. Slice steak against the grain and serve with olive sauce, blue cheese (if using), fries, and salad alongside.

## Notes

- Fries can be made ahead and reheated.
- Sauce improves as it sits; refrigerate for up to 1 day before serving.
