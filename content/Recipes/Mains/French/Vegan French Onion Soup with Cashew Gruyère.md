## Ingredients

### Soup
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil (or plant-based butter)
- 5 yellow onions, sliced
- 1/2 shallot, sliced
- 2 tbsp flour
- 6 cups vegetable broth
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 3 tbsp pure maple syrup
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and pepper to taste

### Cashew Gruyère
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 1/2 cup raw cashews (soaked six hours or boiled 20 minutes)
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp nutritional yeast
- 1 tsp pure maple syrup
- 2 tsp tapioca flour

## Instructions

1. Add the sliced onions and shallot to a stock pot over medium heat and saute with oil or butter, stirring every three minutes or so, until the onions caramelize, about 45 minutes.
2. While the onions caramelize, put all of the ingredients for the vegan Gruyere into a blender and blend until completely smooth. Transfer the mix to a sauce pan over medium heat and whisk until thick and bubbly, about five minutes, then set aside.
3. Once the onions caramelize, coat them with the flour, then add the red wine to the stock pot and turn the heat to high. Allow the wine to reduce by half, about five minutes, then turn the heat back down to medium.
4. Add the broth, spices, soy sauce, rosemary, and bay leaf. Simmer for 15 minutes and turn on the broiler.
5. Remove the rosemary sprig and bay leaf, then ladle the soup into crocks on a baking tray and top with crusty bread and a dollop of the cheese.
6. Broil until the cheese is toasty and bubbly.

## Notes

- Improves as it sits. Can be made 1 to 2 days ahead.
- Keeps in the fridge for 3 to 4 days.
