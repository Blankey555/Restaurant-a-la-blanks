## Ingredients

- 400g fresh udon noodles
- 3 large brown onions, thinly sliced
- 300ml beef stock
- 100g gruyere, grated
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 tablespoon white miso
- 1 tablespoon plain flour
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter and cook the onions over low heat for 25 to 30 minutes, until deeply caramelized.
2. Add the garlic and thyme; cook for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the flour, followed by the stock, miso, and soy sauce.
4. Simmer for 4 to 5 minutes, until glossy and slightly thickened.
5. Fold in the udon and black pepper, coating the noodles completely.
6. Transfer to an ovenproof skillet and cover with the gruyere.
7. Bake at 220°C for 5 to 7 minutes, until bubbling and deeply blistered.

## Notes

- Made with beef stock as written, so this isn't vegetarian despite reading like a produce-forward dish. Swap in a mushroom or vegetable stock to make it one.
