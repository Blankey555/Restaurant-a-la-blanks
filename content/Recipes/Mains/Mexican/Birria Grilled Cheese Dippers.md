## Ingredients

- 200g beef chuck
- 4 slices rustic sandwich bread
- 80g grated cheddar and mozzarella mixture
- 2 dried guajillo chillies
- 250ml beef stock
- 1/4 brown onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1/2 lime

## Instructions

1. Remove the stems from the guajillo chillies and soak them in hot water for 10 minutes.
2. Blend the softened chillies with the onion, garlic, cumin, and a little of the stock.
3. Combine the beef, chili mixture, and remaining stock in a pot.
4. Cover and simmer gently for 60 to 75 minutes, until the beef pulls apart easily.
5. Shred the beef and reserve the cooking broth for dipping.
6. Fill the bread with the shredded beef and cheese, then butter the outer surfaces.
7. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes per side until crisp and the cheese has melted. Cut each sandwich into two dippers and serve with the broth and lime.
