## Ingredients

- 6 lamb shanks, about 1 pound each
- Sea salt
- Grapeseed or canola oil
- 6 cups coarsely chopped yellow onions
- Scant 1/2 cup sliced garlic cloves
- 1/4 cup grated fresh ginger
- 1 1/2 tablespoons ras el hanout
- 2 teaspoons saffron threads
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 6 cups chicken stock or water
- 4 cups pomegranate juice
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar
- 2 teaspoons pomegranate molasses or grenadine
- Seeds of 1 medium pomegranate
- 1/4 cup cilantro, roughly chopped
- 1/4 cup pistachios, chopped
- Flaky salt, like Maldon or fleur de sel, for sprinkling

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 325°F and salt the shanks.
2. Film a large lidded ovenproof pot with oil and brown the shanks in batches over medium-high heat, about 3 minutes per side. Set aside on a rimmed sheet.
3. Add the onion, garlic, and fresh ginger with a pinch of salt and cook over moderate heat about 5 minutes, until the onions turn translucent.
4. Stir in the ras el hanout, saffron, ground ginger, and pepper for 1 minute. Add 1 cup stock and boil about 3 minutes until syrupy, scraping the bottom.
5. Return the shanks and their juices, add the remaining stock and the pomegranate juice, and bring to a boil. Cover and bake 2 to 2 1/2 hours, until very tender.
6. Move the shanks to a sheet and tent with foil. Cool the liquid 15 minutes, skim off the fat, then simmer about 20 minutes, stirring often, until reduced to a sauce.
7. Season with salt, then add vinegar and pomegranate molasses a few teaspoons at a time until balanced. Return the shanks and simmer, basting, until hot and glazed.
8. Serve topped with pomegranate seeds, cilantro, pistachios, and flaky salt.

## Notes

- Recipe from chef Sameh Wadi.
- Water can stand in for the chicken stock; grenadine can stand in for pomegranate molasses.
