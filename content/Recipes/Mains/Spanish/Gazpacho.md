## Ingredients
* 4 chopped tomatoes
* 1 chopped green pepper
* 1 large Spanish onion (use Vidalia for less bite)
* 2 chopped cucumbers
* 3 stalks chopped celery
* 2 tsp snipped fresh parsley
* 1 tsp snipped fresh chives
* 7 tbsp wine vinegar
* 6 tbsp olive oil
* 1/4 tsp fresh ground pepper (or more to taste)
* 1 tsp salt
* 1 clove minced garlic
* 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
* 5 drops Tabasco (or more to taste)
* 1 (64 oz) bottle V-8 juice (use a larger bottle and extra spices if you prefer a juicier soup)

## Instructions
1. Hand chop all vegetables to ensure a uniform size and avoid a mushy texture; do not use a food processor.
2. Mix all ingredients well in a large container.
3. Let stand in the refrigerator for at least 8 hours to allow the flavors to develop.

> **Recipe Note:** The original recipe calls for 1 small jar of green olives, but they are excluded here as they can overpower the flavor of the fresh vegetables.
