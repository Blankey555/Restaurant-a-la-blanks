## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups plain yogurt
- 1 small cucumber, peeled
- Salt and pepper
- 10 cardamom pods, seeds removed and hulls discarded
- 1 3-inch cinnamon stick
- 1 teaspoon cloves
- 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg pieces (smack a whole nutmeg with a hammer)
- 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
- 1 tablespoon fennel seeds
- 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
- 2 tablespoons neutral oil, like corn or grapeseed, if necessary
- 4 6-ounce salmon fillets
- Lime wedges

## Instructions

1. If the yogurt is thin, drain it in a cheesecloth-lined strainer set over a bowl. Halve the cucumber lengthwise, scoop out the seeds, chop the flesh, salt it and let it drain in a strainer.
2. For the spice mix, toast all the spices in a skillet over medium heat until fragrant, about 2 minutes, then grind coarsely in a spice mill.
3. Heat a skillet over medium heat (add the oil if it is not nonstick). Season the salmon with salt and pepper, add to the pan and raise the heat to medium-high. Meanwhile combine the cucumber, yogurt and 1 tablespoon of the spice mix; taste and adjust seasoning.
4. After 4 minutes, flip the salmon and cook another 2 to 5 minutes. Serve browned side up with the sauce and lime on the side.

## Notes

- The spice mix makes more than the 1 tablespoon used here.
