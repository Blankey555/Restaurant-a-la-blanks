## Ingredients

- 5 1/2 ounces ground wheat (atta), plus more for dusting
- 1/2 cup water
- 3 large eggs
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1 green chili, chopped
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1/2 tablespoon red chili powder
- Kosher salt, to taste
- 2 tablespoons ghee (or any oil)

## Instructions

1. Paratha dough: add water to the atta a few spoonfuls at a time until it can be kneaded. Knead until smooth, 3 to 5 minutes, and set aside.
2. Onion salad: mix the onion, chili, and lemon juice.
3. Whisk the eggs with chili powder and salt.
4. Divide the dough into 3 golf-ball-sized pieces, dust with atta, and roll each to a 7-inch circle.
5. Heat 1 tablespoon ghee in a small skillet. Cook each bread 2 minutes, flip for 30 seconds, and remove.
6. Add the remaining ghee and pour in a third of the egg, spreading thin. When the bottom has set but the top is runny, lay a bread on it, longer-cooked side down. Flip and cook 1 to 2 minutes until the bread is done.
7. Spoon a third of the onion salad on the egg side, roll up, and serve hot. Repeat.

## Notes

- From Harneet Baweja of Gunpowder (London), in the Munchies Workaholics series.
- He says pre-made dough works, and the roll can be filled with leftovers such as chicken.
