## Ingredients

### For the herb sauce

- 15 grams mint leaves
- 15 grams parsley leaves
- 15 grams dill
- 15 ml olive oil
- 10 ml white wine vinegar
- 200 grams yogurt
- kosher salt, to taste

### For the lamb patties

- 400 grams ground lamb
- 1/2 red onion, chopped
- 40 grams minced florina pepper
- 4 grams paprika
- 3 grams black peppercorns, finely ground
- kosher salt, to taste
- canola oil, for rolling the patties

### For serving

- 4 medium pita bread
- 80 grams lettuce, shredded
- 1 ripe tomato, sliced

## Instructions

1. Herb sauce: blend the dill, mint, parsley, olive oil, and vinegar until smooth. Stir in the yogurt, salt to taste, and refrigerate.
2. Light a grill. Mix the lamb, onion, florina pepper, paprika, black pepper, and salt, then shape into 4 patties with oiled hands.
3. Grill the patties, flipping once, about 12 minutes for medium-rare. Grill the pitas, flipping once, until lightly charred, about 2 minutes.
4. Set each pita on a sheet of parchment, stuff with lamb, lettuce, and tomato, and drizzle with herb sauce.
