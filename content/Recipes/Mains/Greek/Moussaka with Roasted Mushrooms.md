## Ingredients

### Eggplant
- 2 to 2 1/2 pounds (3 medium or 2 large) eggplant
- Salt
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil

### Filling
- 1/2 pound minced or ground lamb or lean beef
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- 2 to 4 large garlic cloves, minced (to taste)
- 1/2 pound roasted mushroom mix, or for a vegetarian version 1 pound
- 2 pounds tomatoes, peeled and chopped, with juice, or 1 28-ounce can, pulsed in a food processor with juice
- 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 1/4 rounded teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 3 cloves, ground
- Pinch of allspice (2 or 3 berries), ground
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/2 to 1 cup hot water
- Salt to taste
- Freshly ground pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 1 egg, beaten

### Topping
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 1 1/4 cups plain Greek yogurt
- Pinch of salt
- Pinch of pepper
- Pinch of paprika
- 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan, kefalotyri or kashkaval cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 450°F with racks in the middle and lower third. Slice eggplant lengthwise about 1/3 inch thick. Lay on 2 oiled foil-lined sheets, salt, and brush with oil.
2. Roast about 20 minutes, swapping pans after 10, until soft and spotty brown. Fold the foil over into a sealed packet and let steam 20 to 30 minutes.
3. For the filling, soften the onions in oil over medium heat about 5 minutes, add garlic for 30 seconds, then raise heat to medium-high and brown the meat about 3 minutes.
4. Add the mushroom mix, tomatoes, paprika, cinnamon, allspice, cloves, sugar, bay leaf, salt and just enough water to barely cover. Simmer covered on low 45 to 60 minutes, then uncovered 5 to 10 minutes until the liquid is nearly gone. Discard bay leaf, season, cool slightly, and stir in the egg and parsley.
5. Heat oven to 350°F. Oil a 3-quart dish, layer in half the eggplant, all the filling, then the rest of the eggplant. Bake 30 minutes.
6. Beat the eggs and yogurt with about 1/2 teaspoon salt, pepper and paprika. Pour over the moussaka, sprinkle with cheese, and bake 25 to 30 minutes more until puffed and golden. Serve warm.

## Notes

- For a vegetarian version, the source omits the meat and uses 1 pound of roasted mushroom mix.
- The roasted mushroom mix is a separate NYT recipe.
