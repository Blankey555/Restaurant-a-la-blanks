## Ingredients

### Sauce
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups finely diced onion
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1-inch piece cinnamon stick
- Large pinch saffron, crumbled
- Salt and pepper
- 3 cups chicken broth, vegetable broth or water

### Meatballs
- 1 1/2 cups cubed day-old firm white bread
- 1 cup milk
- 1 pound ground beef or lamb
- 1 large egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/8 teaspoon grated nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 teaspoon turmeric
- 2 teaspoons paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1/4 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 3 tablespoons chopped parsley
- 3 tablespoons chopped cilantro
- 3 tablespoons finely chopped scallion
- All-purpose flour, for dusting
- Olive oil or vegetable oil

### Couscous (optional)
- 1 cup giant couscous, m'hamsa, or medium couscous
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1/2 cup golden raisins, soaked in hot water to soften, then drained
- Salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon

## Instructions

1. Sauce: heat the oil in a wide heavy saucepan over medium-high and cook the onion without browning, about 5 minutes. Stir in garlic, tomato paste, cinnamon stick and saffron, season well, and sizzle 1 minute. Add the broth and simmer gently 5 minutes.
2. Meatballs: soak the bread in the milk about 5 minutes, then squeeze dry.
3. Combine the bread, meat and egg with the salt, pepper, garlic and all the ground spices, mixing by hand. Add 2 tablespoons each of parsley, cilantro and scallion and knead 1 minute.
4. Roll into quarter-size balls and dust lightly with flour. Fry in a 1/4-inch layer of hot oil over medium-high until just browned, about 2 minutes per side. Drain.
5. Simmer the meatballs in the sauce, covered, over medium heat about 20 minutes until tender.
6. If making couscous, cook per package, fluff, and stir in the butter and raisins. Season with salt and cinnamon.
7. Garnish with the remaining herbs and serve with the couscous.

## Notes

- Both the sauce and the meatball mixture can be made several hours, up to a day, ahead (per the source).
- The source suggests serving with roasted tomatoes if desired.
- Not gluten-free as written (bread, flour).
