## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 Spanish onions, chopped
- 1 large jalapeño pepper, seeded if desired, chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger root
- 2 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, more to taste
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
- Pinch of cayenne
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 fennel bulb, diced (save fronds for garnish)
- 1 very large bunch chard, stems sliced 1/2-inch thick, leaves torn into bite-size pieces
- 2 carrots, peeled and diced
- 1 large turnip, peeled and diced
- 1 pound dried chickpeas, soaked overnight in water to cover or quick-soaked
- 1/3 cup diced dried apricots
- 2 tablespoons chopped preserved lemon, more to taste
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro, more for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over high heat. Sauté onion and jalapeño until limp, 3 minutes. Add garlic, ginger, salt and all the spices and cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes. Add tomato paste and cook 1 minute until darkened but not burnt (lower the heat if it darkens too fast).
2. Add fennel, chard stems, carrots and turnip and sauté until starting to soften, about 10 minutes. Add the chickpeas and enough water to barely cover.
3. Bring to a simmer over high heat, then partly cover, reduce to medium-low and simmer 1 1/2 to 2 hours until the chickpeas are soft, adding water as needed to keep it stew-like.
4. Stir in chard leaves, apricots and preserved lemon and simmer until the chard is tender, about 5 minutes. Adjust salt and serve topped with cilantro and the fennel fronds.

## Notes

- The source offers quick-soaking the chickpeas as an alternative to soaking overnight.
