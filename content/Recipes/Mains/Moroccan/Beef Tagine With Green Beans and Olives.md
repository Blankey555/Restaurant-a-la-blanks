## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 medium onions, diced (about 3 cups)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced or pressed
- 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 small pinch saffron (optional)
- Fine sea salt and black pepper
- 2 1/2 pounds boneless beef chuck or other stewing beef, trimmed of excess fat and cut into 2-inch pieces
- 3/4 cup vegetable or beef stock, or water, plus more if needed
- 1 cup cilantro leaves and tender stems, finely chopped, plus more for serving
- 1 1/2 pounds green beans, trimmed
- 2 to 3 tablespoons seeded and finely chopped preserved lemon (about 1/2 lemon), plus more to taste
- 1/2 cup pitted Castelvetrano or Kalamata olives, for serving
- Bread, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-low. Add the onions, garlic, turmeric, ginger, saffron if using, 3/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper. Cook, stirring often, 5 to 7 minutes, until the onions start to soften.
2. Add the beef, raise the heat to medium, and cook about 7 minutes, stirring now and then, until lightly browned.
3. Add the stock (or water) and cilantro and bring to a boil over high heat.
4. Cover, lower the heat, and simmer about 1 1/2 hours, until the beef is soft when pressed with a fork but not falling apart.
5. Stir in the green beans and preserved lemon and bring back to a boil over high heat. The beans should be partly submerged; add stock or water if needed. Cover and simmer on low 15 to 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the beans are tender.
6. Taste and add more preserved lemon or salt. Top with the olives and more cilantro and serve with bread.

## Notes

- Recipe by Nargisse Benkabbou. Cooked in the m'qualli style (garlic, ground ginger, turmeric), a staple family meal in many Moroccan homes.
- Variation from the source: swap or add peas, chopped carrots, or both in place of some of the green beans.
- The source suggests it for meal prep.
