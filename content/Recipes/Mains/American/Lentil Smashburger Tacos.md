## Ingredients

### Patty
- 1 can lentils, drained and rinsed
- 1/2 onion
- 1/4 red bell pepper
- 4 to 5 white mushrooms
- 1/2 cup gluten-free oats
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- 1 teaspoon each: salt, pepper, smoked paprika, dried thyme, garlic powder, onion powder, nutritional yeast

### Burger Sauce
- 1/3 cup vegan mayo
- 3 tablespoons ketchup
- 1 tablespoon mustard
- 2 tablespoons sweet relish
- Squeeze of lemon

### To Serve
- Tortillas
- Cheese, optional

## Instructions

1. Add the peppers, onion, and mushroom to a food processor. Pulse until roughly chopped and around the same size.
2. Saute this mixture in a pan over medium heat until most of the moisture has cooked out (optionally sprinkle with salt to draw out more water). Remove from the pan and set aside.
3. In a small food processor, add the oats and spices and blend until roughly combined.
4. In a large bowl, add the lentils, cooked vegetables, blended oats, and ketchup. Mash to form the patty mix, until it holds shape.
5. Form a patty on a tortilla and cook patty-side down. Optionally add a weight on top to keep it flat.
6. Flip after about 3 to 5 minutes, add cheese, and steam to melt.
7. For the burger sauce, add all ingredients to a small bowl and mix.
8. Assemble with toppings of choice and enjoy.

## Notes

- Cheese is called for when flipping the patty but isn't listed with a quantity or type in the original recipe; use whatever melts well for you, or omit to keep it vegan.
- The oats are specified gluten-free; use gluten-free tortillas as well if that matters to you, since tortillas aren't addressed in the original.
- As written (without cheese), this is vegan.
