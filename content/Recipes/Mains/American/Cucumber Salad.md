## Ingredients

- 2 cucumbers (thinly sliced)
- ½ red onion (thinly sliced)
- 2 tablespoons green onion (diced)
- 1 teaspoon sea salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon dried garlic
- ½ lime (juice)
- ¼ cup white vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey

## Instructions

1. Combine cucumbers, red onion, green onion, sea salt, pepper, dried garlic, lime juice, vinegar, and honey in a bowl.
2. Stir to combine.
3. Serve as a side dish or light meal, and enjoy!

## Notes

- This salad is perfect for when you're short on time but still want something crisp and clean.
