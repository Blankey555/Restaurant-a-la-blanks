## Ingredients

### Filling
- 180g beef chuck, cubed
- 1 teaspoon vegetable oil
- 40g onion, diced
- 50g carrot, diced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 teaspoon plain flour
- 80ml cloudy apple cider or unsweetened apple juice
- 120ml beef stock
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
- Salt and black pepper

### Cornbread Topping
- 40g fine cornmeal
- 20g plain flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 35ml milk
- 15g mature cheddar, grated
- 1 teaspoon melted butter
- 1 tablespoon sliced spring onion

## Instructions

1. Season the beef and sear it in hot oil until deeply browned.
2. Add the onion and carrot; cook for 4 to 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the garlic and flour.
4. Add the cider, stock, Worcestershire sauce, mustard, and thyme.
5. Cover and braise at 160°C for 75 to 90 minutes, until the beef is tender and the sauce has thickened.
6. Combine the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, and a pinch of salt.
7. Stir in the milk, butter, cheddar, and spring onion to form a soft dough.
8. Spoon pieces of the dough over the beef filling.
9. Bake uncovered at 200°C for 18 to 22 minutes, until the topping is golden and cooked through.
