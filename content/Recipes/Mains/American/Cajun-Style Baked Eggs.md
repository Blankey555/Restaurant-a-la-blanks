## Ingredients

- 1 cup heavy cream
- 4 large eggs
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1 cup cheese curds (or 2/3 cup favorite melting cheese)
- 1 link andouille sausage, thinly sliced
- Jalapeño Tabasco
- Buttered French bread, to serve

## Instructions

1. Put the cream and cracked eggs in an 8-inch nonstick skillet, season, and gently swirl with your fingers without breaking the yolks.
2. Scatter the cheese curds and sausage into the cream around the eggs and put 2 dashes of Tabasco on the eggs.
3. Bring to a low simmer over medium, then turn to low, cover, and cook 5 minutes, until the whites are set and yolks runny.
4. Serve with buttered French bread.
