## Ingredients

- 4 burger buns (sesame-topped or brioche)
- 1 tbsp (15ml) sunflower oil
- 500g (1lb 2oz) steak mince (15 to 20% fat is good)
- 4 slices mild cheddar or burger cheese
- 1 red onion, finely sliced
- handfuls of iceberg lettuce, finely shredded
- 2 tomatoes, sliced
- 1 large gherkin, sliced lengthways
- mayonnaise, ketchup, and American mustard (or your preferred sauces), to serve

## Instructions

1. Halve the buns and toast or grill the cut sides; set aside. Heat the oven to 100°C (80°C fan, gas 1/4).
2. Split the mince into four loose piles and salt generously. Heat 1/4 tbsp oil in a heavy frying pan or skillet over high heat and add one pile.
3. Quickly cover it with a square of baking parchment and press the patty down as hard as you can with a second, smaller pan (oven gloves or the end of a rolling pin help; mind the heat and steam). Cook 2 minutes.
4. Peel off the parchment, flip, press with a spatula, top with cheese, and cook 1 minute. Keep warm on a tray in the oven while you cook the rest the same way.
5. Assemble: bun base, onion, lettuce, and tomato, then the patty, gherkin slices, and bun top. Serve with sauces on the side.
