## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts or thighs, diced
- 3 tablespoons ponzu sauce
- 1 tablespoon low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 lb ground hot Italian sausage, mild if preferred
- 2 tablespoons neutral oil, divided
- 1 large head green cabbage or 2 small heads, chopped
- 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced
- Pinch of salt
- 2 tablespoons butter, divided
- 4 garlic cloves, minced or pressed
- 6 oz wonton wraps, like Dynasty or Nasoya brand (optional, for noodles)
- Squeeze of fresh lime juice (optional, for the noodles)
- 1/4 cup ponzu sauce
- 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce, plus more to taste
- 2 to 3 tablespoons chili crunch or chili oil, plus more for serving
- 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar, adjust if desired
- Toppings: fresh cilantro, sliced green onions, chopped peanuts

## Instructions

1. Combine the chicken with 3 tablespoons ponzu, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, and 1 tablespoon sesame oil. Refrigerate for 15 to 20 minutes while preparing the vegetables and sausage.
2. Heat a large deep skillet or wok over medium heat. Add neutral oil if needed, followed by the sausage. Cook until browned and cooked through, crumbling as it cooks. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate, leaving about 1 tablespoon of rendered fat in the skillet.
3. Drain and discard the excess chicken marinade. Add the chicken to the hot skillet in a single layer. Cook undisturbed for about 4 minutes, then flip and cook about 3 minutes more.
4. Push the chicken to one side. Add 1 tablespoon butter and 2 cloves garlic. Cook until fragrant, then toss the chicken through the garlic butter and cook about 1 minute longer, until the chicken reaches 165°F. Remove from the skillet.
5. Add the onions and a pinch of salt to the skillet, with a little more neutral oil if needed. Cook about 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until browned.
6. Add the remaining butter and garlic. Cook briefly until fragrant.
7. Add the cabbage and toss with the onions. Spread into an even layer and increase the heat to medium-high. Cook 10 to 12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the cabbage is tender with caramelized edges.
8. Optional wonton noodles: while the cabbage cooks, bring a pot of water to a boil. Slice the wonton wrappers into noodle-like strips and separate the layers. Boil about 2 minutes and drain, then toss immediately with soy sauce, chili crunch, and lime juice. Fold into the finished dish.
9. Combine the chili oil sauce ingredients in a microwave-safe bowl. Warm briefly until the brown sugar dissolves.
10. Return the chicken and sausage to the skillet. Pour the sauce over everything and toss to coat. Taste and add more chili crunch, ponzu, or soy sauce as desired.
11. Serve topped with cilantro, sliced green onions, chopped peanuts, and additional chili crunch.

## Notes

- Optional wonton noodles turn this into more of a noodle bowl; leave them out for a lower-carb skillet dish.
- Keeps 3 to 4 days refrigerated in an airtight container.
