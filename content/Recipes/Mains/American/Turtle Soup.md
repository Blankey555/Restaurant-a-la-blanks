## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 4 ribs celery, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 green bell pepper, stemmed and diced
- 1 medium yellow onion, diced
- 2 pounds turtle meat, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cups veal stock
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 (28-ounce) can whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 lemon
- 1/4 cup dry sherry
- 3 tablespoons finely chopped parsley
- 2 hard-boiled eggs, finely chopped

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium-high. Cook the celery, garlic, bell pepper, and onion until soft, 6 minutes.
2. Add the meat and brown, stirring, 6 minutes. Stir in the tomato paste for 2 minutes, then the flour for 2 more.
3. Add the stock, bay leaves, and tomatoes and bring to a boil. Lower to a simmer and cook, stirring now and then, until the meat is tender, 45 minutes to 1 hour.
4. Season with salt and pepper, squeeze in the lemon juice, and stir in the sherry, two-thirds of the parsley, and half the egg.
5. Ladle into bowls and top with the remaining parsley and egg.

## Notes

- The saved Munchies article linked to this recipe; it says to use veal if you cannot find turtle.
