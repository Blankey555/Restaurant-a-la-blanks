## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon dried tarragon
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh tarragon
- 1 1/2 teaspoons celery salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 1/2 teaspoons fennel seeds
- 1 1/2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1 1/2 teaspoons onion powder
- 8 chicken breast tenders
- 2 cups crushed cornflakes
- 1/2 cup (80 grams) cornflour
- 1/2 cup (80 grams) rice flour
- 1/2 cup (65 grams) wheat flour
- vegetable oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, mix the mustard, both tarragons, celery salt, oregano, fennel, garlic powder, salt, and onion powder. Add the chicken and toss to coat. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.
2. Heat 3 inches of oil in a large saucepan to 350°F on a deep-fry thermometer.
3. Put the cornflakes in one bowl and combine the three flours in another. Toss each piece of chicken in the flour, then in the cornflakes, pressing them on.
4. Fry, in batches if needed, about 4 minutes, until golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels and serve with your favorite dipping sauce.
