## Ingredients

### Burgers
- 1 medium eggplant, cut into eight 1/2-inch-thick slices
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium zucchini, sliced on a mandoline 1/8 inch thick
- Kosher salt
- Pepper
- 1 large red beet, peeled and sliced on a mandoline 1/8 inch thick
- 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and sliced on a mandoline 1/8 inch thick
- 8 slices of Muenster cheese
- 4 poppy seed buns, split and toasted
- Red-leaf romaine lettuce leaves or mixed baby greens and sliced dill pickles, for serving

### Carrot Ketchup
- 4 medium carrots, thinly sliced
- 1/2 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 dried apricots, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Asian fish sauce
- 1 1/2 teaspoons Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/4 teaspoon ground fennel

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and line 2 large rimmed baking sheets with foil. On one sheet, toss the eggplant with 3 tablespoons oil at one end and the zucchini with 1 tablespoon at the other. On the second sheet, toss the beet and the sweet potato each with 1 tablespoon oil at opposite ends. Season all with salt and pepper and spread in single layers.
2. Roast until tender, about 10 minutes for the zucchini and about 25 minutes for the rest, rotating the pans halfway. Cool slightly; leave the oven on.
3. Meanwhile, heat the remaining 2 tablespoons oil in a medium saucepan. Cook carrots, onion, apricots and garlic over moderate heat until just soft, about 5 minutes. Add wine and 3/4 cup water and simmer over moderately low heat until the carrots are very tender, 15 minutes.
4. Off the heat, stir in vinegar, fish sauce, Worcestershire, soy sauce, ginger and fennel. Transfer to a blender, cool slightly, and purée until very smooth, adding water by the tablespoon if needed. Season with salt and pepper.
5. On a foil-lined rimmed baking sheet, build 4 stacks: 2 eggplant slices, 1 cheese slice, 4 to 6 slices each of sweet potato and beet, another cheese slice, and 4 to 6 zucchini slices. Bake 5 minutes until the cheese melts and the stacks are hot.
6. Set the stacks on the bottom buns, top with lettuce and pickles, spread carrot ketchup on the top buns, and close.

## Notes

- Not vegetarian as written: the carrot ketchup contains fish sauce and Worcestershire.
