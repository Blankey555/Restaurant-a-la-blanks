## Ingredients

- Large flour tortilla
- Shredded vegan mozzarella cheese
- Olive oil
- Crispy tofu nuggets or vegan chicken nuggets
- Vegan Caesar dressing
- Romaine lettuce, chopped
- Vegan Parmesan, optional

## Instructions

1. Heat a little olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Sprinkle the shredded vegan mozzarella directly into the pan and let it cook until melted and crispy around the edges. Remove from the pan and set aside.
2. Lay the tortilla flat and spread a generous layer of vegan Caesar dressing in the center.
3. Add the vegan mozzarella, followed by the tofu or vegan chicken nuggets.
4. Place the crispy cheese round on top of the nuggets, then add the chopped romaine and a sprinkle of vegan Parmesan if using.
5. Fold the tortilla into a crunchwrap and place it back in the frying pan. Cook until golden and crispy on both sides.
6. Slice in half and serve with extra Caesar dressing for dipping.

## Notes

- The dressing and tofu nugget recipes are the creator's own; make your favorite version of each.
