## Ingredients

- kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1 pound elbow macaroni
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cups whole milk
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon mustard powder
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 6 ounces pimentos, peeled and diced
- 4 ounces cream cheese
- 3 cups grated cheddar cheese
- 2 tablespoons Crystal hot sauce
- 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Boil the macaroni in well-salted water 5 to 6 minutes, drain, and rinse under cold water.
2. Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat, add the flour, and cook, stirring, until blond and no longer raw-tasting, about 4 minutes.
3. Gradually whisk in the milk, then add the onion powder, Worcestershire, cayenne, garlic powder, mustard powder, and paprika. Cook, stirring, until thick, about 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the pimentos, cream cheese, and cheddar until melted and smooth. Add the hot sauce and season with salt and pepper.
5. Combine with the pasta, pour into a baking dish, and scatter the panko over the top.
6. Bake until crisp on top and bubbling, about 45 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
