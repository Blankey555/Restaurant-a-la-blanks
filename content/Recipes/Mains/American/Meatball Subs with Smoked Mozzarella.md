## Ingredients

### For the red sauce

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 5 garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 Spanish onion, chopped
- 1 leek, chopped
- 1/2 cup (125 ml) red wine
- 1 1/2 teaspoons Spanish paprika
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 3 tablespoons kosher salt
- 1 tablespoon freshly ground black pepper
- 4 (28-ounce/794 gram) cans whole peeled tomatoes

### For the meatballs

- 1 pound (500 grams) ground veal
- 1 pound (500 grams) ground pork
- 1/4 pound (120 grams) ground pork fat
- 2 tablespoons crushed red pepper flakes
- 3 tablespoons dried oregano
- 2 tablespoons fennel seeds, ground
- 2 cups fresh breadcrumbs
- 1/2 bunch Italian parsley, chopped
- 1 cup (240 grams) ricotta cheese, as fresh as possible
- 3 large eggs, beaten
- 1/2 cup (125 ml) whole milk
- 2 1/2 tablespoons kosher salt
- freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### For the sandwich

- 1 large baguette
- all of the meatballs in sauce
- 1/2 pound (227 grams) smoked mozzarella cheese, shredded

## Instructions

1. Sauce: in a heavy pot, sweat the garlic, onion, and leek in the olive oil over medium-high until translucent and starting to color, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the wine and cook off the alcohol, 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Add the paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper, then the tomatoes. Rinse the cans with a splash of water and add it. Simmer partly covered over very low heat 30 to 45 minutes.
4. Blend the sauce smooth with a stick blender or in batches in a blender. Adjust salt and pepper.
5. Meatballs: while the sauce cooks, mix all the meatball ingredients. Cook a small test patty and adjust seasoning; add milk if dry, more pepper flakes for heat.
6. Shape into 2-ounce (60 gram) balls, or any size you like.
7. Sear the meatballs: deep-fry briefly at 250°F until crusted and darkened, or brown in a few tablespoons of oil in a heavy pan over medium.
8. Heat the oven to 275°F. Put the meatballs in a deep roasting pan, cover with sauce, cover tightly, and bake 2 hours.
9. Leave the oven on. Cut the baguette into sandwich lengths and split them. Sprinkle mozzarella inside and toast in the oven until melted.
10. Fill with meatballs (halved for a neater sandwich), spoon sauce over, and add more cheese.
