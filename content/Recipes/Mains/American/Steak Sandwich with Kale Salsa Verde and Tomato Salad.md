## Ingredients

- 1/4 ounce fresh mint
- 2 ounces (60 grams) fresh basil
- 1/4 ounce fresh cilantro
- 1 rib celery
- 1 small yellow onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 2 ounces kale
- 3 lemons
- 2 cups (473 ml) olive oil for the salsa verde, plus 2 teaspoons for the salad, 1 tablespoon for the steak, and a drizzle for the bread
- Salt and black pepper, as directed
- 5 ounces (142 grams) heirloom cherry tomatoes
- 2 1/2 ounces (71 grams) Parmesan cheese, shaved
- 12 ounces (340 grams) flap meat
- 2 torta breads

## Instructions

1. Salsa verde: strip the mint, basil, and cilantro leaves, chop roughly, and put in a large bowl. Dice the celery and onion to 1/4 inch and add. Finely chop 1 garlic clove and add; halve the other and set aside. Finely chop the kale and add, along with the zest and juice of 2 lemons. Season with salt and pepper, cover with the olive oil, and stir.
2. Tomato salad: halve the tomatoes and toss with 2 teaspoons olive oil and 1/4 teaspoon each salt and pepper. Add the Parmesan, the zest of the last lemon, and its juice. Toss and set aside.
3. Pat the steak dry, season with 1/4 teaspoon each salt and pepper per steak, and rub with 1 tablespoon olive oil. Sear in a very hot cast-iron grill pan, 3 minutes per side. Rest.
4. Cut the torta bread in half both lengthwise and crosswise, drizzle with oil, and grill the cut sides until toasted. Rub with the halved garlic.
5. Slice the steak 1/2 inch thick, layer onto the bread, top each with 2 tablespoons salsa verde, and close. Serve the tomato salad alongside.
