## Ingredients

- 1/2 teaspoon neutral oil, like canola, or a pat of unsalted butter
- 2 pounds ground chuck, at least 20 percent fat
- Kosher salt and black pepper to taste
- 8 slices cheese (optional)
- 8 soft hamburger buns, lightly toasted
- Lettuce leaves, sliced tomatoes and condiments, as desired

## Instructions

1. Put the oil or butter in a large cast-iron or stainless skillet over medium heat. Gently divide the beef into 8 portions of about 4 ounces and loosely gather each into a ball about 2 inches tall; do not shape patties.
2. Turn the heat to high. Add half the balls, well spaced, and press each with a stiff metal spatula into a burger about 4 inches wide and 1/2 inch thick. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Cook without moving until deeply crusted, just under 2 minutes. Scrape loose, flip, and add cheese if using.
4. Cook about a minute more until done. Transfer to buns, top as you like, and repeat with the rest.

## Notes

- The source suggests two patties on one bun is a reasonable way to serve them.
