## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound pancetta, sliced 1/4-inch thick, then cut into 1/2-inch squares
- 3/4 pound bulk sweet Italian sausage seasoned with fennel
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 cup chopped onions, plus 1 onion (halved) for pan
- 1 cup chopped carrots, plus 2 carrots (halved crosswise) for pan
- 1 1/2 cups chopped celery
- 2 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 teaspoon fennel seed
- 1 3- to 3 1/2-pound chicken, boned, giblets and wings reserved
- 1 4- to 5-pound duck, boned, giblets and wings reserved
- Sea salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup brandy
- 3 tablespoons chopped fresh tarragon
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh thyme
- 2 cups dry baguette in 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 10- to 12-pound turkey, boned, wings and legs left intact

## Instructions

1. The day before, render the pancetta in a large saute pan over low heat 6 to 8 minutes until browned; drain. Brown the sausage in the same pan, breaking it into 1/2-inch bits; drain.
2. Pour off the fat. Add the oil, chopped onion, carrot, celery, garlic and fennel seed and cook over medium 2 minutes. Add the meaty giblets, season, and cook about 5 minutes, turning once. Raise heat, add the brandy, and reduce until nearly gone. Off heat, stir in tarragon and thyme. Chop the giblets into 1/2-inch pieces and fold everything together with the bread cubes. Adjust seasoning, cool, and chill overnight.
3. Next morning, lay the turkey skin side down and season. Spread a third of the stuffing over it, mostly in the center. Trim about two-thirds of the duck fat, butterfly the duck drumsticks, and lay the duck pieces over the matching turkey parts (leg meat into the thigh cavities). Season and spread with another third of stuffing. Top with the chicken, skin side down and matched the same way, season, and add the remaining stuffing.
4. Heat oven to 250°F. With an upholstery needle and 2 feet of thin twine, sew the turkey legs back into shape if needed.
5. With 3 feet of twine, starting at the tail, stitch the sides of the turkey together about every inch to re-form the body, with a helper holding the bird. Do not sew too tightly or it will split.
6. Turn the bird over, mend any torn skin, and truss with a 4-foot length of twine, crisscrossing around the drumsticks, under the bird, over the wings and back, and tying over the breast.
7. Season a roasting pan, set the turducken breast up, season it, and surround with the chicken and duck wings and the halved onion and carrots.
8. Cover with foil and roast. After 2 hours, check and baste about every 30 minutes, rotating the pan. At 130°F internal (likely 4 to 5 hours), remove the foil, raise the oven to 375°F, and baste about every 15 minutes until 165°F at the thickest point.
9. Rest 10 minutes, move to a platter with sturdy spatulas, tent with foil, and rest 15 to 20 minutes more. Strain and degrease the pan juices.
10. Slice crosswise like a loaf of bread with a bread or carving knife and serve with the juices.

## Notes

- The stuffing is made the day before and chilled overnight (per the source).
