## Ingredients

- 2 small garlic cloves, grated
- 2 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 2 teaspoons black pepper
- 2 teaspoons rosemary leaves
- 2 1/2 pounds boneless beef top loin roast (leave the fat on top)
- Extra-virgin olive oil, as needed

## Instructions

1. Stir together garlic, salt, pepper and rosemary and spread the paste over the meat. Cover loosely with plastic and refrigerate at least 3 hours or overnight.
2. Take the meat out and let it sit at room temperature 30 to 60 minutes.
3. Heat the oven to 325°F and set a wire rack in a rimmed baking sheet.
4. Rub the roast lightly with olive oil, set it on the rack and roast 75 to 95 minutes, until the center reads 125 to 130°F for medium-rare.
5. Move to a cutting board and cool completely before carving. Trim the fat cap if serving cold.

## Notes

- The beef can be roasted up to three days ahead; cool completely before refrigerating.
