## Ingredients

* 1/2 diced white onion
* 900g extra lean beef mince / ground beef
* 4 minced garlic cloves
* **Taco Seasoning Mix:** 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1/2 tsp ground cumin, 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp black pepper, 1 tsp onion & garlic powder, 1.5 tsp oregano (can be substituted for store bought taco seasoning)
* 400g canned diced / chopped tomatoes
* 100g light / low fat cheddar cheese (Dairyworks Natural Cheddar)
* 250ml chicken broth
* Coriander / cilantro
* 600g cooked minute rice (Ben’s Original Mexican Style Rice - can be substituted for white rice or your choice of rice)
* 6 tortillas (Simsons Pantry Large Low Carb Protein Tortillas)
* 1 tbsp nacho cheese sauce per burrito (Old El Paso Creamy Taco Dip)
* 20g light grated mozzarella per burrito

## Instructions

1. Heat a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add the beef and onion, breaking the beef into small pieces. Cook until the onion softens and the beef is browned; check the beef reaches 160°F (71°C).
2. Stir in the garlic and taco seasoning, then add the tomatoes and chicken broth. Simmer uncovered, stirring, until the mixture is thick rather than soupy.
3. Stir in the cooked rice and heat through. Remove from the heat and fold in the cheddar and cilantro. Let the filling cool slightly so it is easier to handle.
4. Divide the filling between 6 tortillas. Add 1 tablespoon queso to each, fold in the sides and roll tightly.
5. Heat a clean nonstick skillet over medium heat. Sprinkle 20g mozzarella in a strip sized to fit one burrito. When it melts, place a burrito seam-side down on it and press gently. Cook until the cheese forms a golden crust and releases from the pan.
6. Turn to warm and lightly crisp the other side if desired. Repeat with the remaining burritos and mozzarella.
