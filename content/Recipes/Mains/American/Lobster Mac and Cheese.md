## Ingredients

- Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1 1/2 pound lobster
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 2 cups whole milk
- 1 teaspoon dry mustard
- Pinch cayenne pepper
- Pinch freshly grated nutmeg
- 1 pound sharp Cheddar cheese, grated
- 1/2 pound macaroni or elbow pasta, uncooked

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the lobster, cover, and cook 8 to 10 minutes until bright red (an antenna should pull out easily). Set aside in a bowl to cool.
2. Heat oven to 375°F with a rack in the upper third. Butter a 9x13-inch pan with 1 tablespoon butter.
3. Blend the cottage cheese, milk, mustard, cayenne and nutmeg with a little salt and pepper. Stir together with the Cheddar and uncooked pasta in a large bowl, pour into the pan, cover tightly with foil, and bake 30 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, crack the lobster over its bowl, saving the liquid, and roughly chop the meat.
5. Uncover, stir in the lobster and up to 2 tablespoons of its juices, dot with the remaining butter, and bake uncovered 30 minutes more until browned. Rest 15 to 20 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- The pasta goes in uncooked and cooks in the oven.
