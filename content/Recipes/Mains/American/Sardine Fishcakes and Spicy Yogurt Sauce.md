## Ingredients

### Sardine Fish Cakes
* 2 tins sardines (~4oz) packed in chili + oil, oil reserved (King Oscar Spanish Style Sardines recommended).
* 1/2 cup plain yogurt (whole milk).
* 1/2 cup Panko breadcrumbs, plus a little extra if needed to bind the cakes.
* 2 twigs fresh thyme.
* 2 teaspoons Old Bay seasoning.
* 1 egg.

### Spicy Yogurt Sauce
* 1/2 cup plain yogurt (whole milk).
* 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard.
* 1 tablespoon capers.
* 1 tablespoon capers brine (from capers jar).
* 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce.
* 1/8 teaspoon cayenne powder, to taste (optional).
* Pinch salt, to taste.

## Instructions

1. Stir together the sauce yogurt, Dijon, chopped capers, caper brine, Worcestershire sauce and optional cayenne. Taste before adding the pinch of salt. Refrigerate while you make the cakes.
2. Drain the sardines well, reserving their oil for frying. Flake the fish into a bowl. Strip the thyme leaves from the stems and chop them; discard the stems.
3. Beat the egg lightly, then fold it into the sardines with the thyme, Old Bay, 1/2 cup yogurt and 1/2 cup panko. Mix just until combined, leaving some texture. Refrigerate for 20 to 30 minutes so the crumbs hydrate and the mixture firms up.
4. Check the consistency: a small portion should hold together when pressed into a patty. If it is still too wet, add extra panko 1 tablespoon at a time and let it absorb for 5 minutes. Shape into 6 to 8 small cakes, about 1/2 inch thick. Handle gently.
5. Heat a thin coating of the reserved sardine oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Cook one small test cake first, about 3 to 4 minutes per side, until browned and set through the center. Lower the heat if it browns before the center sets. If it breaks apart, add a little more panko to the remaining mixture and chill again.
6. Cook the remaining cakes in batches without crowding, adding more reserved oil as needed. Let a crust form before turning with a thin spatula. The cakes should be firm through the center with no wet egg mixture remaining; timing depends on thickness and heat.
7. Serve hot with the chilled spicy yogurt sauce. For a main course, serve with a salad or vegetables.
