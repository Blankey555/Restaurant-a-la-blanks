## Ingredients

### For the Eggs

- 6 large eggs
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 1/2 tablespoons curry powder
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon toasted and ground cumin seeds
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

### For the Potato Chip Dukkah

- 1/2 cup blanched almonds
- 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds
- 2 tablespoons sesame seeds
- 2 cups canola oil
- 1/2 cup curry leaves

### For Serving

- 4 bagels, toasted
- Kimchi
- Salt and vinegar chips
- Rocket fuel sauce or A1 sauce
- Fresh cilantro leaves

## Instructions

1. Boil the eggs 10 minutes, cool under cold running water, and peel. Break them up into chunks in a bowl, stir in the mayonnaise, curry powder, lemon juice, and cumin, and season.
2. Toast the almonds, pumpkin seeds, and sesame seeds in a skillet over medium heat until golden, 4 to 5 minutes. Cool, then pulse in a food processor to coarse crumbs.
3. Heat the oil in a small skillet over medium-high and fry the curry leaves until crisp, about 30 seconds. Drain through a sieve, cool, and mix into the ground seeds.
4. Top each bagel with egg salad, kimchi, sauce, dukkah, potato chips, and cilantro.

## Notes

- Action Bronson's "Sunday Schmooze" breakfast bagel from Munchies.
- The source method also mentions coriander seeds and mixing potato chips into the dukkah, though neither appears in its ingredient list as quantities.
