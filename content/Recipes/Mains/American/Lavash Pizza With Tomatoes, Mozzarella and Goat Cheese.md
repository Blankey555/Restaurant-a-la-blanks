## Ingredients

- 1 9-by-12-inch piece of lavash
- 1/2 cup, tightly packed, grated or shredded fresh mozzarella (2 ounces)
- 2 plum tomatoes, sliced
- 1/4 red onion, sliced (optional)
- 1/2 cup crumbled goat cheese (2 ounces)
- 1 to 2 teaspoons fresh thyme
- Salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 375°F, ideally with a pizza stone inside.
2. Set the lavash on a baking sheet. Scatter the mozzarella over it, then the tomatoes and onion. Tuck the goat cheese around the tomatoes. Season with thyme, salt, and pepper and drizzle with the oil.
3. Bake 15 to 20 minutes, until the lavash is crisp and dark at the edges. Serve hot or slightly cooled, cut with a pizza wheel or scissors.

## Notes

- The source says to assemble just before baking.
