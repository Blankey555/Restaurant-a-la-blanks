## Ingredients

- 4 (6- to 8-ounce) salmon fillets
- Salt and pepper
- Olive oil

## Instructions

1. Build a medium-high fire on a clean gas or charcoal grill.
2. Season the salmon, oil the grate lightly, and lay the fillets skin side up over the fire. Cook 2 to 3 minutes until lightly browned.
3. Carefully flip and cook 3 to 4 minutes more for medium rare, or a little longer if you prefer; do not overcook.

## Notes

- The source warns that salmon will stick to a grate that is not clean.
