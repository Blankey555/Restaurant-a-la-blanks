## Ingredients

- 8 (6-ounce) English-cut short ribs
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons grapeseed oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 carrot, trimmed and chopped
- 2 celery ribs, trimmed and chopped
- 1 lemongrass stalk, trimmed, smashed and minced
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 (1-inch) piece ginger, peeled and minced
- 1/2 cup dry red wine
- 3 cups beef or chicken broth
- 1/2 cup plum sauce
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 sprigs thyme
- 2 sprigs flat-leaf parsley
- 2 bay leaves
- Freshly grated horseradish, for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Pat the ribs dry and season all over with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high until shimmering. Brown the ribs on all sides, about 2 minutes per side, and set aside.
3. Add onion, carrot, celery, lemongrass, garlic and ginger with a pinch of salt; cook, stirring, until the onion softens, about 5 minutes. Add the wine and scrape up the browned bits. Add broth, plum sauce, soy sauce, thyme, parsley and bay leaves and bring to a simmer.
4. Return the ribs and their juices, cover, and braise in the oven until fork-tender, about 2 hours.
5. Lift out the ribs. Strain the liquid and remove the fat with a fat separator or ladle.
6. Discard the bay leaves and thyme stems. Purée the vegetables in a food processor, then pulse in 1 1/2 cups of the defatted liquid.
7. Return the sauce to the pot, season, and turn the ribs in it. Hold until serving.
8. Reheat the ribs in the sauce and serve in shallow bowls with a spoonful of sauce, passing the rest and the horseradish at the table.
