## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound butter
- 1 pound onions (about 3 medium), cut into 1/4-inch dice
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 4 (28-ounce) cans diced tomatoes
- 1 1/4 cups chicken broth
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon celery salt
- 3/4 teaspoon pepper
- 3/4 cup half-and-half
- 2 tablespoons honey

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a large pot over medium-low. Cook the onions gently, stirring now and then, until soft and translucent, about 20 minutes.
2. Stir in the flour and cook about 3 minutes until slightly thick and pale gold; do not let it brown.
3. Add the tomatoes with their juice, broth, sugar, salt, celery salt and pepper. Bring to a bubble over medium, then simmer on low 30 minutes, scraping the bottom often.
4. Stir in the half-and-half and honey. Off heat, puree with a stick blender (or cool slightly and blend in batches). Rewarm over medium and serve hot.
