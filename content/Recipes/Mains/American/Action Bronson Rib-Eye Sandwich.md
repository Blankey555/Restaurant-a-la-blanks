## Ingredients

- 1 boneless ribeye, at room temperature
- kosher salt, to taste
- good olive oil, to taste
- seeded semolina bread
- madre de sagrantino (grape jelly mixed with red wine)
- calabrian chile paste
- sea salt, to taste
- fresh basil
- ricotta salata

## Instructions

1. Heat a large cast-iron skillet over medium-high. Salt the ribeye well on both sides and drizzle with olive oil.
2. Sear, flipping once and keeping the steak flat for an even crust, until cooked to your liking. Rest on a board 10 minutes, then slice.
3. Split the semolina roll lengthwise. Spread the madre de sagrantino on the bottom half and the chile paste on the top half.
4. Layer on the steak, drizzle with olive oil, season with sea salt, and add basil and shaved ricotta salata. Finish with a little more olive oil.
