## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- 2 tablespoons fresh lime juice
- 1 tablespoon seasoned rice vinegar
- Salt to taste
- 1 garlic clove, minced or pureed
- 2 teaspoons minced fresh ginger
- 1/4 cup plus 2 tablespoons grapeseed oil or sunflower oil
- 1 tablespoon dark sesame oil
- 1 to 2 serrano or Thai green chiles, minced (to taste), or 1/8 to 1/4 teaspoon cayenne
- 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts (most weigh 8 to 10 ounces)
- Salt and freshly ground pepper
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour, or a gluten-free flour such as rice flour or corn flour
- 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro
- 4 cups wild arugula or salad mix, rinsed and spun dry

## Instructions

1. Whisk the soy sauce, sugar, 1 tablespoon lime juice, vinegar, salt, garlic, ginger, 1/4 cup grapeseed or sunflower oil and the sesame oil. Pour half into a bowl big enough for the chicken; add the remaining tablespoon lime juice and the chiles or cayenne.
2. Halve each breast (cut in 3 if 12 ounces or more), add to the marinade, stir, and refrigerate 15 to 30 minutes.
3. Lift out the chicken, pat dry, and discard the marinade. Lay a piece between lightly oiled sheets of plastic wrap and pound from the center out to about 1/4 inch thick, gently to avoid tearing. Repeat.
4. Season one side with salt and pepper, dredge lightly in flour, and tap off the excess.
5. Turn the oven on low. Heat a wide heavy skillet over high, add the remaining 2 tablespoons oil, and cook the chicken without crowding about 1 1/2 minutes per side, until spotted brown. Move to a platter, sprinkle with cilantro, and keep warm in the oven.
6. Toss the greens with the reserved dressing and serve with the chicken.
