## Ingredients

### The Turkey & Brine
* 1 whole large turkey (approx. 5kg, preferably Bronze or heritage breed)
* 50g fine sea salt
* 15g brown sugar
* Olive oil
* 100 to 150ml water (for roasting tray)

### Cavity Aromatics
* Onions, lemons, garlic and herbs (the saved recipe does not specify quantities; use loosely rather than packing the cavity)

### Compound Butter
* 500g unsalted butter (softened)
* 1 tbsp each: fresh rosemary, thyme, sage, and parsley (chopped)
* 2 lemons (zested)
* 3 cloves garlic (minced)
* 1 tsp sea salt and 1 tsp black pepper

### Maple Glaze
* 3 tbsp maple syrup
* 3 tbsp sherry vinegar
* 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
* 1 tsp dark soy sauce
* 3 strips orange peel
* 2 sprigs each: fresh rosemary and thyme
* 3 cloves garlic (gently smashed)
* 4 tbsp unsalted butter

### Pan Gravy
* 3 tbsp reserved turkey fat
* 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
* 150ml dry white wine
* 500ml high-quality chicken stock
* Reserved roasting juices
* 1 fresh sprig rosemary

## Instructions

### Day 1: The Dry Brine
1. Pat the turkey completely dry with paper towels. 
2. Mix the listed 50g salt and 15g brown sugar.
3. Place the turkey on a wire rack over a tray to allow air circulation. 
4. Sprinkle the brine liberally over the skin and inside the cavity.
5. Refrigerate uncovered for 24 hours.

### Day 2: Herb Butter Infusion
1. Blot away any excess surface moisture from the bird.
2. Mix the compound butter ingredients until fragrant.
3. Gently separate the skin from the breast meat using a silicone spatula or hands. 
4. Stuff a generous amount of butter under the skin and push it evenly across the breasts.
5. Smear the remaining butter over the wings, legs, and outer skin.
6. Place aromatics (onions, lemons, garlic, herbs) into the cavity.
7. Refrigerate overnight to infuse.

### Day 3: Roasting and Glazing
1. Remove the turkey from the fridge 2 hours before cooking; do not wait for the center to reach room temperature.
2. **Make the Glaze:** Simmer all glaze ingredients in a saucepan for 7 minutes until reduced, glossy, and thick enough to coat a spoon. Strain and set aside.
3. **Initial Roast:** Preheat oven to 185°C. Brush the bird with olive oil and pour 100-150ml water into the tray. Roast on the middle shelf for 25 minutes.
4. **Glazing Cycle:** Remove the bird, apply the first layer of glaze, and rotate the tray. Repeat the glazing and rotating process every 25 minutes until the turkey reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest breast and innermost thigh and wing, avoiding bone. See the review note for the original lower-temperature target.
5. **Resting:** Drain juices from the cavity into the tray. Let the turkey rest uncovered for at least 1 to 1.5 hours.

### The Pan Gravy
1. Scrape the baking tray to release the fond and pour all liquids into a jug. 
2. Skim the fat from the top. 
3. In a saucepan over medium-high heat, whisk 3 tbsp of the fat with 2 tbsp flour for 1-2 minutes.
4. Whisk in the white wine until a thick paste forms, then stream in the chicken stock and roasting juices.
5. Simmer until thickened to your preference.
6. Turn off the heat and steep a sprig of rosemary in the gravy for 15 minutes before serving.

### Review notes

- Pre-roasting counter time is capped at 2 hours, including preparation; if the room is above 90°F (32°C), cap it at 1 hour. This is a maximum, not a requirement to warm the bird fully.
- The saved 150 to 155°F breast target is retained as historical recipe information, not a validated safety protocol. No hold-time procedure was supplied. For a straightforward consumer endpoint, cook to 165°F (74°C), checking the thickest breast and innermost thigh and wing without touching bone.
- Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours after cooking; the resting and serving time count toward that limit.
- Guidance: https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/safe-food-handling
