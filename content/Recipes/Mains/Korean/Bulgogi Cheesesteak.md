## Ingredients

### For the sandwich

- 1 pound thinly cut steak (flank steak or tenderloin)
- 1/4 pound sliced provolone cheese (or American if you prefer)
- 2 big hero rolls
- 1 scallion, chopped

### For the marinade

- 1 bulb garlic, slivered
- 1 green onion, chopped
- 1 teaspoon minced ginger
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons toasted sesame oil
- handful toasted sesame seeds
- freshly ground black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Toss the meat with the marinade ingredients in a large bowl and let sit at least 30 minutes.
2. Heat a charcoal grill, or a heavy pan with a little oil over high heat.
3. Cook the meat until browned and done to your liking. In a pan, pour off the juices a few times so it sears rather than stews, and melt the cheese right onto the meat. If grilling, add the cheese when assembling.
4. Pile the hot bulgogi into the rolls and sprinkle with the chopped scallion.
