## Ingredients

- 6 boneless, skinless chicken thighs (about 900g / 2 lb)
- 3 tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste)
- 1 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes), optional
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 to 2 tbsp light brown sugar
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger paste (or freshly grated ginger)
- 1 tbsp Korean corn syrup (mulyeot) — see notes for substitutes
- 1 tbsp sweet rice wine (mirim), optional
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 5 to 6 perilla leaves (kkaennip), thinly sliced, to garnish (optional)
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds, to garnish

---

## Instructions

**Prepare the chicken**

1. Place a sheet of plastic wrap over the chicken thighs and pound gently with a meat mallet to even out the thickness.

**Marinate**

2. In a large bowl, combine gochujang, gochugaru (if using), soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, ginger, corn syrup, rice wine, sesame oil, and black pepper.
3. Add the chicken and toss to coat evenly. Marinate at room temperature for 10 to 30 minutes, or refrigerate overnight for deeper flavor.

**Broil**

4. Preheat oven broiler and position the rack 5 to 6 inches below the heat source.
5. Lightly grease a wire cooling rack with oil spray and set it over a foil-lined sheet pan.
6. Arrange chicken on the rack in a single layer without overlapping.
7. Broil for 4 to 5 minutes until lightly charred. Flip and broil another 4 to 5 minutes until fully cooked. Watch carefully to prevent burning.

**Serve**

8. Slice into bite-sized pieces. Garnish with perilla leaves and sesame seeds. Serve hot with steamed rice.

---

## Alternative Cooking Methods

**Grill:** Cook over medium-high heat on a mesh grill mat for the most authentic Korean BBQ flavor. Turn once until lightly caramelized and cooked through.

**Stovetop:** Heat a lightly oiled skillet over medium-high heat. Cook 3 to 4 minutes per side until cooked through and slightly caramelized. Do not overcrowd the pan.

**Wings:** Use the same marinade. Bake first, then broil to finish. Cooking time will be longer.

---

## Storage
- **Fridge:** Up to 3 days in an airtight container.
- **Reheat:** Over medium-high heat, stirring frequently, until heated through. Let sit at room temperature for 10 to 15 minutes before reheating.

---

## Notes
- Chicken thighs are strongly recommended over breast. They stay juicy and absorb the gochujang marinade far better.
- Korean corn syrup (mulyeot) adds gloss and helps the marinade caramelize. Substitute with honey or rice syrup if unavailable.
- Gochugaru adds extra heat and color but is optional. Skip it for a milder dish.
- Perilla leaf garnish is optional but adds a distinctly Korean herbal note that pairs well with the gochujang.
- The broiling method mimics the char of grilling without extra oil and produces the best indoor result.
