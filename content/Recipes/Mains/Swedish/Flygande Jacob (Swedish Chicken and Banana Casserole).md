## Ingredients

- 1 rotisserie chicken
- One big pinch of Italian seasoning
- 4 bananas
- 1 cup Heinz Chili Sauce
- 3 cups heavy whipping cream
- 1/4 lb bacon
- 1/2 cup cocktail peanuts

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 450°F. Pull the chicken meat off the bones, discarding skin and bones, and spread it in a casserole dish. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning.
2. Quarter the bananas lengthwise and layer them over the chicken.
3. Lightly whip the cream and chili sauce together in a bowl, pour over the chicken and bananas, and bake 20 minutes.
4. Meanwhile, chop the bacon and fry in a separate pan until crisp.
5. Top the baked casserole with the bacon and peanuts. Serve over rice with a side salad.

## Notes

- From The A.V. Club's Acquired Tastes column; recovered from a 2017 Internet Archive snapshot. The dish was created by Ove Jacobsson and first published in the Swedish magazine Allt Om Mat in 1976.
