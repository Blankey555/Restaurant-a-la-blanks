## Ingredients

### Basic Tomato Sauce

- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 3 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- 1/2 medium carrot, finely shredded
- 2 (28-ounce) cans whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand
- Kosher salt, to taste

### Ribollita

- 8 ounces dried white cannellini or brown borlotti beans, soaked overnight
- 1/4 cup extra virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
- 1 medium red onion, chopped into 1/2-inch dice
- 2 cloves garlic, peeled and thinly sliced
- 1 medium carrot, peeled and chopped into 1/4-inch half moons
- 2 ribs celery, chopped into 1/4-inch pieces
- 2 waxy potatoes, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
- 4 cups reserved bean liquid
- 1 cup basic tomato sauce
- 1 bunch Swiss chard, chopped into 1/2-inch ribbons
- 1 bunch kale, chopped into 1/2-inch ribbons
- 1 bunch black cabbage (cavolo nero), chopped into 1/2-inch ribbons
- 4 slices country bread
- Hot pepper flakes, for garnish
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan

## Instructions

1. Tomato sauce: heat the oil in a 3-quart saucepan over medium. Cook the garlic and onion until soft and light golden, 8 to 10 minutes. Add thyme and carrot and cook 5 minutes more. Add tomatoes, bring to a boil, then simmer 30 minutes until thick as hot cereal. Salt to taste.
2. Cover the beans with water, bring to a boil, and simmer until soft, 50 to 55 minutes. Drain, keeping 4 cups of the liquid and the beans separately.
3. In an 8-quart pot, heat the oil over medium and soften the onion and garlic, 5 minutes. Add carrot, celery, potatoes, 2 cups of the beans, the 4 cups bean liquid, and 1 cup tomato sauce; bring to a boil.
4. Reduce to a simmer, add the chard, kale, and cavolo nero, and cook until everything is soft, about 1 1/2 hours.
5. Add the remaining beans and simmer 20 seconds. Season with salt and pepper, cool, and refrigerate overnight.
6. Next day, bring to a boil and simmer until thick.
7. Oil the bread and grill over high heat until charred on both sides; rub with garlic. Put a toast in each shallow bowl, grate parmesan over, and ladle the soup on top. Finish with olive oil and pepper flakes, and pass more parmesan.

## Notes

- Mario Batali's version from the Munchies show MOLTISSIMO.
- The tomato sauce keeps 1 week refrigerated or up to 6 months frozen (per the source). Leftover sauce makes more than the 1 cup used here.
