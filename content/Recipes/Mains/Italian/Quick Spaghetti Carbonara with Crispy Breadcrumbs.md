## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 ounces diced lardo
- 3 ounces diced pancetta
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/3 cup fresh breadcrumbs
- 1/3 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 10 egg yolks
- 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1 pound dry spaghetti

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high and cook the lardo and pancetta until crisp, 4 to 5 minutes. Lift onto paper towels.
2. Wipe out the skillet, melt the butter until foamy, and toast the breadcrumbs until golden, about 2 minutes. Cool slightly and mix in half the parmesan.
3. Beat the yolks with the remaining parmesan, the garlic, and black pepper in a large bowl.
4. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until al dente, about 9 minutes. Reserve 1 cup water and drain.
5. Whisk about half the pasta water into the yolks, add the pasta, and toss, adding more water until the sauce is smooth and silky. Fold in the lardo and pancetta, transfer to a platter, and top with the breadcrumbs.

## Notes

- No cream, in keeping with the companion Munchies article saved alongside it.
