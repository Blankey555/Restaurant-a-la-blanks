## Ingredients

- 1 12-inch round of pizza dough, stretched (see [[Pizza Dough (Roberta's Style)]])
- 4 teaspoons heavy cream
- 2 ounces fresh mozzarella
- 1 1/2 ounces fresh taleggio
- Ground black pepper
- 1 ounce Parmesan, finely grated

## Instructions

1. Set a pizza stone or tiles on the middle rack, turn the oven to its highest setting, and heat at least 1 hour.
2. Drizzle the cream over the stretched dough. Tear the mozzarella and taleggio into large pieces and scatter them on top. Grind on plenty of black pepper, about 8 to 10 grinds.
3. Slide the pie onto the hot stone with a peel and bake 4 to 8 minutes, until the crust is golden and the cheese bubbles.
4. Finish with the Parmesan and serve right away.
