## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons saffron threads
- 3 cups chicken or vegetable stock
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 0.71 ounces (20 grams) shallot, minced
- 1 cup short grain rice, such as arborio, carnaroli or sushi rice
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 0.88 ounces (25 grams) Parmigiano Reggiano, grated

## Instructions

1. Combine the saffron and stock in a pot and keep at a gentle simmer over medium-low heat.
2. In another pot, melt the butter over medium-high heat and fry the shallot until fragrant and just beginning to color, without browning.
3. Add the rice and fry until it has absorbed the fat.
4. Add the wine and stir constantly until no liquid remains.
5. Add the hot stock one ladleful at a time, stirring until each addition is absorbed, until the rice is creamy and as tender as you like, about 16 to 20 minutes. You may not need all the stock.
6. Stir in the cheese.

## Notes

- By Marc Matsumoto for the PBS Food Fresh Tastes blog.
- The source notes the traditional version uses lard and beef stock; he uses chicken or vegetable stock and often serves it with osso buco.
