## Ingredients

- 1 lb (400 g) coarsely ground beef  
- 6 oz (150 g) fresh pork pancetta, sliced  
- ½ onion, peeled (about 2 oz / 60 g)  
- 1 medium carrot, peeled (about 2 oz / 60 g)  
- 1 celery stalk, trimmed (about 2 oz / 60 g)  
- ½ cup (1 glass) red or white wine  
- 7 oz (200 g) strained tomatoes  
- 1 tbsp double‑concentrated tomato paste  
- ½ cup (1 glass) whole milk (optional)  
- Light meat or vegetable broth (or stock cubes), as needed  
- 3 tbsp extra‑virgin olive oil  
- Salt and pepper, to taste  

## Instructions

1. In a heavy non‑stick 10‑inch (24‑26 cm) casserole (aluminium, enamelled cast‑iron Dutch oven, or terracotta saucepan), melt the pancetta with the olive oil.  
2. Finely chop the onion, celery, and carrot (do not use a food processor). Add the vegetables to the oil and pancetta and cook over low heat, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon, until softened but not browned.  
3. Raise the heat to medium, add the ground beef, break it up, and cook for about ten minutes, stirring, until it sizzles and browns.  
4. Add the wine and cook over medium heat until it has completely evaporated.  
5. Stir in the tomato paste and purée, then mix well. Add a cup of boiling stock (or water) and bring to a simmer. Cover and simmer for about 2 hours (or 3 hours depending on preference and type of meat), adding hot broth or water as needed.  
6. Halfway through the cooking time, add the milk (if using) and allow it to evaporate completely.  
7. Season with salt and pepper before serving.  

## Notes

- Traditional Bolognese uses hanger or skirt steak; modern versions favor cuts rich in collagen such as shoulder, chuck, brisket, plate, or flank.  
- Permitted variants: mixed beef and pork (≈ 60 % beef), meat minced with a knife, cured pancetta instead of fresh, a pinch of nutmeg.  
- Unacceptable variants: veal, smoked pancetta or bacon, only pork, garlic or other herbs/spices, brandy instead of wine, flour as a thickener.  
- Enrichments: chicken livers, hearts and gizzards; peeled and crumbled pork sausage; blanched peas added at the end; rehydrated dried porcini.
