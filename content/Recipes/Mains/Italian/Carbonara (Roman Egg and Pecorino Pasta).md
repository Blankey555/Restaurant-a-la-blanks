## Ingredients

- Salt
- 2 large eggs and 2 large yolks, room temperature
- 1 ounce (about 1/3 packed cup) grated pecorino Romano, plus more for serving
- 1 ounce (about 1/3 packed cup) grated Parmesan
- Coarsely ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 3 1/2 ounces slab guanciale, pancetta or bacon, sliced about 1/4 inch thick by 1 1/3 inch square
- 12 ounces spaghetti (about 3/4 box)

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water (no more than 1 tablespoon salt) to a boil. Fill a large serving bowl with hot water to warm it.
2. Whisk the eggs, yolks, pecorino and Parmesan with a pinch of salt and plenty of pepper.
3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium, add the pork and cook until the fat renders and the edges are nearly crisp but not hard. Take off the heat.
4. Boil the pasta until slightly firmer than al dente. Rewarm the pork if needed. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, drain, and add the pasta to the skillet over low heat; stir about a minute.
5. Empty and dry the serving bowl, add the hot pasta, and stir in the egg and cheese mixture, adding pasta water as needed for creaminess. Serve at once with more pecorino and pepper.
