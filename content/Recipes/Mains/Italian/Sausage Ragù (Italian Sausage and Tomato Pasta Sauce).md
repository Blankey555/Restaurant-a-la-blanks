## Ingredients

- 1 pound sweet Italian sausage or bulk sausage
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 onion, minced
- 1 carrot, minced
- 1 celery stalk, minced
- 1/4 cup minced flat-leaf parsley, plus extra for garnish
- 1 (28-ounce) can whole tomatoes, preferably San Marzano, with its juice
- 1 large sprig fresh thyme
- 1 large sprig fresh rosemary
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- Salt
- Ground black pepper
- 1 pound tubular dried pasta such as mezzi rigatoni, paccheri or penne
- Freshly grated Parmesan cheese, for garnish, optional

## Instructions

1. Slit the sausage casings and crumble the meat into a wide heavy skillet or Dutch oven over medium-low heat. Add oil a tablespoon at a time if needed so the meat fries gently instead of steaming. Cook, breaking it up, until opaque but not browned, about 5 minutes.
2. Add onion, carrot, celery and parsley, plus more oil if dry. Cook over very low heat, stirring often, until the vegetables melt and caramelize and the meat is toasty brown, up to 40 minutes. Do not rush this.
3. Add the tomatoes and juice, crushing them by hand or with a spoon. Bring to a simmer, add thyme and rosemary, and simmer uncovered until thick and nearly dry, 20 to 25 minutes.
4. Mix the tomato paste into 1 cup hot water, add to the pan, and cook over very low heat until velvety, dark red and glistening, about 10 minutes. Remove the herb sprigs, season with pepper and taste.
5. Meanwhile, boil the pasta in salted water until just tender. Reserve 2 cups of the water, drain, and return the pasta to the pot over low heat. Add a ladle of ragù and a splash of water, stir and cook 1 minute; repeat until the pasta is done and seasoned to taste.
6. Warm a serving bowl with hot pasta water, empty it, add the pasta, top with the rest of the ragù and parsley, and serve at once with cheese on the side.
