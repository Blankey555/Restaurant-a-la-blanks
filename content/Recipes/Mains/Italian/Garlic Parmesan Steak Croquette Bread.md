## Ingredients

- 250 g steak, cut into very small cubes (chicken or ground meat also work)
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 35 ml heavy cream
- 30 g grated parmesan
- 180 g shredded cheese (mozzarella and emmentaler, or any mix)
- Olive oil, for frying the filling
- 1 teaspoon seasoning paste (optional)
- Salt, black pepper, paprika, ground cumin, curry powder, garlic powder
- 300 g bread flour or wheat flour (type 550)
- 190 ml warm milk
- 20 g sugar
- 6 g dry yeast
- 6 g salt
- 35 g softened butter
- Milk, for brushing
- 1 1/2 cups panko breadcrumbs
- Oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. **Make the dough.** Combine the flour, warm milk, sugar, yeast, and salt in a bowl or stand mixer. Knead until a rough dough forms, then add the softened butter and continue kneading about 10 minutes, until smooth and elastic. Cover and let rest about 1 hour, until slightly risen.
2. **Prepare the steak filling.** Heat a little olive oil in a pan and sauté the onion until soft. Add the steak and cook until lightly browned. Add the garlic, spices, cream, parmesan, and optional seasoning paste, and stir until creamy. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
3. **Divide and shape.** Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces (about 66 g each) and shape into balls. Let rest 10 minutes. Flatten each piece, add some shredded cheese, a portion of the steak filling, and more cheese on top. Seal the dough tightly so nothing leaks during frying. Place on a lightly floured tray and let rest 30 minutes.
4. **Coat and fry.** Brush each filled dough ball lightly with milk, then coat evenly in panko. Fry in hot oil until golden brown and crispy on all sides. Drain briefly before serving.

## Notes

- The source gives no times. Budget about 1 hour 40 minutes of resting (1 hour rise, 10 minutes after dividing, 30 minutes after filling) on top of the active work.
- Not really Italian beyond the parmesan; it is a German-style yeast dough (type 550 flour, emmentaler) with a panko crust. Filed here as the closest existing folder.
