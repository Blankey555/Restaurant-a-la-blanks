## Ingredients

### Pork
- 1 bone-in pork butt (6 to 8 lb)

### Marinade
- 1 cup sofrito (homemade or store-bought)
- ½ cup fresh orange juice
- ¼ cup fresh lime juice
- ¼ cup fresh lemon juice
- ⅓ cup olive oil
- 1 tbsp dried oregano
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp smoked paprika

### Garlic Studs
- 8 to 10 cloves garlic, halved lengthwise
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp black pepper

---

## Instructions

### Day One: Marinate

1. Mix salt, cumin, and black pepper together in a small bowl. Toss the halved garlic cloves in the mixture to coat.
2. Using a sharp paring knife, cut deep slits all over the pork butt, 1 to 2 inches deep. Space them evenly across the top, sides, and bottom of the roast. Push a seasoned garlic half into each slit as far as it will go.
3. Combine all marinade ingredients in a bowl and whisk to combine.
4. Place the pork in a large zip-lock bag or deep baking dish. Pour the marinade over and massage it into every surface, working it into the garlic slits as much as possible.
5. Cover tightly and refrigerate overnight, or for a minimum of 8 hours. Turn the pork once or twice if possible.

### Day Two: Roast

6. Remove the pork from the refrigerator at least 1 hour before roasting to take the chill off.
7. Preheat oven to 300°F.
8. Place the pork fat side up in a deep roasting pan or Dutch oven. Pour any remaining marinade over the top.
9. Cover tightly with a lid or two layers of foil.
10. Roast covered for 5 hours, undisturbed.
11. Uncover and increase oven temperature to 375°F. Roast for a further 1 to 1½ hours until the exterior is deeply caramelized and the internal temperature reaches 195 to 205°F.
12. Rest uncovered for at least 20 to 30 minutes before pulling or slicing.

---

## To Serve
- Pull the pork into large chunks and spoon the pan juices over the top.
- Serve with white rice, black beans, fried plantains, and sliced avocado.
- The pan drippings make an excellent sauce on their own or as a base for congri.

---

## Notes
- Bone-in pork butt is strongly preferred. The bone bastes the meat from the inside during the long roast and adds significant flavor to the drippings.
- The citrus balance of orange, lime, and lemon is the defining characteristic. Do not use bottled juice.
- Sofrito varies significantly by brand and recipe. A homemade sofrito of onion, garlic, peppers, cilantro, and tomato gives the best result.
- Do not rush the rest. The collagen continues to relax as the roast sits and the meat becomes easier to pull and more flavorful.
- Leftovers make excellent Cuban sandwiches, tacos, or hash the next day.
- The uncovered high-heat finish is essential for the crust. Check every 15 minutes and tent loosely with foil if it darkens too quickly.
