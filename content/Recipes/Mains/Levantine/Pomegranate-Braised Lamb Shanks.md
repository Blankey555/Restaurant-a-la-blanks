## Ingredients

- 2 lamb shanks (about 450 to 500g each)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 350ml pomegranate juice
- 300ml beef or chicken stock
- 3 tablespoons pomegranate molasses
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1 bay leaf
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 80g pomegranate seeds
- 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 160°C. Pat the lamb shanks dry and season with the salt and pepper.
2. Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven and sear the lamb for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until deeply browned. Remove.
3. Cook the onion for 5 minutes, until softened. Add the garlic, tomato paste, cumin, cinnamon, and coriander; cook for 1 minute.
4. Pour in the pomegranate juice and stock, scraping up the browned bits. Stir in the pomegranate molasses, honey, bay leaf, and thyme.
5. Return the lamb to the pot; the liquid should reach about halfway up the shanks. Cover tightly.
6. Braise in the oven for 2.5 to 3 hours, turning once, until the lamb is very tender and pulls easily from the bone.
7. Remove the lamb. Simmer the sauce uncovered for 10 to 15 minutes, until rich and slightly thickened. Adjust salt if needed.
8. Spoon the sauce over the lamb and finish with the pomegranate seeds and parsley.
