## Ingredients

### Shawarma Marinade
- 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Salt and pepper
- 1 1/2 cups plain yogurt
- 1/4 cup minced yellow onion
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for the grill
- 2 garlic cloves, minced

### Chicken and Serving
- 3 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs (or a combination)
- 1/2 lemon, for serving
- Fresh parsley or mint leaves (optional), for serving
- 1 recipe [[Tahini-Yogurt Sauce]]

## Instructions

1. Make the marinade: In a large bowl, combine the paprika, cumin, coriander, ginger, turmeric, allspice and cinnamon with 1 1/2 teaspoons salt and 1/2 teaspoon pepper. Add the yogurt, onion, olive oil, and garlic; mix well, then set aside.
2. Pat the chicken pieces dry. If using breasts, cut them in half crosswise. Working in batches, cover the chicken with parchment and pound to an even thickness between 1/2 and 3/4 inch, taking care not to tear it.
3. Add the chicken to the marinade and toss well. Cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours or up to 24 hours.
4. Meanwhile, make the [[Tahini-Yogurt Sauce]] and refrigerate.
5. Before grilling, let the chicken come to room temperature for 30 minutes. Clean the grates and brush them with oil. Set the grill to medium-high, or heat an oiled grill pan over medium-high.
6. Lift the chicken from the marinade (no need to brush off excess) and grill until cooked through, 6 to 8 minutes per side, covering the grill halfway through if needed to retain heat.
7. Transfer to a plate, sprinkle generously with salt, and squeeze the lemon half over the pieces. Rest at least 10 minutes. Garnish with parsley or mint and serve the sauce on the side.

## Notes

- The chicken can be marinated up to 24 hours ahead.
- Leftover chicken packs well into a wrap with cucumbers, tomatoes, lettuce, and extra tahini-yogurt sauce.
