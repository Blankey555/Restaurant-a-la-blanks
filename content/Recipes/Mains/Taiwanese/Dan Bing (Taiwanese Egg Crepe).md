## Ingredients

### For the crepe

- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 cup water

### For the egg

- 3 eggs
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon salt, to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- Dash of sesame oil
- 2 to 3 scallions, chopped

### For the sauce

- 2 tablespoons soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
- Chili oil and sesame oil, to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon crushed garlic (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mix the flour, cornstarch, and water and let the batter rest 10 minutes.
2. For each crepe, beat 1 egg with about 1 tablespoon scallions, a dash of sesame oil, and a pinch each of salt and white pepper.
3. Heat a lightly oiled nonstick pan over medium. Add about 1/2 cup batter, swirling to coat, and cook 3 to 4 minutes until the top sets.
4. Flip the crepe out onto a plate and slide it back in cooked side up. Pour the egg over and spread it to stay mostly on the crepe.
5. Cook until the egg is almost set, flip for about 10 seconds, then slide onto a cutting board. Roll up with the egg inside and slice.
6. Whisk the sauce ingredients together and serve alongside.
