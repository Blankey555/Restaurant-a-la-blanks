## Ingredients

- 1 slice bread
- 1 teaspoon butter

## Instructions

1. Toast the bread for 3 minutes until golden.
2. Spread the butter on the toasted bread.
3. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- Ideal for a quick breakfast.
