## Ingredients

- 6-8 g dried chilies (see note)
- 3 slices galangal, chopped
- 1 stalk lemongrass, bottom half only, thinly sliced
- 2 in turmeric root or 1 ½ tsp turmeric powder
- ⅓ cup chopped shallots
- 4 cloves garlic
- Zest from half a kaffir lime (optional)
- 1 tsp fermented shrimp paste (gapi)
- 1 Tbsp fish sauce
- 1 lb ground pork (fattest you can find)
- 5 kaffir lime leaves, stems removed, thinly julienned and minced
- 5-6 stems cilantro, finely chopped
- 1 small green onion, finely chopped

## Instructions

1. Grind dried chilies in a coffee grinder until fine.  
2. Blend or pound all curry‑paste ingredients (galangal, lemongrass, turmeric, lime zest, chilies, garlic, shallots, shrimp paste) until a smooth paste forms; stir fish sauce in at the end.  
3. Using gloves, knead the ground pork with the curry paste until thoroughly combined. Add cilantro, green onion, and kaffir lime leaves and knead just until incorporated.  
4. Cover the mixture and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight.  
5. **For broiled sausages:** Form caseless sausages, place on a foil‑lined tray, and broil on the top rack until browned on one side, about 4-5 minutes. Flip and brown the other side. Transfer to a rack to finish broiling until dry and caramelized. Rest 5 minutes before slicing.  
6. **For sausage patties:** Form patties, preheat a skillet over medium heat, add a thin coat of oil, and pan‑fry until well browned on both sides and fully cooked, a few minutes per side. Rest 5 minutes before serving.  
7. **For stuffed sausages:** Stuff the mixture into casings, rest 2 hours (or overnight), poke holes with a toothpick, then grill or fry until browned and cooked through. Rest 5 minutes before cutting.  
8. Serve as an appetizer or with a main meal, accompanied by fresh vegetables and sticky rice.

## Notes

- The sausage mixture can be prepared up to a day ahead; keep refrigerated.  
- Adjust chili amount to taste; the dish is naturally spicy.  
- If using a broiler, monitor closely to avoid excessive smoke; consider changing foil halfway through.
