## Ingredients

- 350 g tender fish meat, dried thoroughly if thawed from frozen
- 2–3 Tbsp red curry paste
- 1 egg yolk
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ cup long beans
- ⅓ cup Thai basil or holy basil, sliced into ribbons if large
- 5 kaffir lime leaves, finely julienned
- Fish sauce, as needed
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 spur chili (prik chee fah) or another type of big red chili pepper that’s not too spicy
- Thai chilies to taste, optional
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- ⅓ cup white vinegar, cane vinegar or rice vinegar
- 3 Tbsp water
- Cucumber slices for serving
- Optional add‑ins for the dipping sauce: roasted crushed peanuts, sliced shallots

## Instructions

1. Place both chilies, garlic, vinegar, water, sugar and salt in a blender and blend just until there are no more chunks. Use low speed so some chili seeds and pieces remain for visual appeal.  
2. Pour the blended mixture into a small pot, bring to a simmer, and simmer for about 3 to 5 minutes until it reaches a thin‑syrup consistency. The sauce will thicken as it cools; add more water if it becomes too thick. Set aside.  
3. Combine the fish, red curry paste, egg yolk and 1 tsp sugar in a food processor. Process until fine, scraping the sides as needed, then continue processing for a few minutes longer until the fish paste holds its shape when spooned. Taste and adjust salt or spiciness with fish sauce or extra curry paste as desired.  
4. Transfer the fish paste to a mixing bowl and add the chopped long beans, Thai basil and kaffir lime leaves. Stir until well combined.  
5. Heat a skillet over medium heat and add enough oil to coat the bottom. Prepare a bowl of cold water and a paper towel for your hands. Wet your hands and a tablespoon in the cold water, scoop a heaping tablespoon of fish paste, pat it into a patty and place it in the hot skillet. Fry until browned on both sides, about 2 minutes per side, adding more oil as needed. Form the cakes directly in the skillet rather than pre‑forming them all.  
6. To serve, stir cucumber slices, peanuts and shallots into the dipping sauce and serve alongside the fish cakes. Extra cucumber can be served on the side.

## Notes

- If using frozen fish, dry it thoroughly before processing to avoid excess moisture.  
- The recipe can be deep‑fried instead of pan‑fried; follow the same shaping steps and fry in hot oil until golden.  
- Adjust the number of chilies and the amount of Thai chilies to control heat level.
