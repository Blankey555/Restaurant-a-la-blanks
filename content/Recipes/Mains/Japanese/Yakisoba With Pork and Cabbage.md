## Ingredients

- Salt
- 6 ounces dried Chinese egg noodles, or 10 to 12 ounces fresh
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 3 tablespoons peanut oil
- 2 tablespoons minced ginger
- 2 pork chops, thinly sliced
- 1 small head Napa or savoy cabbage, shredded (about 4 cups)
- 2 carrots, shredded
- 2 tablespoons ketchup
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tablespoons mirin, or a bit of sugar
- Few drops Tabasco sauce, or to taste
- 1 bunch scallions, chopped, white parts only

## Instructions

1. Cook the noodles in boiling salted water about 3 minutes, until just done. Drain, rinse under cold water, and toss with the sesame oil.
2. Heat the peanut oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Cook the ginger about 1 minute until fragrant, then add the pork and cook about 5 minutes, until no longer pink and browning at the edges.
3. Add the cabbage and carrots, salt them, and cook until soft, adding splashes of water to prevent sticking.
4. Meanwhile, mix the ketchup, soy sauce, Worcestershire, mirin, and Tabasco.
5. Once the vegetables are soft and the liquid is gone, add the noodles and sauce and toss until heated through. Serve topped with scallions.
