## Ingredients

### Sushi Rice
- 1 1/2 cups sushi rice
- 2 cups water
- A little salt
- 4 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt

### Shrimp
- 400 g shrimp
- 1/2 teaspoon salt, or to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- Olive oil, for the pan

### Bang Bang Sauce
- 110 g mayonnaise
- 20 g sriracha (or sriracha mayo for a milder sauce)
- 45 g sweet chili sauce

### Filling and Shell
- 1/2 cucumber, sliced slightly thick
- 1 medium carrot, peeled and cut into very thin strips
- Optional: mango, avocado, or cream cheese
- 8 nori sheets

### Panko Batter
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup water, plus up to 1/4 cup more as needed
- 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
- Oil, for frying

### Toppings
- Sriracha mayo
- Teriyaki sauce
- White and black sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Rinse the sushi rice several times until the water runs mostly clear. Cook with the water and a little salt according to the package, rest briefly, then spread in a wide bowl.
2. Stir the rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Fold gently into the warm rice with a spatula, without mashing. Cool completely before assembling.
3. Slice the cucumber thick enough to stay crunchy. Peel the carrot and cut into very thin strips.
4. Heat a little olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the shrimp, sprinkle over the salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, and onion powder, and toss until evenly coated and cooked through.
5. Whisk the mayonnaise, sriracha, and sweet chili sauce into the bang bang sauce. Toss the cooked shrimp with one-third of it; reserve the rest for dipping.
6. Mix the flour with 1/2 cup water into a smooth batter, adding more water gradually if needed. Put the panko in a second bowl.
7. Lay a sheet of plastic wrap on the counter and set a nori sheet on it. Press a thin, even layer of rice over the nori, leaving a margin at the edges.
8. Center the cucumber, carrot, bang bang shrimp, and any optional fillings on the rice. Do not overfill.
9. Cover the filling with another thin layer of rice and press gently.
10. Use the plastic wrap to fold the nori up around the burger, flip it, and cap the open side with a second nori sheet.
11. Wet the nori edges lightly so they stick. Wrap tightly in plastic and refrigerate at least 20 to 30 minutes to firm up.
12. Dip the chilled burger into the batter, turning to coat, then press into the panko.
13. Fry in hot oil, turning occasionally, until golden and crisp on all sides.
14. Drizzle with sriracha mayo and teriyaki sauce, sprinkle with sesame seeds, and serve with the reserved bang bang sauce.

## Notes

- Best eaten the same day, chilled. Store assembled burgers tightly wrapped in the fridge; do not microwave them.
- The source gives no times or yield. Eight nori sheets at two per burger works out to about 4 burgers.
