## Ingredients

- 6 ounces thinly sliced beef
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sake
- 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
- 2 tablespoons sake
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons ginger, finely chopped
- 3.2 ounces broccoli (about half a head, cut into small pieces)
- 14 ounces cooked udon noodles

## Instructions

1. Marinate the beef in the soy sauce and 1 teaspoon sake.
2. Whisk the oyster sauce, 2 tablespoons sake, and pepper into a sauce.
3. Heat the oil in a frying pan and sauté the ginger until fragrant.
4. Add the broccoli and toss to coat with oil, then add the beef.
5. Stir-fry until the beef is mostly cooked, then add the udon and sauce.
6. Keep stir-frying until the liquid is gone and the noodles begin to caramelize. Serve right away.

## Notes

- By Marc Matsumoto for the PBS Food Fresh Tastes blog.
- The noodle weight is cooked weight; about 5.6 ounces dry udon yields 14 ounces cooked. Cook udon per the package, as times vary by brand.
- If you cannot buy thinly sliced beef, slice a tender cut as thin as possible.
