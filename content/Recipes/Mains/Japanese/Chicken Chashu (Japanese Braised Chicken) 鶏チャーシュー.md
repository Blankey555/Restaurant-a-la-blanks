## Ingredients

- 2 boneless, skin-on chicken thighs (large, about 210g each)
- 2 green onions, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 5 thin slices fresh ginger, peeled
- 2 tsp neutral oil
- 1 cup water

### Seasonings
- ½ cup mirin
- ½ cup soy sauce
- ½ cup sake
- 2 tbsp sugar

---

## Instructions

### Prepare the Chicken
1. Trim excess fat and tendons from the thighs.
2. With skin side down, score the flesh at ½-inch intervals across the connective fibers, especially in the thickest parts. Pound with the back of a knife to flatten evenly.
3. Turn the chicken sideways and roll tightly from the bottom edge, skin side out. Tie snugly with butcher twine at ½-inch intervals. Not too tight, not too loose.

### Sear
4. Preheat a pot just large enough to hold the rolls over medium-low heat. Add oil.
5. Place rolls seam side down. Sear without moving for 2 minutes until the seam sets. Rotate and brown all sides, about 10 minutes total.
6. Turn off heat and wipe excess fat with a paper towel.

### Braise
7. Add water and scrape up the browned bits.
8. Add mirin, soy sauce, sake, and sugar. Stir to combine.
9. Add ginger and green onion. Bring to a boil over medium heat.
10. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 7 to 8 minutes.
11. Flip the chicken and place a drop lid (otoshibuta) directly on the meat. Simmer uncovered for another 6 to 7 minutes.
12. Increase to medium-high. Baste the chicken with the sauce until it thickens and becomes glossy, about 5 minutes.
13. Remove and discard the ginger and green onion.

### Marinate Overnight
14. Transfer the chicken and all braising liquid to a container. Cool completely, cover, and refrigerate for at least 8 hours. Optional: add peeled soft-boiled eggs to the liquid to make ramen eggs.

### Serve
15. Remove twine with kitchen shears. Slice crosswise into ½-inch rounds.
16. Optional: sear the surface with a kitchen torch until lightly charred.
17. Serve over ramen or rice, drizzled with warmed braising sauce.

---

## Storage
- **Fridge:** Up to 7 days in an airtight container, separate from the marinade.
- **Freezer:** Up to 1 month. Defrost overnight in the fridge.
- **Reheat:** Soak slices in hot braising sauce, add directly to hot ramen, or sear with a kitchen torch.

---

## Notes
- Use the largest chicken thighs you can find. Small thighs are hard to roll successfully.
- Don't skip the overnight rest. It's essential for flavor absorption.
- The braising liquid doubles as a marinade for ramen eggs and a drizzle sauce for rice bowls.
- For gluten-free, substitute tamari for soy sauce.
