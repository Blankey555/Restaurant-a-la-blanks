## Ingredients

### Chicken Dashi
- 8 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 16 dried shiitake mushrooms
- 30 g kombu (kelp), about a 10-inch square piece
- 20 g dried bonito flakes (about 2 cups loosely packed)

### Tare and Chashu
- 1 1/4 cups low-sodium soy sauce
- 1 1/4 cups mirin
- 1/2 cup sake
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons packed brown sugar
- 1 2-inch piece fresh ginger, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, peeled and smashed
- 3 green onions, halved
- 1 lb pork belly, skin on, cut into 2-inch-wide strips

### Nitamago
- 4 cold large eggs
- 2 1/2 cups reserved chashu-tare liquid

### Garlic La-Yu
- 8 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1/2 cup canola oil
- 1 tablespoon ground chili or 1 1/2 tablespoons crushed red pepper flakes
- 2 teaspoons sesame seeds

### To Serve (per bowl)
- 4 oz fresh ramen noodles
- 1 1/2 cups chicken dashi
- 2 reserved shiitake mushrooms, sliced
- 3 tablespoons or more tare, to taste
- 2 slices chashu
- 1 nitamago, halved
- 1 green onion, white and light green parts only, very thinly sliced
- 2 teaspoons la-yu
- Nori (optional)

## Instructions

### Dashi
1. Bring the broth to a bare simmer over medium heat, take off the heat and cool 2 minutes. Steep the mushrooms and kombu 5 minutes, add the bonito and steep 5 minutes more. Strain, saving the solids for the eggs and serving.

### Tare and Chashu
2. Bring everything except the pork to a low simmer in a medium saucepan, then lower the heat.
3. In another pot, bring the pork and 6 cups water to a low simmer, then drain at once and rinse the pork gently.
4. Add the pork to the tare, return to a simmer, cover and cook on low, turning occasionally, until tender, about 1 hour 20 minutes.
5. Cool 20 minutes, strain and reserve the liquid. Refrigerate pork and liquid separately. Slice the pork to serve, torching lightly if you like.

### Nitamago
6. Bring 6 cups water to a rolling boil. Prick the rounded end of each egg with a pin, lower in gently, reduce to medium-low and simmer 6 minutes. Drain.
7. Ice the eggs 5 minutes, crack the shells all over gently, return to the ice bath 10 minutes more, then peel.
8. Cover the eggs with the reserved pork liquid, weigh them down with the spent kombu and refrigerate at least 4 and up to 12 hours.

### Garlic La-Yu
9. Cook the garlic in the oil over the lowest heat until tender and translucent, about 15 minutes, without letting it fry.
10. Off the heat, stir in the chili, wait 2 minutes, then stir in the sesame seeds.

### To Serve
11. Boil the noodles in salted water, stirring, until al dente, about 1 minute (for instant ramen, skip the seasoning packet and follow package timing). Drain well.
12. Warm the dashi with the shiitake until barely simmering, 1 minute; remove the shiitake.
13. Put dashi, tare and noodles in a bowl and top with chashu, nitamago, shiitake, green onion, a drizzle of la-yu and nori if using.

## Notes

- Dashi keeps chilled up to 1 week.
- La-yu keeps refrigerated in a closed container up to 2 weeks once fully cool.
- The serving quantities are per bowl.
