## Ingredients

- One 3-ounce package instant ramen noodles
- 1 large egg
- 1/4 cup shredded cabbage
- 2 thin slices maple glazed ham
- 1 tablespoon thinly sliced scallions
- Sriracha, for serving

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine the noodles, seasoning packet and 2 cups water. Microwave on high until the noodles are cooked, 4 minutes.
2. Stir, crack the egg on top, and microwave on high 1 minute, until the white is just set and the yolk still runny.
3. Top with cabbage, ham and scallions, drizzle with Sriracha and serve right away.

## Notes

- The source suggests spinach in place of cabbage, or roasted chicken in place of the ham.
