**Prep:** 10 minutes
**Cook:** 15 minutes
**Rest:** 5 minutes
**Servings:** 2

---

## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets (approximately 6 oz / 175g each)
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- ¼ tsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp lemon juice, freshly squeezed
- 1 tbsp honey
- ⅓ cup raw pistachios, coarsely chopped

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. Rinse salmon fillets and pat dry. Place skin side down on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Season with salt and pepper.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together garlic, olive oil, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, and honey.
4. Spread ¾ of the glaze evenly over the salmon fillets.
5. Add the chopped pistachios to the remaining glaze and stir to combine. Spoon the pistachio mixture over the salmon and press gently with the back of a spoon to adhere.
6. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes until the salmon flakes easily with a fork. Pull it just before fully cooked as it will continue to cook while resting.
7. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

---

## Air Fryer Method

1. Pat salmon dry and season with salt and pepper.
2. Brush with the garlic-Dijon-honey glaze and press pistachios firmly onto the top of each fillet.
3. Place in a single layer in a lightly greased or parchment-lined air fryer basket.
4. Air fry at 375°F (190°C) for 10 to 12 minutes until the salmon flakes easily and reaches 125 to 130°F internal temperature for medium.
5. Rest 2 to 3 minutes before serving.

---

## Storage
- **Fridge:** Up to 3 days in an airtight container.
- **Reheat:** In a 300°F oven or air fryer for 5 to 10 minutes. Avoid microwaving as it softens the crust and dries out the fish.
- **Freezer:** Not recommended. Cooked salmon becomes rubbery when frozen and thawed.

---

## Notes
- Pat the salmon completely dry before glazing. This helps the crust adhere and ensures it crisps properly.
- Chop pistachios to small, uneven pieces. Too fine becomes powder; too coarse and they fall off.
- Target internal temperature is 125 to 130°F for medium. The salmon will finish cooking as it rests.
- Parchment paper prevents the crust from sticking and makes cleanup easy.
- Maple syrup can substitute for honey.
- Walnuts, almonds, or pecans can substitute for pistachios.
