## Ingredients

### Dough
- 1 package dry yeast
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1 cup lukewarm water
- 3 1/2 cups flour
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons melted shortening
- oil

### Meat Filling
- 1 pound ground lamb and beef
- 2 medium onions, chopped fine
- 1/2 cup minced parsley
- 1 red pepper, chopped fine
- 1/2 green pepper, chopped fine
- 1 can (16 oz) Italian-style tomatoes
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon crushed dried mint
- 1 clove garlic, mashed
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Instructions

1. Dissolve yeast and sugar in one-quarter cup lukewarm water. Stir and let stand a few minutes to proof.
2. In a large bowl combine three cups flour and the salt. Make a well in the center and add the yeast mixture, melted shortening and remaining water. Mix.
3. Knead until dough is smooth and elastic, about five minutes. Add more flour if needed. Lightly oil surface of dough. Place a sheet of plastic wrap across bowl, wrap well, and place in a warm, draft-free place until doubled in bulk, or about three hours.
4. Mix together all the ingredients for filling. Remove tomatoes from can and crush them before adding to mixture. Do not use the juice remaining in can. Taste to adjust seasoning. Set aside.
5. Punch down dough when doubled in bulk and shape into fourteen balls. Place them on a tray and cover with a plastic sheet or dish towel. Set aside for ten minutes.
6. Roll each ball into a circle about six inches in diameter, sprinkling a little flour on surface and rolling pin as needed. Place circles on greased cookie tray (or line tray with baking parchment, ungreased). Spread two or three tablespoonfuls of meat mixture over the entire surface of circle.
7. Bake in preheated 450 degree oven. Place tray on lower rack for six minutes then on upper rack for six minutes. Lahmajoons should be slightly brown on the bottom and meat mixture sizzling and light brown when done. Serve hot with lemon wedges. Squeeze lemon over just before eating.

**Yield:** Fourteen meat pies.

## Notes

- Meat pies may be baked and frozen. Freeze with meat sides faced together and pack in a plastic bag, secured tightly with a twister seal. To reheat, place pies in pairs on cookie sheet in hot oven for five minutes, turning once.
- Variation 1: divide dough into three balls, divide meat mixture in three sections, roll each ball out to fit a cookie tray. Follow same baking directions. Cut into squares and serve.
- Variation 2: use English muffins instead of dough. Split 12 or 14 muffins in half, toast tops and spread with meat mixture. Follow same baking directions.
- Variation 3: use Armenian lavash bread instead of the dough. Brush baking pan with melted butter, place lavash in pan, spread with meat mixture and bake until meat sizzles and is a light brown, about fifteen minutes.
- Variation 4: for an interesting taste, sprinkle unbaked tops of pies with pomegranate seeds.
