## Ingredients

### Chelo (Rice)

- 1 3/4 cups fragrant long-grain white rice, such as basmati
- Water
- Salt
- 1/3 cup melted unsalted butter

### Kebab

- 5 large onions
- 1/8 teaspoon saffron threads
- 2 pounds boneless lamb or beef, in kebab pieces
- Salt and pepper
- 1/4 cup butter, melted (optional)
- Cilantro
- 4 onions, quartered (optional)
- 1 to 2 teaspoons ground sumac (optional)

## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice in three changes of lukewarm water, then soak 1 hour in cold water with 1 1/2 tablespoons salt.
2. Boil 2 quarts water with 1 tablespoon salt. Add the drained rice and boil 5 to 10 minutes, stirring twice, until nearly done but not soft. Drain and rinse with lukewarm water.
3. Put a third of the butter and 2 tablespoons water in the pot. Spoon the rice in loosely so it forms a cone, pour the remaining butter over, and poke 2 to 3 holes to the bottom with a spoon handle.
4. Cover the pot with a towel, then the lid. Cook on medium 10 to 15 minutes, then on low 35 to 40 minutes. It holds in a warm oven up to 1 hour.
5. For the kebab, purée 1 onion in a food processor and push it through a fine strainer. Grind the saffron to a powder and dissolve it in the onion juice.
6. Trim the meat, toss with the onion juice, cover, and marinate 1 hour at room temperature.
7. Season with salt and pepper, thread onto skewers, and grill until done. Brush with melted butter.
8. Set the rice pot in cold water for 1 minute, invert onto a platter, and share out the crust. Serve with the kebab, cilantro, the remaining onions quartered, and a sprinkle of sumac.

## Notes

- This is the LA Times chunk kebab (chelo kebab), not ground-meat koobideh; the saved bookmark set treated them as the same dish.
- Without saffron, the source substitutes 2 teaspoons ground cumin and 2 tablespoons ground coriander. The saffron marinade also works for chicken and fish.
- The source uses onion juice rather than chopped onion for a richer aroma; open a window when puréeing.
