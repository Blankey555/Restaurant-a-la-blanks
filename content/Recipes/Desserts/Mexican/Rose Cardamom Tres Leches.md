## Ingredients

### Cake
- Yellow cake (box or recipe)
- Ingredients needed for the cake (eggs, oil, etc.)
- 1/2 teaspoon cardamom

### Milk Mixture
- One 14-ounce can sweetened condensed milk
- One 12-ounce can evaporated milk
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 1 to 2 tablespoons rose water

### Garnish
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1 to 2 teaspoons sugar (optional)
- Fresh raspberries (or fruit of your choice)
- Chopped pistachios

## Instructions

1. Make the cake according to the box (or your own recipe), working the cardamom into the batter. If you plan to serve it in cups, bake it on a parchment-lined sheet pan; it bakes fastest and breaks up easily.
2. Combine all the milk mixture ingredients in a bowl, ideally one with a spout. Set aside.
3. Chill the cream, bowl, and whisk. Whip the cream on low until slightly thickened, then on medium-high to stiff peaks, adding the sugar a little at a time. Put it in a piping bag (or zip-top bag) and refrigerate.
4. Break the cake into pieces and pack lightly into 6 to 8 ounce glasses, filling about halfway. Pour the milk mixture over each until it reaches the top of the cake. Soak at least 1 to 2 hours, or overnight.
5. To serve, pipe whipped cream on each cup and top with fruit and pistachios.

## Notes

- Recipe by Abeer Najjar (abeernajjar.com), via NPR's The Salt; recovered from a 2017 Internet Archive snapshot. No yield was given.
- If keeping the cake whole, one batch of milk mixture is enough for the whole cake. In cups, it covers about 6 to 8 cups and leaves about half the cake; double the milk mixture to use all the cake (and get about twice the servings).
- Make ahead: the cups can soak overnight.
