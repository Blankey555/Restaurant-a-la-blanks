## Ingredients

- 270 grams unsalted butter (2 sticks plus 3 tablespoons), at room temperature
- 130 grams light brown sugar (about 2/3 cup)
- 2 teaspoons fresh lemon juice
- 2 medium-sized blood oranges
- 122 grams fine cornmeal (about 1 cup)
- 65 grams all-purpose flour (about 1/2 cup)
- 8 grams baking powder (about 1 1/2 teaspoons)
- 2 grams fine sea salt (about 1/2 teaspoon)
- 200 grams granulated sugar (about 1 cup)
- 4 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1/3 cup sour cream
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9-inch round cake pan.
2. Melt 3 tablespoons (45 grams) butter in a small pan over medium heat. Add the brown sugar and lemon juice and stir about 3 minutes until the sugar melts. Scrape into the pan.
3. Grate 1/2 teaspoon zest from one orange. Cut the peel and pith off both oranges, slice into 1/4-inch wheels, remove seeds, and arrange in a single tight layer over the sugar mixture.
4. Whisk the zest, cornmeal, flour, baking powder, and salt. In another bowl, cream the remaining 225 grams butter with the granulated sugar, beat in the eggs one at a time, then the sour cream and vanilla. Fold in the dry mix by hand.
5. Spread the batter over the oranges and bake 40 to 50 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
6. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, run a knife around the edge, invert onto a platter, and cool completely before serving.
