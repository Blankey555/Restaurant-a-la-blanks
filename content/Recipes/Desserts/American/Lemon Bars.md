## Ingredients

### Base
- 2 cups sifted flour
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 1 cup butter

### Filling
- 4 large eggs, beaten
- 2 cups white sugar
- 1/3 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon fresh lemon rind (optional)

## Instructions

1. Base: work the butter into the flour and powdered sugar with your hands until it holds together. Press into a 13 x 9 x 2-inch pan.
2. Bake at 350°F for 20 to 25 minutes, until lightly browned. Let cool.
3. Filling: beat the eggs, sugar and lemon juice together. Sift the flour with the baking powder and stir in (with the lemon rind, if using).
4. Pour over the cooled crust and bake at 350°F for 25 minutes.
5. Cool, dust with powdered sugar and cut into bars.

## Notes

- The source lists the yield as 12 bars or so (8 to 12 servings). It does not list a separate quantity for the powdered sugar used for dusting.
