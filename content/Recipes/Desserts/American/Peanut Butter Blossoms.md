## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 4 ounces (1 stick) butter, at room temperature
- 1/2 cup smooth peanut butter (or other creamy nut butter)
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar, plus more for rolling
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 tablespoon milk, half-and-half, oat milk or nut milk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Nonstick spray or vegetable oil for cookie sheet (optional)
- 5 dozen (one 11-ounce package) Hershey's Kisses, foil removed

## Instructions

1. Sift the flour, baking soda, and salt together.
2. With a mixer, cream the butter, peanut butter, 1/2 cup granulated sugar, and brown sugar. Beat in the egg, milk, and vanilla. Gradually mix in the flour mixture. Chill about 1 hour if the dough is very soft.
3. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease or line a cookie sheet. Roll the dough into 1-inch balls (divide into 5 pieces of 12 balls each for an exact count).
4. Roll the balls in sugar and space 2 inches apart. Bake 6 to 8 minutes, until puffed and very lightly browned.
5. Press a Kiss into the center of each so the cookie cracks slightly, then bake 2 to 3 minutes more, until light golden. Cool completely.

## Notes

- Store in an airtight container.
- Any creamy nut butter can replace the peanut butter.
- The source frames these as a Pittsburgh wedding cookie-table classic.
