## Ingredients

- 1 cup butter, softened
- 1 1/2 cups sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 3/4 cups flour
- 2 teaspoons cream of tartar
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 3 teaspoons cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. In a large bowl, mix the butter, 1 1/2 cups sugar, vanilla and eggs thoroughly.
3. In another bowl, combine the flour, cream of tartar, baking soda and salt, then blend into the butter mixture.
4. Chill the dough, along with an ungreased cookie sheet, in the fridge for 10 to 15 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, stir together the 3 tablespoons sugar and the cinnamon.
6. Scoop 1-inch balls of dough and roll gently in the cinnamon sugar.
7. Set on the chilled sheet and bake 10 minutes. Remove from the pan right away.
