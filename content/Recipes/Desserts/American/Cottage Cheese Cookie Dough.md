## Ingredients

- 1 pound cottage cheese (the full-fat kind)
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- Almond flour, blanched and fine if you can get it
- Protein powder
- Chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Blend the cottage cheese, maple syrup, and vanilla until creamy. Stop and stir a few times; a few small curds are normal.
2. Transfer to a bowl and mix in almond flour and protein powder until it holds together like dough.
3. Stir in chocolate chips.

## Notes

- Neither the creator nor Delish gives amounts for the almond flour, protein powder, or chocolate chips; go by texture and taste.
- Whole (unblanched) almond flour works but adds brown flecks and grit. Delish suggests super-fine almond flour, or blending the dough without the chips to smooth it out.
