## Ingredients

- 1 pound high-fat cottage cheese
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup whole almond flour
- Protein powder (optional, for added protein)
- Chocolate chips (optional)

## Instructions

1. Blend 1 pound of high-fat cottage cheese with 1/4 cup of maple syrup and 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract in a blender until creamy.
2. Transfer the mixture to a bowl and add 1/4 cup of whole almond flour and protein powder (if using). Mix well.
3. Add chocolate chips and mix until combined.
4. Taste and adjust as needed.

## Notes

- This recipe can be made up to 2 days ahead and stored in the refrigerator.
- For a smoother texture, consider using a super-fine almond flour or blending the dough in the blender.
- The protein powder significantly increases the protein content, making this a high-protein snack.
