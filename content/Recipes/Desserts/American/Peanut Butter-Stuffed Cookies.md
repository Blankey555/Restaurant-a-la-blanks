## Ingredients

### Filling
- 1 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar

### Cookies
- 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, softened
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar, plus more for rolling
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 375°F and line two large baking sheets with parchment.
2. Filling: mix the peanut butter and powdered sugar until smooth, scoop into about 1 1/2-inch balls and freeze.
3. Cookies: beat the peanut butter, butter and both sugars with a hand mixer on medium until fluffy. Beat in the egg and vanilla, then mix in the flour, baking soda and salt just until combined.
4. Flatten a heaping tablespoon of dough into a disc, set a frozen filling ball on top and pinch the dough closed around it, adding dough as needed to cover it fully.
5. Roll in sugar and place on the sheets about 2 inches apart. Repeat with remaining dough.
6. Bake until golden on the bottom, 12 to 15 minutes. Serve warm or at room temperature.
