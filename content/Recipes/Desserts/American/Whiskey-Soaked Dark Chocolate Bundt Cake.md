## Ingredients

- 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened, more for greasing pan
- 2 cups all-purpose flour, more for dusting pan
- 5 ounces unsweetened chocolate
- 1/4 cup instant espresso powder
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cup bourbon, rye or other whiskey, more for sprinkling
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 3 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- Confectioners' sugar, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Grease and flour a 10-cup Bundt pan (or two 8- or 9-inch loaf pans). Preheat the oven to 325°F. Melt the chocolate in the microwave or over a double boiler; let cool.
2. Put the espresso and cocoa powders in a 2-cup glass measure and add boiling water up to the 1-cup line. Stir to dissolve, then add whiskey and salt. Let cool.
3. With an electric mixer, beat the butter until fluffy. Beat in sugar, then the eggs one at a time. Beat in vanilla, baking soda and melted chocolate, scraping the bowl.
4. On low speed, beat in a third of the whiskey mixture, then 1 cup flour once absorbed. Repeat, finishing with the whiskey mixture.
5. Scrape into the pan, smooth, and bake until a tester comes out clean, about 1 hour 10 minutes for a Bundt (start checking loaf pans at 55 minutes).
6. Cool on a rack 15 minutes, unmold, and sprinkle the warm cake with more whiskey. Cool fully and dust with confectioners' sugar if you like.

## Notes

- Can be baked in two 8- or 9-inch loaf pans instead of a Bundt pan; they bake faster.
