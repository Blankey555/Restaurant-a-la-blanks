## Ingredients

### Crust
- 1 cup graham cracker crumbs
- 3 to 4 tablespoons butter

### Mixture 1
- 1 1/2 pounds cream cheese
- 4 eggs
- 3/4 teaspoon vanilla
- 1 cup sugar

### Mixture 2
- 1/2 pound sour cream
- 2/3 cup sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees. Butter a 10-inch springform pan.
2. Line the pan with the crust mixture.
3. Mix Mixture 1 at high speed for 15 minutes.
4. Put Mixture 1 in the pan. Bake for 30 minutes (until firm).
5. Cool for 10 to 15 minutes.
6. Mix Mixture 2 until smooth.
7. Top the cake with Mixture 2.
8. Let the cake set in the refrigerator overnight.

**Makes:** 1 cake.
