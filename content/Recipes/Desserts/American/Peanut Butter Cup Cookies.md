## Ingredients

- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1/2 cup white sugar
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons milk
- 40 miniature chocolate-covered peanut butter cups, unwrapped

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Sift the flour, salt and baking soda together and set aside.
2. In another bowl, cream the butter, white sugar, peanut butter and brown sugar until fluffy. Beat in the egg, vanilla and milk.
3. Mix in the flour mixture.
4. Roll into 40 balls and set each in a cup of an ungreased mini muffin pan.
5. Bake 8 minutes.
6. Right out of the oven, press a mini peanut butter cup into each. Let cool before carefully removing from the pan.
