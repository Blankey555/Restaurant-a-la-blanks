## Ingredients

- 1 pound 4 ounces white chocolate, chopped
- 1 1/2 ounces crispy rice, broken into pieces
- 2 cups unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 3/4 cups bread flour
- 1/4 cup ground coffee
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 1/4 teaspoons baking soda
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 1/2 cups light brown sugar
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs
- 6 ounces red miso
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (or paste)

## Instructions

1. Caramelized white chocolate: heat the oven to 250°F. Spread three-quarters of the chocolate on a parchment-lined sheet and bake, stirring thoroughly every 5 to 6 minutes, until caramelized, about 40 minutes.
2. Stir in the rest of the chocolate to temper, add the crispy rice, spread on parchment, and refrigerate until hard. Break into small pieces.
3. Brown the butter in a 12-inch skillet over medium until nutty and golden, 8 to 10 minutes. Cool completely until solid (about 1 1/2 cups).
4. Combine the flours, coffee, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
5. Beat the brown butter and sugars on medium with a hand mixer until fluffy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the miso and vanilla.
6. Beat in the dry ingredients until smooth, then fold in the chocolate pieces.
7. Heat the oven to 300°F. Roll into 2-ounce balls, flatten slightly, and bake on parchment until lightly golden, 12 to 14 minutes. Cool 5 to 10 minutes on the sheet, then on a rack.
