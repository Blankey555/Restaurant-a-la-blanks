## Ingredients

### Cake
- 12 tablespoons (1 1/2 sticks) butter, more for pan
- 6 ounces bittersweet chocolate, chopped into small pieces
- A few drops almond extract
- 2 tablespoons strong coffee
- 4 large eggs, separated
- Pinch of salt
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 3/4 cups finely ground almonds

### Glaze
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 tablespoon corn syrup
- 4 ounces bittersweet chocolate, chopped into small pieces
- 1 tablespoon butter

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 325°F. Butter a 9-inch springform pan and line the side with parchment. Melt the butter, 6 ounces chocolate, almond extract and coffee in a heavy pan over low heat; transfer to a bowl to cool.
2. Beat the egg whites and salt with a mixer to soft peaks, then slowly add 1/2 cup sugar until thick and glossy.
3. In another bowl, whisk the yolks with the remaining 1/2 cup sugar until thick. Fold in the chocolate mixture and the almonds. Lighten with a dollop of whites, then gently fold in the rest, keeping it airy.
4. Scrape into the pan and bake 30 to 40 minutes, until dry on top and slightly gooey in the center (check at 30 minutes; keep baking if very wet). Cool on a rack 20 minutes, remove the side, and cool further. Cracks on top are fine; the glaze covers them.
5. For the glaze, bring the 2 tablespoons sugar, corn syrup and 1/4 cup water to a boil, then take off the heat. Add the 4 ounces chocolate, swirl, and let stand about 3 minutes to melt.
6. Whisk in 1 tablespoon butter, pour evenly over the cake, and ease it to the edges with a spatula. Let it cool and set before slicing.

## Notes

- Flourless as written (ground almonds).
