## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup/180 grams clarified butter, at room temperature
- 1 3/4 cups/215 grams confectioners' sugar
- 2 teaspoons/1 gram dried lavender, ground to a powder
- 1/2 teaspoon/3 grams kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon/5 milliliters orange blossom water
- 1 3/4 cups/228 grams all-purpose flour, more as needed

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 325°F with a rack in the center. Line two sheet pans (not dark metal) with parchment.
2. Beat the butter on high about 6 minutes until fluffy. Add 3/4 cup (92 grams) confectioners' sugar, the lavender and salt and beat 2 minutes more. Scrape down, then mix in the orange blossom water on low.
3. Add the flour about 1/2 cup at a time until the dough is crumbly and slightly dry but holds when squeezed, adding more flour if needed.
4. Quarter the dough and press each piece into a long log about 1 inch high and wide. Cut diagonally into diamonds 1 to 2 inches long and space them 2 inches apart on the pans. Bake 15 to 20 minutes, rotating halfway, until the edges are golden.
5. Sift the remaining confectioners' sugar over the cookies while warm.

## Notes

- Store airtight for up to 1 week (per the source).
- Recipe from Maureen Abood; the source includes a note on making clarified butter.
