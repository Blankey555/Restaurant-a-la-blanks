## Ingredients

- 2 (13.4-ounce) cans sweetened condensed milk, labels removed
- 1 tablespoon/15 milliliters brandy
- 1 teaspoon/2 grams finely grated lemon zest
- 2 teaspoons/10 milliliters vanilla extract
- 1 cup/125 grams all-purpose flour
- 1 cup plus 1 tablespoon/165 grams cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon/5 grams baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon/1 gram baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon/3 grams fine sea salt, plus a pinch for dulce de leche
- 1/2 cup/100 grams granulated sugar
- 10 tablespoons/141 grams unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 2 large egg yolks
- 1 cup/100 grams finely ground dried coconut

## Instructions

1. Dulce de leche: lay the unopened cans on their sides in a large pot of boiling water and simmer 3 hours 15 minutes, topping up with hot water so they stay submerged (never let the water boil dry or the cans could burst). Cool completely on a rack before opening.
2. Mix the brandy, lemon zest and 1 teaspoon vanilla in a small bowl.
3. Whisk the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl.
4. Beat the sugar and butter until fluffy, about 3 minutes. Beat in the yolks, then the brandy mixture, then mix in the dry ingredients on low just until the dough comes together.
5. Halve the dough, roll each half into a log about 1 1/2 inches thick, wrap, and chill at least 2 hours until firm.
6. Heat oven to 350°F and line 3 baking sheets with parchment. Slice the logs into 1/8-inch rounds and bake until the edges begin to turn golden, about 7 minutes. Cool completely on racks.
7. Stir the remaining 1 teaspoon vanilla and a large pinch of salt into the dulce de leche and load it into a piping bag or a zipper bag with a corner snipped.
8. Turn half the cookies over, pipe a thick layer of filling on each, top with the remaining cookies, and roll the edges in coconut. Eat right away or refrigerate until serving.

## Notes

- Store in the refrigerator until serving (per the source).
- A spoon works in place of a piping bag for the filling.
