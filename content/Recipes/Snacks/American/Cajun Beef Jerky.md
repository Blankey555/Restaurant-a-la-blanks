## Ingredients

### Lean Meat

- 1 lb Beef Eye of Round or Venison

### Marinade

- 1/2 cup cold water
- 1/4 cup white vinegar
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 tsp seasoning salt
- 2 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp horseradish
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1/4 tsp curing salt (Prague Powder #1)

## Instructions

1. Trim all visible fat from the beef and freeze it 1 to 2 hours until firm but not solid.
2. Meanwhile, mix the water, vinegar, and all the seasonings (including the curing salt) in a bowl or zip-top bag.
3. Slice the meat into 1/4 inch strips: against the grain for a tender chew, with the grain for a chewier one.
4. Add the strips to the marinade and refrigerate 8 to 24 hours.
5. Drain off the marinade and pat the strips dry with paper towels.
6. Lay the strips on dehydrator racks without touching and dry at 160°F, about 5 hours. Thicker slices take longer.
7. It is done when it bends and cracks but does not snap in half, with white fibers visible when bent or torn. Let cool on the racks.

## Notes

- This is the Cajun Beef Jerky recipe Jerkyholic uses to demonstrate the dehydrator method in the bookmarked guide.
- Aim for an internal temperature of 160°F.
- The source says jerky keeps 7 to 10 days in zip-top bags, or 1 to 2 months vacuum sealed.
- A jerky slicer can replace the freezing step for even strips.
