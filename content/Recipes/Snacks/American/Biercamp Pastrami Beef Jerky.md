## Ingredients

- 3 lbs flank steak, brisket or eye-round
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tbsp pureed garlic
- 2 tbsp cracked pepper
- 2 tbsp coriander seed

## Instructions

1. Slice the meat into thin strips, 1/4 to 1/2 inch thick, with a very sharp knife.
2. Combine the soy sauce, brown sugar, Worcestershire, and garlic in a large zip-top bag (hold back the pepper and coriander). Add the meat and marinate in the fridge overnight.
3. Let the meat come to room temperature.
4. Heat the oven to 180°F with a sheet tray on the bottom rack, and spray the racks with nonstick spray.
5. Lay the strips across the racks with space between them, and sprinkle evenly with the cracked pepper and coriander. More makes it spicier.
6. Prop the oven door open a crack and dry 2 to 4 hours, depending on how chewy you want it. It is done when the color looks right and it pulls apart gently rather than snapping.

## Notes

- From Biercamp in Ann Arbor, via Men's Journal's "10 Great Beef Jerky Recipes".
- Biercamp suggests shredding it over salad, chopping it into chili, or serving on a charcuterie board.
