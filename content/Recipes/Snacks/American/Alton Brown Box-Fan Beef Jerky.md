## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 to 2 pounds flank steak
- 2/3 cup Worcestershire sauce
- 2/3 cup soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon liquid smoke
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- Special equipment: 1 box fan, 4 paper air-conditioning filters, and 2 bungee cords

## Instructions

1. Trim excess fat from the steak, bag it, and freeze 1 to 2 hours to firm up.
2. Slice thinly with the grain into long strips.
3. Put the strips and all remaining ingredients in a 1-gallon zip-top bag, toss to distribute, and refrigerate 3 to 6 hours.
4. Pat the strips dry. Lay them in the grooves of 3 filters, stack the filters, and cap with the empty fourth filter.
5. Lay the box fan on its side, set the stack on it, and strap it on with the bungee cords. Stand the fan up, set to medium, and dry 8 to 12 hours.

## Notes

- Recipe courtesy of Alton Brown, 2005.
- A commercial dehydrator works too; follow its manufacturer's directions.
- The source says it keeps 2 to 3 months in an airtight container in a cool, dry place.
