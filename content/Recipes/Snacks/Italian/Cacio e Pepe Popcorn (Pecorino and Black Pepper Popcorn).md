## Ingredients

- ½ cup popcorn kernels
- 3 tbsp neutral oil (for popping)
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- ¾ cup finely grated Pecorino Romano (microplane-grated for best adhesion)
- 1½ tsp freshly cracked black pepper, or more to taste
- ½ tsp flaky salt

---

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat. Add 3 kernels and cover. When they pop, the oil is ready.
2. Add remaining kernels in a single layer, cover, and cook, shaking the pot frequently, until popping slows to 2 to 3 seconds between pops.
3. Immediately transfer to a large bowl.
4. Drizzle melted butter over the popcorn and toss quickly to coat evenly before it cools.
5. Add the grated Pecorino and black pepper and toss vigorously. The cheese will clump slightly onto the warm, buttered popcorn.
6. Finish with flaky salt. Taste and adjust pepper and cheese as needed.
7. Serve immediately.

---

## Notes
- Microplane-grated Pecorino is essential. Pre-grated or coarsely grated cheese will not adhere properly and will fall to the bottom of the bowl.
- Work quickly after popping. The cheese sticks best while the popcorn is still hot.
- Black pepper is the star here. Be generous. It should have a noticeable bite.
- Parmesan can substitute for Pecorino but will produce a milder, less sharp result.
- For extra richness, brown the butter before drizzling.
