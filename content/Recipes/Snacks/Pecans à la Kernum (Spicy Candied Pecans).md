*Candied pecans with a stiff, crunchy candy shell. Heavy on cayenne if you like heat. Wing the measurements or follow the guide below.*

---

## Ingredients

- Pecans
- Brown sugar
- Cinnamon
- Salt
- Vanilla extract
- Cayenne pepper (generous, to taste)
- Egg whites

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 300°F.
2. Whip the egg whites vigorously for 5 to 10 minutes until very stiff and frothy, with firm peaks that hold their shape.
3. Combine brown sugar, cinnamon, salt, vanilla extract, and cayenne in a bowl. Mix well.
4. Fold in the whipped egg whites until fully incorporated.
5. Add the pecans and toss until evenly coated.
6. Spread in a single layer on a parchment-lined baking sheet.
7. Bake until the coating is set, dry, and crunchy, turning once halfway through, about 30 to 40 minutes total.
8. Cool completely on the pan. The candy shell will continue to harden as they cool.

---

## Notes
- The egg whites are the key. Whip them until stiff peaks hold without collapsing. This is what creates the thick, crunchy candy shell rather than a sticky glaze.
- Cayenne is the defining characteristic of this recipe. Be generous.
- Do not rush the cooling step. The candy hardens fully at room temperature.
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks.
