*A crunchy, candy-shelled pecan with heat from cayenne and warmth from cinnamon. Measurements are flexible — adjust to taste.*

---

## Ingredients

- 2 cups pecans
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ¼ to ½ tsp cayenne pepper (or more to taste)
- 1 egg white

---

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 300°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Whip the egg white vigorously for 5 to 10 minutes until very stiff and frothy with firm peaks that hold their shape without collapsing.
3. In a large bowl, combine brown sugar, cinnamon, salt, vanilla extract, and cayenne. Mix well.
4. Fold in the whipped egg white until fully incorporated.
5. Add the pecans and toss until evenly coated.
6. Spread in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
7. Bake for 30 to 40 minutes, turning once halfway through, until the coating is dry, set, and crunchy.
8. Cool completely on the pan before serving or storing. The candy shell continues to harden as they cool.

---

## Notes
- Stiff egg white peaks are essential. This is what builds the thick candy shell rather than a thin sticky glaze.
- Do not rush the cooling step.
- Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to two weeks.
- For extra heat, increase cayenne freely. The sweetness handles a surprising amount.
