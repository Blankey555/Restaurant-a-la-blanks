## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked chickpeas, rinsed if canned
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons za'atar
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt

## Instructions

1. Spread the chickpeas on paper towels, pat dry, and let them air-dry for about an hour.
2. Heat the oven to 400°F. Spread the chickpeas on a parchment-lined rimmed sheet pan and bake in the center of the oven about 30 minutes, stirring and rotating the pan every 10 minutes, until crunchy.
3. Transfer the hot chickpeas to a bowl and toss with the oil, za'atar, and salt.

## Notes

- The chickpeas keep crisping as they cool.
- Store cooled leftovers in an airtight container for up to a week.
