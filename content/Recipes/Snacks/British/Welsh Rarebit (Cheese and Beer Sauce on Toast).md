## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 1 tablespoon mustard powder, or to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne, or to taste
- 3/4 cup strong dark beer, like Guinness
- 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce, or to taste
- 1 pound Cheddar, Double Gloucester or other English cheese (or other good semi-hard cheese, like Comté or Gruyère, or a mixture), grated
- 4 to 8 pieces lightly toasted bread

## Instructions

1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat and stir in the flour. Cook 3 to 5 minutes, stirring, until golden and fragrant.
2. Stir in the mustard and cayenne, then whisk in the beer and Worcestershire.
3. Once smooth, lower the heat and stir in the cheese until melted and smooth. Pour into a wide container to set.
4. Spread thickly on the toast and broil until bubbling and the toast edges are crisp. Serve immediately.

## Notes

- The set cheese mixture can be refrigerated for up to a day before broiling.
