## Ingredients

- 3 pounds mussels
- 2 pounds onions, chopped (about 6 cups)
- 1 cup olive oil
- 1 cup rice
- 1 medium tomato, chopped
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
- 2 tablespoons chopped dill
- 1 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon red cayenne pepper
- 1/2 cup pine nuts
- 1/4 cup currants, optional
- 2 cups water
- 1 lemon

## Instructions

1. Rinse mussels well. Place in a basin of cold water, sprinkle generously with salt, and let stand for one hour to loosen sand and dirt. Drain and rinse well. Scrub mussels with a stiff brush or steel pad, going over each mussel at least twice. Use a small knife to open shells part way. Cut off mussel hairs and waste matters on the inside without disturbing the pink, fleshy meat. Wash each shell under cold running water once more, then set aside in cold water.
2. Saute onions in olive oil until transparent, about twenty minutes. Shut flame and add all ingredients except the water and lemon.
3. Mix thoroughly, then taste for additional seasoning before stuffing mussels.
4. Place a tablespoonful of filling in each mussel, then close carefully. Twist each mussel closed before stuffing the next. Arrange stuffed mussels in alternating rows in a roasting pan. Add the water slowly. Cut the lemon in half and squeeze one half over the mussels. Place an inverted dish on top to prevent shells from opening while baking.
5. Bake covered in a 350 degree oven for one to one and a half hours. Allow to cool completely before removing mussels from pan. Serve cold, garnished with lemon slices cut from the remaining half lemon.

**Yield:** About three dozen mussels.

## Notes

- Margin note from the book: twist mussel closed before stuffing the next.
- This may be cooked on top of the stove: reduce water by half a cup, bring to the boil, then simmer for one hour and fifteen minutes.
