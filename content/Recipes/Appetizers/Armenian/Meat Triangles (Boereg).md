## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound lean lamb, ground
- 1/2 pound beef, ground
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 cup minced onions
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/4 cup minced parsley
- 1 tablespoon flour or 2 tablespoons bread crumbs
- 1 pound phyllo dough
- 1 1/2 cups clarified butter, melted
- 1/4 cup sesame seeds, optional
- 2 slightly dampened dish towels

## Instructions

1. Place meat in a skillet, add water and cook over moderate heat until water is absorbed, about twenty to thirty minutes. Stir occasionally to break up meat clusters. Add onions and butter and continue to cook over low flame another fifteen minutes.
2. Add salt, spices and parsley, shut flame, let cool, then add the flour (or bread crumbs), mixing lightly.
3. Remove the dough from its plastic wrapper, unfold onto one towel and cover with the other towel. Work quickly, one sheet at a time, and keep remaining sheets always covered.
4. Place one sheet down flat, brush surface with melted butter and fold over in half lengthwise. Place a spoonful of the filling at the bottom center of the strip and bring both sides over to cover the filling.
5. Fold from the bottom up to the top in a triangular fold. Place on buttered baking pan, seam side down and brush top with melted butter. Sprinkle with sesame seeds if desired.
6. Bake in preheated 375 degree oven for twenty minutes or until a light golden brown. Serve hot.

**Yield:** About 30 triangles.

## Notes

- Instead of triangles, roll into individual or large cylinders: layer three sheets of the desired length, buttering between, spread the filling, and roll up like a burrito.
- See the notes on [[Cheese Triangles (Boereg)]]: a one pound package of phyllo contains 25 to 35 sheets depending on brand, and the triangles may be prepared in advance, frozen, and then baked. Allow an extra five minutes of baking from frozen.
