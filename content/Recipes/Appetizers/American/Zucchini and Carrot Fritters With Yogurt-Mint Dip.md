## Ingredients

### Fritters
- 1 cup all-purpose flour, more as needed
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt, more for serving
- 1 cup milk, more as needed
- 1 large egg
- 1/4 teaspoon grated lemon zest
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper
- 2 large carrots, grated (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 1 large zucchini, grated (about 2 cups)
- 2 scallions, finely chopped
- Olive oil, for frying

### Yogurt-mint dip
- 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tablespoon chopped mint
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil

## Instructions

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, coriander and 1/2 teaspoon salt in one large bowl, and the milk, egg, lemon zest and pepper in another.
2. Pour the dry into the wet and whisk just until combined. The batter should be a little thicker than cream; adjust with milk or flour. Stir in the carrots, zucchini and scallions and rest 30 minutes.
3. For the dip, mash the garlic with 1/4 teaspoon salt into a paste, then whisk with the yogurt, mint and olive oil. Cover and refrigerate.
4. Heat 1 inch of olive oil in a wide saucepan to 375°F. Line a sheet pan with paper towels. Fry tablespoonfuls of batter in batches without crowding, turning, until golden, 3 to 4 minutes. Drain, transfer to a platter, salt, and serve with the dip.
