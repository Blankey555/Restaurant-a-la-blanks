## Ingredients

- 3/4 pound mini sweet peppers (1 package, usually 12) or small lipstick peppers
- 6 ounces goat cheese (1/2 of a 12-ounce log)
- 1/3 cup low-fat (not nonfat) cottage cheese
- 3 tablespoons plain low-fat (not nonfat) Greek yogurt

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan with foil. Roast the peppers 8 to 10 minutes, turning halfway, until soft but not charred (smaller peppers cook faster; give larger ones up to 5 more minutes if needed). Let cool.
2. Process the goat cheese, cottage cheese and yogurt in a food processor until very smooth, at least 1 minute; scrape down and process 1 minute more. Transfer to a pastry bag with a 3/8-inch star tip.
3. Slice the ends off the cooled peppers just below the shoulders and remove seeds and membranes. Stand intact peppers in a small glass and pipe in the filling; for split peppers, pipe filling on and wrap the pepper around it.
4. Serve right away, or chill and take out 30 minutes before serving so the cheese is soft.

## Notes

- Can be chilled after filling; bring to room temperature (about 30 minutes) before serving.
