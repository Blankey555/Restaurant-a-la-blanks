## Ingredients

- 1 pound low-moisture mozzarella (preferably Polly-O)
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 large eggs
- 3 tablespoons whole milk
- 2 cups panko (Japanese breadcrumbs)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon dried basil
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried parsley
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- Vegetable oil (for frying; about 6 cups)
- Kosher salt
- Spicy marinara sauce (for serving)
- Special equipment: a deep-fry thermometer

## Instructions

1. Cut the mozzarella into 3 x 1/2 inch pieces. Put the flour in a shallow bowl, whisk the eggs and milk in a second, and mix the panko with the garlic powder, onion powder, and dried herbs in a third.
2. One piece at a time, dredge the cheese in flour (shake off excess), dip in egg, and coat in panko, pressing it on. Repeat the egg and panko for a second coat. Set on a wire rack in a rimmed baking sheet.
3. Freeze the rack of breaded cheese at least 1 hour.
4. Clip a deep-fry thermometer to a large pot and add oil to come 2 inches up the sides. Heat over medium-high to 350°F.
5. Fry in batches, turning now and then, 2 to 2 1/2 minutes, until golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels and salt while hot. Serve with marinara.

## Notes

- Substitute recipe: the saved Food & Wine piece about Zach Pollack's mozzarella sticks at Cosa Buona (Los Angeles) had no recipe and the link is dead, so this is Bon Appétit's mozzarella sticks. (Pollack's version uses smoked mozzarella with a garlicky marinara.)
- Do ahead: the sticks can be breaded up to 3 days ahead and kept frozen in an airtight freezer bag.
