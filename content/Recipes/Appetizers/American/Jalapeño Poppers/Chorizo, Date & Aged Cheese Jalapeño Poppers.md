## Ingredients

### Peppers
- 50 jalapeños, tops removed and hollowed

### Filling
- 350g Mexican chorizo
- 150g Oaxacan cheese, shredded
- 75g Manchego, shredded
- 75g Comté, shredded
- 150g medjool dates, pitted and finely chopped

### Breading
- All purpose flour
- Mexican lager or beer
- Masa harina

---

## Frying Instructions
1. Render chorizo in a pan over medium heat until cooked through. Drain fat, reserving a small amount to reincorporate.
2. Let chorizo cool, then combine with Oaxacan cheese, Manchego, Comté, dates, and reserved chorizo fat. Mix until evenly incorporated.
3. Fill hollowed jalapeños with the mixture. Pack tightly.
4. Freeze on a sheet pan until solid, then bag and store until ready to fry.
5. To fry: dredge frozen poppers in flour, dip in beer, coat in masa harina.
6. Fry at 375°F until golden and crispy, about 4 to 5 minutes.

## Baking Instructions
1. Follow steps 1 through 4 above.
2. Preheat oven to 425°F.
3. Dredge frozen poppers in flour, dip in beer, coat in masa harina.
4. Place on a wire rack over a sheet pan. Spray lightly with cooking oil.
5. Bake 20 to 25 minutes until golden, flipping halfway through.

---

## Notes
- Comté and Manchego are both aged and assertive. Together they replace the richness and binding that cream cheese provided, leaning the filling in a more savory, complex direction.
- Oaxacan cheese remains the dominant melt component. Keep its ratio higher than the aged cheeses.
- The dates provide the sweetness and moisture that cream cheese previously contributed. Do not reduce them.
- Comté varies significantly by age. A younger Comté (6 to 8 months) melts more smoothly. An older one (12 months plus) adds more depth but may be grainier in the filling.
