## Ingredients

* 12 medium button mushrooms or cremini mushrooms, cleaned with stems removed and discarded
* Olive oil spray
* 2 tablespoons sour cream
* 1/2 cup fresh bread crumbs or panko breadcrumbs
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 1/2 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
* 1/2 cup finely chopped green onions
* 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
* 2-3 tablespoons fresh chopped Italian parsley
* 8 ounces cooked shelled crab, finely chopped (King Crab)
* Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees.
2. Lightly spray mushrooms with olive oil spray and arrange cap side down on a baking sheet or dish.
3. In a large bowl, stir sour cream, bread crumbs, garlic, Worcestershire sauce, green onions, Parmesan, parsley, and crab meat. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
4. Scoop about 1 1/2 tablespoons of the mixture into each mushroom.
5. Bake for 20 minutes. Turn broiler on at last minute to brown tops lightly.

## Portobello Variant

Divide the filling among 4 large portobello caps for a main course. Expect a longer bake than the small caps; pull them when the mushrooms are tender and the tops are browned.

## Notes

* Family adjustments: crab set at 8 ounces and the 8 ounces of cream cheese replaced with 2 tablespoons of sour cream, for a filling that leans crab rather than cheese.
* Broilers can be unpredictable; stay close by when browning tops.
