## Ingredients

### The Sauce
* 1 tbsp dried shrimp (soaked in hot water for 15 min, then drained)
* 1/2 cup + 2 tbsp unsweetened dried coconut flakes
* 1 stalk lemongrass (white bulb only, thinly sliced)
* 1 small shallot (sliced)
* 2 slices galangal (coarsely chopped)
* 2 tbsp fresh ginger (peeled and chopped)
* 1 tbsp Thai shrimp paste
* 1/2 cup palm sugar + 1/3 cup dark brown sugar
* 1 tbsp fish sauce
* 1/4 cup water
* 2 tbsp roasted peanuts (finely chopped)

### The Salad Components
* 20 to 30 Cha-phlu leaves (substitute: young collard greens or Chinese broccoli leaves)
* 1 lime (cut into tiny 1/4-inch dice, skin on)
* 1 piece fresh ginger (3-inch, peeled and 1/4-inch diced)
* 2 shallots (1/4-inch diced)
* 1/2 cup roasted peanuts
* 5 Bird's eye chilies (sliced thin)
* 1/3 cup dried shrimp
* Toasted coconut (remaining from sauce prep)

## Instructions

### Prepare the Sauce Base
1. Toast all coconut flakes in a dry skillet over medium heat until golden brown (2 to 3 minutes). Set aside 2 tablespoons for the sauce and keep the rest for the salad platter.
2. In the same skillet, toast the sliced lemongrass, shallot, galangal, and ginger over medium-high heat until fragrant and the shallots look dry (about 5 minutes).
3. Process the toasted aromatics, drained shrimp, and shrimp paste in a food processor or mortar and pestle until a smooth paste forms.

### Cook the Dressing
1. Combine the paste, sugars, fish sauce, and water in a small saucepan.
2. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, stirring constantly.
3. Simmer until the sauce thickens and reduces to approximately 1 cup (about 3 minutes).
4. Remove from heat and cool completely. 
5. Stir in the 2 tablespoons of toasted coconut and the finely chopped peanuts.

### Assemble and Serve
1. Arrange the diced lime, ginger, shallots, peanuts, chilies, dried shrimp, and leaves on a large platter.
2. Place the sauce in a central bowl.
3. To eat, fold a leaf into a small cup or cone. Place a small amount of each ingredient inside, top with a spoonful of sauce, and eat in one bite.
