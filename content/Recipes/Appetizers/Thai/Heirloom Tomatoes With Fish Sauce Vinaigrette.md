## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons finely chopped garlic
- 1 teaspoon finely chopped shallot
- ¼ cup fresh lime juice (from 2 to 3 limes)
- 2 tablespoons fish sauce
- 2 tablespoons sugar or honey
- Kosher salt (such as Diamond Crystal) and finely ground black pepper
- 1 to 2 fresh Thai chiles (optional), thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons extra‑virgin olive oil
- 2 pounds ripe heirloom tomatoes, cored and cut into pretty pieces
- Handful of mint or shiso leaves, finely chopped
- Handful of cilantro leaves and tender stems or Thai basil leaves, finely chopped
- Extra‑virgin olive oil, for drizzling
- Flaky sea salt, for sprinkling
- ¼ cup unsalted roasted peanuts, chopped
- ¼ cup fried shallots, homemade or store‑bought

## Instructions

1. Make the vinaigrette: In a large bowl, combine the garlic and shallot with the lime juice and let sit for 2 minutes to tame their sharpness. Stir in the fish sauce, sugar, salt, a few turns of pepper and the chiles (if using). Slowly drizzle in the olive oil, whisking constantly, until emulsified. The vinaigrette will keep in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to one week.
2. Make the salad: Add the tomatoes, mint and cilantro to the vinaigrette bowl. Season with a generous pinch of kosher salt and a few turns of pepper, and toss gently but well.
3. Arrange the tomatoes in a large, shallow bowl in an even layer and pour the vinaigrette over them. Generously drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with a little flaky salt. Sprinkle on the peanuts and shallots, and serve immediately.

## Notes

- Keep the vinaigrette in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to one week.
