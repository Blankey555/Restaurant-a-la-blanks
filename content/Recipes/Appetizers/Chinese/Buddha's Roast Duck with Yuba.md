## Ingredients

- 3 large round sheets fresh yuba (beancurd skin), cut in half
OR 3 large rectangular sheets dried yuba (beancurd skin)
- 1/3 c. mushroom bouillon, or the water from soaking dried mushrooms
- 2 T. soy sauce
- 2 T. dry sherry
- 3/4 tsp. organic sugar
- 2 tsp. dark (Asian) sesame oil
- oil for shallow-frying

## Instructions

1. If using dried yuba, soak the sheets in warm water for 5-10 minutes, then pat them dry and cut them in half.
2. Mix the broth, soy sauce, sherry, sugar, and sesame oil in a small saucepan and heat until the sugar is dissolved.
3. Pour this mixture into a bowl and allow to cool slightly.
4. Spread a piece of fine cheesecloth or thin white cotton sheeting over a cookie sheet.
5. Place one half-sheet fresh or reconstituted dried yuba on this, brush with soy sauce mixture, cover with another piece of yuba, and repeat until all the yuba and sauce is used up.
6. Fold the short side in once, then again, so that it is folded in thirds, and flatten lightly.
7. Steam the roll, covered, on a steaming try with holes over boiling water for 10 minutes.
8. Remove the cloth carefully and cut the roll into 2 sections if needed.
9. Heat oil about 1/4-1/2" deep over high heat in a large skillet.
10. When the oil is hot, add the "package" (s) and fry until golden-brown for only a few seconds.
11. Turn over and fry the other side; it will probably puff up.
12. Remove from the pan and drain on paper.

## Notes

- Leftovers can be chopped and used in stuffings or rice or noodle dishes.
- To make MOCK PEKING DUCK, serve thinly sliced Buddha's Roast Duck with Mandarin pancakes or flour tortillas, and finely-shredded green onion.
