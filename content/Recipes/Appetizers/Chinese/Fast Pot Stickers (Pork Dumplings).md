## Ingredients

- 3/4 pound ground pork or other meat
- 1 cup minced cabbage
- 2 tablespoons minced ginger
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 6 scallions, the white and green parts separated, both minced
- 1/2 cup plus 2 tablespoons good soy sauce
- 48 dumpling wrappers
- 1 egg, lightly beaten in a bowl
- 4 tablespoons peanut oil or vegetable oil, more or less
- 1/4 cup rice vinegar or white vinegar

## Instructions

1. Mix the meat, cabbage, ginger, garlic, scallion whites and 2 tablespoons soy sauce with 1/4 cup water.
2. Brush egg along half the edge of a wrapper, put a rounded teaspoon of filling in the center, fold and pinch to seal. Do not overfill. Repeat, setting dumplings on a plate.
3. Heat about 2 tablespoons oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high for a minute. Add dumplings one by one in a single flat layer (touching is fine). Cook about 2 minutes, until lightly browned and most of the oil is absorbed.
4. Add 1/4 cup water per dozen dumplings, cover, lower to medium and simmer about 3 minutes.
5. For the sauce, combine the remaining soy sauce, scallion greens and vinegar.
6. Uncover, return to medium-high and cook 1 to 2 minutes more until the bottoms are dark brown and crisp and the water is gone, adding oil if needed. Serve hot with the sauce.

## Notes

- Shaped dumplings can be covered and refrigerated for a few hours before cooking, or frozen for up to two weeks.
