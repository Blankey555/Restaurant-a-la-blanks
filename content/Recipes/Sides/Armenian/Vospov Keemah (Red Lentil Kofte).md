## Ingredients

* 2 1/2 cups red lentils, picked over and rinsed.
* 6 cups water.
* 4 teaspoons salt.
* 2 cups cracked wheat, fine.
* 1 1/2 cups olive oil (or less).
* 2 cups coarsely chopped onion.
* 2 teaspoons cayenne red pepper (use 1 teaspoon for less hot).

### Garnish
* 1 cup chopped parsley.
* 1 cup finely chopped red and green bell peppers.
* 1 cup finely chopped scallions or sweet onions or both.

## Instructions

1. Place the lentils in a kettle, add the water, and bring to a boil. Simmer for 5 minutes, removing the thick foam that rises to the surface.
2. Add the salt and continue simmering, covered, for 40 to 50 minutes, stirring occasionally. Remove from the heat when the mixture has cooked to a thick, yellow mass and the water is absorbed.
3. Measure the cracked wheat into a deep bowl and spoon the cooked lentils over it, mixing to blend. Set the bowl aside, covered, for 10 minutes.
4. Heat the olive oil in a skillet and add the onions, sautéing them just until they begin to brown. Stir in the red pepper, then add the skillet contents to the bowl. Knead or mix thoroughly, and taste to adjust the seasoning.
5. Shape and garnish.
