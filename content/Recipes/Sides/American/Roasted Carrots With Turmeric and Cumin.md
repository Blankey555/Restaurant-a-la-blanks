## Ingredients

- 10 medium carrots, peeled
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Salt and freshly ground pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds, lightly toasted
- 1/2 teaspoon coriander seeds, lightly toasted
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
- 1/2 teaspoon Aleppo pepper or mild chili powder
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh mint

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Halve the carrots lengthwise and crosswise, splitting thick pieces again so all are about the same thickness. Toss with the oil, salt, pepper, and thyme.
2. Heat a heavy baking sheet in the oven for 3 to 4 minutes. Spread the carrots on it in one layer and roast 20 to 25 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes, until tender and lightly caramelized.
3. Meanwhile, grind the cumin and coriander in a mortar. Work in the butter, turmeric, and Aleppo pepper with the pestle until blended. Put the mixture in a large pan.
4. Add the roasted carrots and mint to the pan and toss. Adjust salt and pepper and serve.
