## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter, plus more for baking dish
- 1 lb good-quality day-old white bread, torn into 1-inch pieces (about 10 cups)
- 2 1/2 cups chopped yellow onions
- 1 1/2 cups (1/4-inch slices) celery
- 1/2 cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh sage
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh rosemary
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh thyme
- 2 teaspoons Diamond Crystal or 1 teaspoon Morton kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth or vegetable broth, divided
- 2 large eggs

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 250°F and butter a 13x9x2-inch baking dish. Spread the bread in one layer on a rimmed baking sheet and bake, stirring now and then, until dry, about 1 hour. Cool and move to a very large bowl.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onions and celery and cook, stirring often, until just starting to brown, about 10 minutes. Add to the bread with the parsley, sage, rosemary, thyme, salt and pepper. Drizzle in 1 1/4 cups broth, toss gently and let cool.
3. Raise the oven to 350°F. Whisk the eggs with the remaining 1 1/4 cups broth, add to the bread and fold until combined. Transfer to the dish, cover with foil and bake until the center reads 160°F, 35 to 40 minutes.
4. Uncover and bake until set with a golden, crisp top, 35 to 45 minutes more.

## Notes

- Make ahead: bake (before the browning step) up to 1 day ahead; uncover, cool, then cover and chill. Bake uncovered until browned and crisp, 45 to 60 minutes.
- Originally published by Epicurious (2012) as "Simple Is Best Dressing."
- Vegetarian if made with vegetable broth.
