## Ingredients

- 13 to 15 cups dry bread cubes (a heavier bread soaks up more juice)
- 1 cup chopped celery
- 1 cup diced onion
- 1 1/2 cups butter
- 2 1/4 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 1/2 tablespoons poultry seasoning
- 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
- 2 large eggs

## Instructions

1. In a frying pan, saute the onion and celery in the butter 10 to 12 minutes, until tender.
2. Put the bread cubes in a large bowl, pour the butter mixture over, and mix. Add the remaining ingredients and mix well.
3. Spray a 2-quart casserole dish with cooking spray, add the stuffing, and bake at 350°F for 30 to 35 minutes, until golden brown.

## Notes

- Recovered from a 2019 Internet Archive snapshot (the live site blocks automated access).
- Pack the bread cubes lightly into the cup when measuring so there is enough bread to absorb the liquid; use more bread for a drier stuffing.
- Dry the bread cubes on the counter for about 48 hours, stirring once or twice a day, or dry them in a low oven if short on time.
- To stuff the turkey instead: stuff it right before it goes into a preheated oven, and make sure the center of the stuffing reaches 165°F. Plan on about 3/4 cup stuffing per pound of turkey.
