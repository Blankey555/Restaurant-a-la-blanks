## Ingredients

- 4 to 6 large tomatoes, ideally heirloom, cut into 1 1/4-inch cubes
- 1 small seedless watermelon, cut into 1 1/4-inch cubes
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons sherry vinegar
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 cup feta cheese, torn into large crumbles

## Instructions

1. Gently toss the tomatoes and watermelon in a large nonreactive bowl. Add the salt and let stand 5 to 10 minutes.
2. Whisk the oil and vinegar and season with salt and pepper.
3. Add the feta to the bowl, then the dressing, and toss gently.
