## Ingredients

- 2 pounds zucchini (or a combination of yellow and green zucchini)
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt and freshly ground pepper
- 3/4 cup Fresh Tomato Sauce (optional)
- 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan, for serving (more to taste)

## Instructions

1. Cut the zucchini into long ribbons with a vegetable peeler, rotating as you go, until you reach the seedy core; discard the core. A mandoline set very thin also works.
2. Working in 2 batches, heat 1 tablespoon oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high, add half the ribbons and 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and toss 2 to 3 minutes, just until softened and turning translucent. Season with salt and pepper and transfer to a dish. Repeat.
3. Serve topped with tomato sauce and Parmesan if you like.

## Notes

- The tomato sauce is optional and is a separate NYT recipe.
- Vegetarian assuming a vegetarian-rennet Parmesan.
