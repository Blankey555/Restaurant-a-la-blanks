## Ingredients

* 1/2 cup mayonnaise
* 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
* 2 tablespoons maple syrup
* 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
* 1 teaspoon celery seed
* Salt and pepper to taste
* 16 oz shredded coleslaw mix

## Instructions

1. In a mixing bowl, combine the mayonnaise, apple cider vinegar, maple syrup, Dijon mustard, celery seed, salt, and pepper. 
2. Whisk the ingredients together until the dressing is completely smooth.
3. Add the shredded coleslaw mix to the bowl.
4. Toss thoroughly until all the cabbage is evenly coated with the dressing.
5. Serve immediately, or cover and refrigerate to allow the flavors to develop.
