## Ingredients

- 4 medium zucchini, about 2 pounds
- 3 eggs
- Salt
- Black pepper
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 cups bread crumbs, preferably fresh
- Neutral oil, such as canola or grapeseed, for deep-frying
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley leaves, for garnish
- 2 lemons, quartered, for serving

## Instructions

1. Trim the zucchini and cut into 1/2-inch rounds or fry-shaped sticks. Heat the oven to 200°F. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper in a shallow dish, and set up plates of flour, egg and bread crumbs, plus a baking sheet and some wax or parchment paper.
2. Coat each piece in flour, then egg, then crumbs, shaking off excess for a thin, even coating. Lay in a single layer on the sheet, separating layers with paper. Chill at least 10 minutes or up to 3 hours.
3. Heat at least 1/2 inch of oil in a large heavy skillet or wide saucepan over medium heat; line a plate with paper towels. The oil is ready when a pinch of flour sizzles on contact.
4. Fry a few pieces at a time without crowding, 2 to 3 minutes per side until browned, adjusting heat so the oil sputters but does not smoke. Drain on towels, then hold on an ovenproof platter in the oven while you finish. Add oil as needed.
5. Garnish with parsley and serve with lemon wedges.

## Notes

- Coated zucchini can be chilled up to 3 hours before frying.
