## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 pounds potatoes (like Yukon Gold), peeled and sliced
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
- 1/2 cup buttermilk
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 2 teaspoons unsalted butter
- Freshly ground pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Cover the potatoes and 1 teaspoon salt with water in a saucepan. Boil, then simmer about 10 minutes until fork-tender. Drain, saving a few tablespoons of the cooking water.
2. Partly mash the potatoes in a large bowl. Warm the buttermilk gently over low heat; do not let it get hot or it will separate.
3. Add the buttermilk and baking soda and mash completely.
4. Stir in the butter, salt, and pepper. Add the reserved cooking water for creamier potatoes. Serve right away.
