## Ingredients

- One 15-ounce can of chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- Vegetable oil, for frying
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- Kosher salt
- Pepper
- 1/2 pound thin asparagus, cut crosswise into 1-inch pieces
- 4 ounces sugar snap peas, sliced crosswise in half
- 1/2 cup fine bulgur
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, plus 1 tablespoon finely grated lemon zest
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 2 cups chopped parsley leaves
- 1 cup chopped cilantro leaves
- 1 cup chopped mint leaves

## Instructions

1. Line a rimmed baking sheet with paper towels. Pat the chickpeas dry and remove their skins.
2. Heat 1/2 inch of vegetable oil to 375°F in a medium saucepan. Fry the chickpeas in batches about 3 minutes, until golden and crisp. Drain on the lined sheet and season right away with the cumin and salt.
3. In a large pot of salted boiling water, cook the asparagus and snap peas about 2 minutes, until crisp-tender; drain well. Bring the water back to a boil and cook the bulgur about 10 minutes, until tender; drain well.
4. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, vinegar, and honey in a small bowl; season with salt and pepper.
5. In a large bowl, toss the bulgur with the asparagus, snap peas, lemon zest, and herbs. Fold in the dressing and season. Transfer to a serving bowl, top with the fried chickpeas, and serve.

## Notes

- Recipe by Paige McCurdy-Flynn for Food & Wine; recovered from a 2017 Internet Archive snapshot.
- Make ahead: the fried chickpeas keep in an airtight container up to 2 days; the undressed salad can be refrigerated overnight.
- The source suggests adding a few teaspoons of rose water to the vinaigrette for a twist.
