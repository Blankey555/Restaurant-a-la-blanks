## Ingredients

- 3/4 pound halloumi
- 2 sweet red peppers
- 1 large zucchini
- 1 Japanese eggplant
- 1 bunch scallions, trimmed
- Salt and pepper
- About 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 teaspoons fresh thyme or crumbled dried mint
- 1/2 to 1 teaspoon sumac (optional)

## Instructions

1. Prepare a hot grill. Cut the halloumi into 8 to 12 slabs, 1/3 to 1/2 inch thick. Cut the peppers into wide pieces along their natural folds, discard seeds, and halve crosswise. Slice the zucchini and eggplant on the diagonal into 1/4- to 1/3-inch rounds.
2. Season the vegetables with salt and pepper and brush vegetables and halloumi with oil.
3. Grill the vegetables, turning, until tender and charred: about 3 to 4 minutes for scallions and about 10 minutes for the rest. A grill pan keeps them from falling through. Arrange on a platter.
4. Grill the halloumi, turning, 2 to 6 minutes until marked; it should yield when pressed but not run.
5. Add the cheese to the platter, drizzle with oil, and sprinkle with herbs and sumac if using.
