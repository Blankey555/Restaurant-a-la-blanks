# Gluten-Free Filo Pastry

A delicate, paper-thin pastry made without gluten. This recipe uses psyllium husk to provide the necessary elasticity for stretching the dough.

## Ingredients

### Psyllium Gel
* 15g psyllium husk (whole)
* 200g warm water

### Dough
* 200g gluten-free flour blend (rice, potato, maize)
* 50g tapioca starch (plus extra for dusting)
* 1/2 tsp xanthan gum
* 1/2 tsp salt
* 1 large egg (room temperature)
* 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
* 2 tbsp vegetable oil

## Instructions

### Prepare the Gel
1. Whisk the psyllium husk and warm water in a small bowl.
2. Let it sit for 10 minutes until it forms a thick, jelly-like paste.

### Mix the Dough
1. Combine the flour, starch, xanthan gum, and salt in a large bowl.
2. Create a well in the center. Add the psyllium gel, egg, vinegar, and oil.
3. Mix by hand or with a wooden spoon until a rough dough forms.
4. Knead on a lightly floured surface for 5 minutes until smooth and pliable. If the dough feels too sticky, add a teaspoon of flour at a time.

### Resting
1. Wrap the dough tightly in plastic wrap.
2. Let it rest at room temperature for at least 30 minutes. This allows the flours to hydrate and the dough to become more extensible.

### Rolling and Stretching
1. Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces. Keep the pieces you are not working with covered to prevent drying.
2. Dust your work surface generously with tapioca starch.
3. Roll one piece into a thin rectangle using a rolling pin.
4. Use your hands to gently stretch the dough from the center outwards until it is translucent and paper-thin. 
5. Trim the edges to your desired size.

### Storage
1. Place a sheet of parchment paper between each layer of filo to prevent sticking.
2. Use immediately in your chosen recipe or wrap tightly and refrigerate for up to 2 days.
